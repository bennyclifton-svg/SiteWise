# Owner rulings

The owner's answers to domain questions. Every domain map, review and
scenario follows these. A ruling overrides a reviewer and an author. Where
a ruling names a figure, the figure has been checked against the source
cited; anything still unconfirmed stays `verified: false`.

## Gate 1, 30 October 2026

Gate 1 is approved. The depth and structure of the T01, T05 and T09 maps is
right, and they are the template for the other 17 types.

### R1. Civil engineer on alterations and additions

A civil (stormwater) engineer is needed whenever the roof area grows or the
impervious area outside changes, because the stormwater catchment changes
and the drainage must be recalculated. Most alterations and additions do one
or the other, so the civil engineer is in the **base** scenario of T05 and
similar extend jobs. Drop the civil engineer only in a variant where the
documents say the roof footprint and hard surfaces are unchanged (for
example, an internal-only alteration).

This reverses the T05 round 1 reviewer's ruling, which made civil a trigger
only.

### R2. Waterproofing remediation needs three packages

T09 needs only: a remedial building consultant, a structural engineer, and
the builder. Confirmed as mapped.

### R3. Design and Building Practitioners Act scope

The DBP Act obligations (registered designs, compliance declarations,
including for remedial work) apply to **Class 2** buildings and to **aged
care, Class 9c**. They do not apply to a Class 1 house. The 2023 extension
to Class 3 is the author's recollection, not the owner's; keep it
`verified: false`.

### R4. Home warranty insurance (HBCF)

Required for residential building work where the contract price is
**over $20,000 including GST**. The principal contractor takes it out before
starting work or taking a deposit. An owner-builder cannot take it out. A
variation that takes the price over $20,000 needs a certificate before any
further payment.

The owner recalled $25,000; the NSW source says $20,000, so $20,000 is used.
Source: SIRA, "Insurance obligations for residential building work",
https://www.sira.nsw.gov.au/resources-library/home-building-compensation-resources/publications/insurance-obligations-for-residential-building-works
The Home Building Act section numbers are still `verified: false`. The
exemption for buildings over 3 storeys (relevant to T02, T13) stays
`verified: false`.

### R5. BASIX for alterations and additions

Alterations and additions need a BASIX certificate, through the NSW Planning
Portal, when the work is valued at **$50,000 or more**, or when it installs a
pool or spa over 40,000 litres. It is a simpler process than for a new
dwelling. Work that in effect creates a new dwelling is assessed as a new
dwelling.
Source: NSW Planning Portal, BASIX, "Alterations and additions",
https://www.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/basix/about-basix/dwelling-types/alterations-and-additions

### R6. Livable housing

Livable housing design is optional for houses in NSW: guidelines and
standards, not a mandatory requirement. Do not expect it in the base scope
of a Class 1 house. The NCC clause references stay `verified: false`.

### R7. Critical-stage inspections (Class 1 and 10)

The principal certifier inspects:

1. a pre-start (pre-commencement) inspection of the site (the owner's
   addition; it comes before the construction certificate or the start of
   works);
2. after excavation and before footings are placed;
3. before pouring any in-situ reinforced concrete element (the slab);
4. before covering the framework (the frame inspection);
5. before covering waterproofing in wet areas;
6. before covering stormwater drainage connections;
7. for a pool: before pouring its in-situ concrete, and once the pool
   barrier is erected;
8. a final inspection after the work is finished and before the occupation
   certificate.

Items 2 to 8 are listed by Hornsby Council, citing the 2000 Regulation:
https://www.hornsby.nsw.gov.au/Property/Building-and-development/Building-certification/Critical-Stage-Inspections-and-Occupation-Certificates
The current clause, clause 61 of the EP&A (Development Certification and
Fire Safety) Regulation 2021, stays `verified: false`. The project's CC or
CDC lists the inspections that apply.

### R8. Defects liability period

HIA and MBA residential contracts carry a defects liability period. Keep
the length `verified: false`.
