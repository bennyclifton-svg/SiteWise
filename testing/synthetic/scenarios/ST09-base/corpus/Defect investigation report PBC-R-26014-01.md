# Balcony waterproofing defect investigation report

Parapet Building Consultants Pty Ltd
Kurrajong Apartments, 41 Kurrajong Parade, Hurstville NSW 2220
Prepared by Joel Marchetti, registered design practitioner

## Purpose

This report covers the remediation of failed balcony waterproofing at an existing residential
building. It sets out what we found, why the balconies leak, the options for remedial works and our
recommendation, so that the strata committee can put a proposal to owners.

We inspected all 42 balconies between 9 and 20 March 2026, mapped the leaks against the strata
manager's leak log, carried out flood tests at four representative balconies (Balconies 207, 303, 502
and 605) and opened up the same four balconies on 24 and 25 March 2026.

## Building description

Kurrajong Apartments is a 6-storey residential flat building of 42 apartments, with seven apartments
on each level. The building is 6 storeys above ground.

- NCC building classification: Class 2
- The building has a rise in storeys of 6
- The works are to an existing building
- The building was constructed in 2008

The frame and floors are reinforced concrete. Each balcony is a cantilevered slab finished with
ceramic tiles and an aluminium-framed glass balustrade on core-drilled posts. The building has no
podium and no basement; residents park on a separate at-grade car park at the rear. The roof is a
pitched metal roof and is outside the scope of this report.

## Findings

1. **Membrane terminations.** At all four opened balconies the membrane stops short of the door sills
   and is lapped onto the sill flashing with sealant only. Set-down is adequate at all thresholds and
   the door frames are retained; the membrane can be turned up behind the sills without replacing the
   doors.
2. **Falls.** The bedding was laid with reversed falls at three of the four balconies, so water runs
   towards the doors. The flood tests showed ponding at the outlets and along the door sills.
3. **Outlets.** The outlets have no puddle flange or clamping ring, so the membrane is not sealed to
   them. No balcony has an overflow.
4. **Balustrade posts.** The posts are core-drilled through the membrane and grouted. Every opened
   post fixing let water through to the slab.
5. **Tiles.** The tiles are bonded directly to the membrane with no protection, and the membrane has
   cracked under them.
6. **Concrete.** We found no spalling, cracking or exposed reinforcement on any balcony. Karrak
   Consulting Engineers is reporting separately on the slabs and post fixings.
7. **Hazardous materials.** The 2019 hazardous materials survey found no asbestos in the balcony
   build-ups, and none of the materials we removed was suspect.
8. **Services.** No plant or services are located on the balconies.

The membrane cannot be repaired in place: it is cracked, wrongly terminated and penetrated at every
post. The leaks follow the same pattern on every level, so we expect the same defects on all 42
balconies.

## Options

**Option A: patch repair.** Seal the sills and posts and regrout. Low cost, but it leaves the membrane
and falls as they are. We expect leaks to return within two to three years. Not recommended.

**Option B: full renewal like for like.** Remove the tiles, bedding and membrane; lay a new falls
screed; fit new outlets with puddle flanges and add overflows; lay a new external membrane turned up
behind the door sills; re-fix the balustrade posts to the slab edge outside the membrane; re-tile to
match. The balconies look the same when finished. Recommended.

## Recommended budget

We recommend the owners corporation adopts a construction budget of $1,680,000 excluding GST for
Option B (construction cost of the works including preliminaries and provisional sums). This allows
for the remedial works to all 42 balconies, a provisional sum for concrete repairs found on opening
up, and a 10% contingency. It excludes consultant fees.

## Approvals

The works are like-for-like repair and maintenance with no change to the external appearance and do
not require development consent. The property is not a heritage item and is not within a heritage
conservation area.

The works are building work under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020; regulated designs
and design compliance declarations are required. We will prepare them with the specification, and the
builder must be a registered building practitioner.

## Warranty

The building is out of warranty and no claim is being pursued against the original builder or
developer. The owners corporation funds the works through a special levy.

## Next steps

1. Committee to accept this report and Option B, and to put the budget to a general meeting.
2. Parapet to prepare the remedial specification and details, coordinated with Karrak's design.
3. Tender to remedial builders on a lump sum.

All work will be done from within the balconies, with no external access equipment. All 42 balconies
will be renewed in one continuous stage, working on a few balconies at a time so residents lose the
use of their balcony for as short a time as practical.
