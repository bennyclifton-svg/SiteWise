# Structural assessment of balcony slabs and balustrade fixings

Karrak Consulting Engineers Pty Ltd
Kurrajong Apartments, 41 Kurrajong Parade, Hurstville NSW 2220
Prepared for The Owners - Strata Plan No. 912345, c/- Northgate Strata Management
Engineer: Lachlan Pyne

## Scope

We inspected the four balconies opened up by Parapet Building Consultants on 24 and 25 March 2026
(Balconies 207, 303, 502 and 605) to assess the slabs and the balustrade post fixings before the
waterproofing is renewed. We reviewed the 2008 structural drawings held by the strata manager.

## The structure

The building was constructed in 2008. The balconies are reinforced concrete slabs of the existing
building, cantilevering about 2.4 m from the edge of each floor plate. The building has no
transfer slabs or transfer beams; the balcony slabs cantilever from the floor plates.

## Observations

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| Slab thickness | 200 mm at the root, 180 mm at the edge, matching the 2008 drawings |
| Cover to top reinforcement | 30 to 40 mm by cover meter |
| Concrete condition | Sound. No spalling, cracking or exposed reinforcement |
| Post fixings | Posts core-drilled through the membrane into the slab, grouted; grout cracked and wet at most posts |
| Post steel | Sound; full section, coating intact |

## Assessment

1. The slabs are in good condition and need no strengthening.
2. The core-drilled post fixings break the membrane and should not be reused. We recommend the posts
   are re-fixed to the slab edge with side-fixed stainless steel brackets outside the membrane, to our
   design. The existing glass panels and handrails can be retained.
3. The new falls screed and tile bed add about 0.6 kPa. The slabs carry this with the design live load.
4. The finished level rises by up to 25 mm. The barrier height measured from the new finished level
   will still be at least 1 m at every balcony we measured.
5. Old core holes are to be filled with a non-shrink grout before the membrane is laid.

## Concrete repairs

Defects may be found on the other 38 balconies when they are opened. We recommend a provisional
repair method: break out to sound concrete, clean and prime the reinforcement, and reinstate with a
polymer-modified repair mortar. Each repair is to be inspected by us before it is covered.

## Other matters

The works are confined to the balconies; no excavation, basement or ground works are involved, and
nothing is done near the adjoining boundaries or neighbouring structures.
