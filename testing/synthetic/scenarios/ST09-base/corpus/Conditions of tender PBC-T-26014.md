# Conditions of tender and contract particulars

Parapet Building Consultants Pty Ltd, for the Principal

## Contract and procurement

Traditional procurement; the owners corporation's consultant has completed the design and the works
are tendered to five remedial builders.

- Form of contract: AS 4000-1997 General conditions of contract, with special conditions.
- Basis of payment: Lump sum contract. A schedule of rates is tendered for the provisional quantities
  only.
- Superintendent: Parapet Building Consultants Pty Ltd.
- Tender documents: remedial specification PBC-SPEC-26014 Rev A, drawing RW-101 Rev B and Karrak
  Consulting Engineers' balustrade fixing design.
- Contract period: tenderers state their period; the Principal expects no more than 26 weeks.
- Security: 5% of the contract sum, reducing to 2.5% at practical completion.
- Defects liability period: 12 months from practical completion.
- Liquidated damages: $500 per working day.
- The contract and the levy to fund it are subject to approval at a general meeting of owners. No
  tenderer is bound until the contract is signed.

## Tenderer eligibility

Tenderers must hold:

1. a current NSW contractor licence covering waterproofing and general building work;
2. registration as a building practitioner under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020;
3. a licensed waterproofer on staff or as a named subcontractor.

## Returnables

1. Lump sum price, ex GST and inc GST, on the tender form.
2. Completed schedule of rates for the provisional quantities.
3. Proposed contract period and a bar-chart programme.
4. Membrane system proposed, with the manufacturer's technical data sheets.
5. Copies of licences, registration and insurances.
6. Two references for balcony waterproofing on occupied strata buildings.
7. Work method for resident access, protection and daily clean-up.

## Insurance

The contractor holds contract works insurance for the contract sum, public liability of at least
$20 million and workers compensation.

HBCF cover is not required because the building is a multi-storey building with a rise of more than
three storeys.

## Site inspection

A site inspection will be held at 10.00 am on 18 June 2026, meeting in the front lobby. Attendance is
recommended. Access to balconies is by arrangement only.
