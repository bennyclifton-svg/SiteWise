# Typical balcony waterproofing details

Parapet Building Consultants Pty Ltd
Project: Balcony waterproofing, Kurrajong Apartments, 41 Kurrajong Parade, Hurstville NSW 2220
Client: The Owners - Strata Plan No. 912345
Scale: 1:5 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Detail 1: door sill, with the membrane turned up behind the sill and the existing door frame retained
- Detail 2: outlet with puddle flange and clamping ring, connected to the existing downpipe
- Detail 3: balustrade post re-fixed with a side-fixed bracket to the slab edge, outside the membrane,
  set out to the structural engineer's design
- Detail 4: falls screed, membrane, protection screed and tile bed build-up
- Detail 5: membrane upturn at the walls and the bond-breaker at internal corners
- Detail 6: overflow through the balustrade line, discharging clear of the slab edge

## General notes

1. Do not scale from this drawing. Use written dimensions.
2. Read with the remedial specification PBC-SPEC-26014 and Karrak Consulting Engineers' fixing design.
3. Membrane to AS 4654.2. Minimum fall 1:80 to the outlet.
4. Brackets and fixings to the structural engineer's design. Do not substitute.
