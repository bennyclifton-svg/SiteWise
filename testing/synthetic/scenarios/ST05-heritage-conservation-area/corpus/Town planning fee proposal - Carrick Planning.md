## 1. Introduction

Thank you for asking us to propose town planning services for your rear addition. The site is in
the Tallowwood Estate Heritage Conservation Area, so the work needs a development application to the
City of Canada Bay, assessed against the LEP and the DCP controls for the conservation area. We will
work with Kestrel Hill Architects and your heritage consultant so the application is consistent.

## 2. Scope of services

### Pre-DA meeting and planning advice

- Review of the planning certificate, the LEP and the DCP, including the conservation area controls.
- Written planning advice on the concept design before it is developed.
- Preparation of the pre-DA meeting request and attendance at the meeting with council's planner and
  heritage officer, with notes of the advice given.

### Statement of environmental effects

- A statement of environmental effects under section 4.15 of the Environmental Planning and
  Assessment Act 1979, with compliance tables for the LEP and DCP controls.
- A summary of the heritage impact statement and of how the design responds to the pre-DA advice.

### Planning response during assessment

- Review of council's requests for information and of any neighbour submissions.
- Written responses and one meeting with council's assessing officer.
