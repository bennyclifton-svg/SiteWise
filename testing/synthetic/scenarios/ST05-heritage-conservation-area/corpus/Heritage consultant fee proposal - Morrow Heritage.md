## 1. Background

Thank you for your enquiry. We understand that 41 Tallowwood Avenue is within the Tallowwood Estate
Heritage Conservation Area under the Canada Bay Local Environmental Plan 2013, and that you propose a
single-storey rear addition and internal alterations to the house. Council requires a heritage impact
statement with the development application, and its heritage officer will assess the proposal
against the conservation area controls in the LEP and the Canada Bay Development Control Plan.

## 2. Scope of services

### Heritage impact statement for the DA

- Site inspection of the house and the street, with photographs of the streetscape.
- Review of the conservation area listing, council's statement of significance and the DCP controls
  for the conservation area.
- A brief history of the house and the estate, and an assessment of how the house relates to the
  significance of the conservation area.
- Review of the concept and DA drawings with the architect, with comments on form, siting, materials
  and colours before the drawings are finalised.
- An assessment of the impact of the proposal on the significance of the conservation area, with
  recommendations where needed.
- A heritage impact statement in the format council asks for, issued as a PDF for lodgement.

### Response to heritage referral

- Review of council's heritage referral comments during assessment.
- One written response and, if needed, one meeting or call with council's heritage officer.
