## Our understanding

The works add a kitchen, living and dining room at the rear of the brick house, with a wide opening
from the existing house into the addition, and remove two loadbearing internal walls. The existing
house has a suspended timber floor on brick piers and a pitched roof framed in timber.

No transfer structures are required; steel beams over the new openings bear on the existing brick
walls. New piers or pad footings will be provided where the beam reactions call for them.

## Site inspections

Inspections during construction (footings, steel beams, framing) are charged at $330 per visit
excluding GST, plus a short inspection record.

## Exclusions
