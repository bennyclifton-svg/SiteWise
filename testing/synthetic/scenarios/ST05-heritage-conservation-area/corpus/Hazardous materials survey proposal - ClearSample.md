## Background

The house dates from the late 1950s, and the proposed works will demolish the rear skillion and
laundry lean-to, strip out the bathroom and laundry and remove two internal walls. Materials of that
age can contain asbestos and lead paint, so a survey is needed before the work is priced and before
any demolition starts.

## Scope

- A pre-demolition survey of every area affected by the works, including the roof space, subfloor
  and eaves, with sampling of suspect materials
- Analysis of the samples by a NATA-accredited laboratory
- A hazardous materials register and report suitable for the tender documents
