## Scope

This report accompanies our detail, level and boundary survey drawing PR-26-0217 of the property,
carried out on 24 February 2026. Levels are on the Australian Height Datum (AHD), derived from a
permanent mark in Tallowwood Avenue. Boundaries were marked by reference to the deposited plan and
existing occupation.

## Site description

Site area by survey: 557.4 m2.

The lot is rectangular, with a frontage of 12.2 m to Tallowwood Avenue and a depth of 45.7 m. It is
generally level, falling about 300 mm from the front boundary to the rear fence.

The lot has a narrow 12.2 m frontage with street parking only and no rear lane. Side access to the
rear yard is along a 1.0 m path on the eastern side of the house. There is no driveway; the kerb is unbroken
across the frontage.

## Boundaries and easements

The boundary dimensions agree with the deposited plan within normal tolerances. Fences are generally
on or within 50 mm of the boundaries. No easements are shown on the title, and none were found on
site.
