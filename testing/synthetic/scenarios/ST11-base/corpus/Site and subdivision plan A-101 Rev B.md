# Site and subdivision plan

Fernleigh Architects Pty Ltd
Project: Dual occupancy and Torrens title subdivision, 18 Wattlebird Street, Carlingford NSW 2118
Client: Marko and Ana Kovac
Scale: 1:200 at A3
Issue: For DA

## Sheet shows

- Existing lot boundary (Lot 9 DP 236154) and the existing house and garage to be demolished, dashed
- Proposed lot boundary on the separating wall line, dividing the site into proposed Lots 1 and 2
- Building footprints of Dwelling 1 (Lot 1) and Dwelling 2 (Lot 2), with garages at the front
- Front setback 6.0 m; side setbacks 0.9 m to the ground floor and 1.5 m to the upper floor
- Two driveways and two vehicle crossings, one for each lot
- OSD tank under the Lot 1 driveway
- Rainwater tank for each dwelling, in the side setback
- Front fence line amended: low open fence with planting behind
- Private open space and rear courtyards
