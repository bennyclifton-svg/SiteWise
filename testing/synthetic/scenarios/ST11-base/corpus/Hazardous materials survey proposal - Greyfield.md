## Scope

Before the house and garage are demolished, a hazardous materials survey is needed so that any
asbestos and lead paint can be identified, recorded and removed safely, and so that builders can
price the removal at tender.

We will survey the whole house and the garage, including the roof space, the subfloor and the eaves.
Suspect materials will be sampled and sent to a NATA-accredited laboratory. Paint on representative
surfaces will be sampled for lead. Access will be arranged through your managing agent.
