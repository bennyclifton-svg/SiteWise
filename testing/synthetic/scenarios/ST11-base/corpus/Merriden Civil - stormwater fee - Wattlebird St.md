Background

We have reviewed the survey and the concept plans. The land falls gently to the street, so roof
and paved areas can drain by gravity to the kerb in Wattlebird Street.

Council's stormwater management policy requires on-site detention for the dual occupancy. There is no existing on-site detention on the lot. We will size the detention storage for the
development and locate the tank with the architect. Gravity
discharge to the kerb has been confirmed from the survey levels. No stormwater quality treatment or MUSIC modelling is required at this scale. The site is outside the drinking water catchment, so no NorBE assessment applies. No public road works are required.
