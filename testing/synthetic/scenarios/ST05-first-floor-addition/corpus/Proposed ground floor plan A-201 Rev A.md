# Proposed ground floor plan

Kestrel Hill Architects Pty Ltd
Project: Alterations and additions, 41 Tallowwood Avenue, Concord NSW 2137
Client: Tomas and Leah Ferreira
Scale: 1:100 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Existing walls to be retained, existing walls to be demolished (dashed), and new walls
- Demolition outline: rear skillion, laundry lean-to, two internal walls and the existing rear roof
  (removed for the first floor)
- New rear opening and the removed internal walls on steel beams (refer to structural)
- New kitchen, living and dining, with the bathroom and laundry rebuilt
- Paved terrace and rear deck, with steps down to the garden
- New stair from the living area to the first floor, with balustrade
- Side setbacks to the addition and the side path

## Area schedule

- Existing house 140 m2
- Addition 55 m2 (ground 30 m2, first floor 25 m2)
- Total after the works 195 m2

## General notes

1. Do not scale from this drawing. Use written dimensions.
2. Boundaries and levels from survey PR-26-0217 by Pemberton & Rao Surveyors.
3. Wet area waterproofing to AS 3740.
4. Read with the first floor plan, elevations, sections and structural drawings.
