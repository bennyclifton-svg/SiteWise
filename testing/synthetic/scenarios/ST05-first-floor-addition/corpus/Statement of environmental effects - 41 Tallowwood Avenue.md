# Statement of environmental effects

Prepared by Kestrel Hill Architects Pty Ltd for Tomas and Leah Ferreira
Site: 41 Tallowwood Avenue, Concord NSW 2137 (Lot 23 DP 30418)

## 1. Introduction

This statement accompanies a development application to the City of Canada Bay. The owners have chosen a development application rather than a complying development certificate, so that the design is assessed against council's DCP.
It describes the site and the proposal and assesses the likely impacts under section 4.15 of the
Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979. It should be read with the architectural drawings,
the survey, the stormwater concept plan and the BASIX certificate.

## 2. The site

The site is 41 Tallowwood Avenue, Concord NSW 2137, a rectangular lot with a 12.2 m frontage, on the
northern side of the street. It holds a detached brick house with a hipped tiled roof, set in a
street of similar houses. Council's records hold the 1958 building approval and the 1987 approval for the rear skillion and laundry lean-to.

## 3. The proposal

The proposal is alterations and additions to an existing building, a brick dwelling. It comprises:

- demolition of the rear skillion and laundry lean-to
- a new rear addition containing a kitchen, living and dining room
- removal of the existing rear roof and a new first floor over the rear rooms containing the main
  bedroom and ensuite, reached by a new internal stair
- a paved terrace and low rear deck
- internal alterations: the bathroom and laundry rebuilt, and two internal walls removed

Existing gross floor area 140 m2. Added gross floor area 55 m2. The proposal adds a first floor over the rear of the existing house; the dwelling becomes two storeys. The rear addition has a low skillion roof, and the first floor is set
back behind the existing front roof.

## 4. Planning controls

The land is zoned R2 Low Density Residential under Canada Bay Local Environmental Plan 2013. Dwelling
houses and alterations to them are permitted with consent.

| Control | Standard | Proposed | Complies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Height of buildings (LEP cl 4.3) | 8.5 m | 7.6 m (new first-floor ridge) | Yes |
| Floor space ratio (LEP cl 4.4) | 0.5:1 | 0.35:1 | Yes |

No clause 4.6 variation is required.

## 5. Environmental effects

### Building height

The first floor is the highest part of the house at 7.6 m, below the 8.5 m standard. It sits over the
rear rooms and behind the existing front roof, so the house looks much the same from the street and
the added bulk sits towards the rear.

### Privacy and overshadowing

The first-floor side windows are highlight windows with sills 1.7 m above the floor, and the
bedroom's main window faces the rear garden, so there is no direct overlooking of the adjoining rear
yards from the first-floor windows. The rear addition has a floor close to ground level, and its side
windows face the side fences. The shadow diagrams
show that the adjoining houses keep at least three hours of sun to their living rooms and private
open space between 9 am and 3 pm at the winter solstice.

### Stormwater

Roof water from the addition and the terrace connects to the existing system, which drains by gravity
to the kerb in Tallowwood Avenue. A stormwater concept plan accompanies the application.

### Heritage

The land is not a heritage item and is not within a heritage conservation area. The front of the
house is unchanged.

### Hazards

The land is not identified as bush fire prone land on the planning certificate. The land is not flood
affected. The planning certificate records no contamination or acid sulfate soils.

### Waste

Demolition and construction waste will be managed under a site waste management plan, with brick and
metal separated for recycling.

### Construction

The owners will vacate the house during the works. Construction hours will follow council's standard
conditions.

## 6. Notification and determination

Neighbour notification under council's community participation plan; no public exhibition.
Determination under delegation; no local planning panel referral is expected.

## 7. Conclusion

The proposal is a modest rear addition and first floor that suits the house and the street. It meets
the LEP standards and the DCP controls, has acceptable impacts on the neighbours, and is recommended
for approval.
