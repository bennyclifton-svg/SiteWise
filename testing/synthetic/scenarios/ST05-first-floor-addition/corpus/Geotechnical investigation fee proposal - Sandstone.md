## Purpose

You have asked for a site classification to AS 2870 for the footprint of the proposed rear addition,
and for the depth, type and founding of the existing footings under the walls that will carry the new
first floor, so the structural engineer can check those footings, design any underpinning and design
new footings that suit both.

## Scope

1. Review of the survey, the concept plan and published geological mapping.
2. Fieldwork: two hand-auger boreholes within the addition footprint, and test pits at three
   existing footings, to confirm the founding of the walls that will carry the first floor, recording
   the depth, width and founding material of each.
3. Laboratory shrink-swell testing of clay samples to assess reactivity.
4. A report with the subsurface profile, an AS 2870 site classification, an allowable bearing
   pressure for underpinning and footing recommendations.
