## Our understanding

The works add a kitchen, living and dining room at the rear of the brick house, with a wide opening
from the existing house into the addition, add a first floor over the rear rooms of the existing
house, and remove two loadbearing internal walls. The existing house has a suspended timber floor on
brick piers and a pitched roof framed in timber. The rear part of that roof is removed for the first
floor.

No transfer structures are required; the first floor is carried on new steel beams bearing on the underpinned existing brick walls. The existing walls and footings were not designed for a second
level, so we will check their capacity first and design underpinning where they fall short.
