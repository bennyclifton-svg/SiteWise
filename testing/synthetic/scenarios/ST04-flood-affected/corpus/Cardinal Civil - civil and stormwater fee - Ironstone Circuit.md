# Fee proposal: civil and stormwater engineering

Dear Owen,

Thank you for asking us to provide a fee for the civil and stormwater engineering on Ironstone
Circuit. Our understanding of the work is set out below.

## Background

The work covers bulk earthworks to trim the benched pad, heavy-duty concrete pavements to the
hardstand and flexible pavements to the car park, and the stormwater drainage of the whole lot.

On-site detention is required under council's stormwater policy. We expect an OSD tank under the car
park. There is no existing on-site detention to retain or modify.

Stormwater quality targets are demonstrated with a MUSIC model. We expect a gross pollutant trap and
proprietary cartridge filters upstream of the OSD tank.

The lot is within the flood planning area, so a flood impact assessment with floor levels is needed
for the DA. We will obtain council's flood advice and model the overland flow along the eastern
boundary, set the warehouse and office floor levels at the flood planning level and show that the
works do not worsen flood behaviour on neighbouring land.

The lot is not in a drinking water catchment, so no neutral or beneficial effect assessment applies.

Access is by two heavy-duty vehicle crossings to Ironstone Circuit. No works in the public road other
than the two heavy-duty vehicle crossings and making good the footpath.
