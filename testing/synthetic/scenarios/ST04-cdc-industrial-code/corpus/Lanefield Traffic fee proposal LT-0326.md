## Scope

We will prepare a traffic and parking impact assessment to accompany the CDC application,
covering:

- car parking provision against the Penrith Development Control Plan, including accessible spaces
  and bicycle parking;
- peak-hour truck and car generation, and its distribution onto the estate road network;
- sight distance at the two vehicle crossings;
- the internal layout of the car park, hardstand and docks.

The site is designed for 26 m B-double access to the hardstand and docks; we will check B-double swept
paths at both crossings.

The development is below the Schedule 3 thresholds, so it will not be referred to TfNSW.

Access is from a local industrial road; the site has no classified road frontage.

No SIDRA intersection modelling is required; the estate road network was assessed at subdivision.

A green travel plan is not required for this development.

In design development we will review the hardstand, dock approach and car park layout against
AS 2890.1 and AS 2890.2 as the design changes.
