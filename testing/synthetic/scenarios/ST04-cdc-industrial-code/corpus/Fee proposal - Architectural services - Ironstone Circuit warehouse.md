## Our understanding

Redgum Logistics Estates intends to develop an 8,000 m2 warehouse and office for a single logistics
tenant on a vacant estate lot: a 7,500 m2 warehouse with a 12 m clear height and recessed docks, and a
500 m2 single-storey office facing Ironstone Circuit. The works will be procured as a design and
construct lump sum.

The facade is precast concrete panels with metal cladding above and an aluminium-framed office
frontage; there is no curtain wall or unitised facade system.

## Scope by phase

**Concept design and brief.** Confirm the brief with you and the tenant, check the site layout for
truck circulation, car parking and the office position, and prepare a concept design with an area
schedule.

**CDC documentation.** CDC documentation to the Industrial and Business Buildings Code for the
certifier's determination: plans, elevations, sections, a materials and finishes board and a table
showing the setback, height and landscaping standards are met, coordinated with the traffic, civil
and landscape consultants. Our CDC documentation is issued to Redgum's development manager, who will
lodge the CDC application with the certifier.

**Design development.** Develop the certified scheme, coordinate the structure and services, and
respond to the conditions of the complying development certificate.

**D&C tender documentation and performance brief.** Drawings and a performance brief for the D&C
tender, attendance at tender interviews and responses to tenderer queries.

After the D&C tender our appointment will be novated to the successful design and construct
contractor.
