## 1. Purpose

This report accompanies our detail and contour survey of the property, drawing CM-26-0412 sheet 1.
The survey was prepared for architectural design and a development application. Fieldwork was
carried out on 17 February 2026.

## 2. Control and datum

Levels are on Australian Height Datum (AHD), derived from permanent mark PM 41873 in Corella Rise.
Horizontal control is on MGA2020 coordinates. Contours are shown at 0.25 m intervals.

## 3. Boundaries

Boundaries were identified against the deposited plan and the marks found on site. The boundary
dimensions agree with the plan within survey tolerance. Site area by survey: 650.2 m2.

Full-width street frontage of 16.5 m with unrestricted vehicle access. The side boundaries are
39.4 m long. No encroachments by neighbouring fences or structures were found. No easements are
noted on the title or the plan.

## 4. Existing improvements

No structures on site; the lot is vacant. The lot is grassed. A timber paling fence runs along both
side boundaries and the rear boundary. There is no fence on the street boundary.

## 5. Levels

The lot has a gentle 0.9 m fall from the street to the rear, from RL 61.40 at the front boundary to
RL 60.50 at the rear. For design purposes it is generally level. No existing stormwater detention or
drainage structures on the lot.
