# Statement of environmental effects

Prepared by Harbourline Architects Pty Ltd for Dan and Mai Nguyen
Proposal: Construction of a new two-storey dwelling house with an attached double garage
Site: 27 Corella Rise, Denistone NSW 2114 (Lot 14 DP 1302865)

## 1. Introduction

This statement accompanies a development application to the City of Ryde. It describes the site and
the proposal and assesses the proposal against the relevant planning controls and the matters in
section 4.15 of the Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979. It should be read with the
architectural drawings, the survey, the stormwater concept plan and the BASIX certificate.

## 2. The site

The site is a rectangular vacant lot of 650.2 m2 on the eastern side of Corella Rise, with a 16.5 m
frontage. It falls gently from the street to the rear. The street is residential, with houses set
behind landscaped front gardens. Water, sewer, electricity and telecommunications
are available in the street.

## 3. The proposal

The proposal is a new dwelling house with four bedrooms, a double garage, a covered rear terrace,
a driveway and crossing, a rainwater tank, front fencing and landscaping. The gross floor area is
298 m2. The walls are brick veneer to the ground floor and painted cladding above, under a metal roof.

## 4. Ryde Local Environmental Plan 2014

The site is zoned R2 Low Density Residential. A dwelling house is permitted with consent in the zone,
and the proposal is consistent with the zone objectives.

| Control | Standard | Proposed | Complies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Height of buildings (cl 4.3) | 9.5 m | 8.1 m | Yes |
| Floor space ratio (cl 4.4) | 0.5:1 (325 m2) | 0.46:1 (298 m2) | Yes |

No clause 4.6 variation is required.

The site is not a heritage item and is not in a conservation area. The land is not identified as bush
fire prone land on the planning certificate. The land is not subject to flood related development
controls.

## 6. Section 4.15 matters

- Planning instruments: the proposal complies with the LEP standards and the DCP controls above.
- Likely impacts: the impacts on the natural and built environment are minor and typical of a new
  house in a residential street.
- Suitability of the site: the site is vacant, serviced and of a regular shape, and is suitable for
  a dwelling house.
- Submissions: neighbour notification under council's community participation plan; no public
  exhibition. Any submissions will be considered by council.
- Public interest: the proposal provides new housing consistent with the zone and the controls.

Determination under delegation; no local planning panel referral is expected.

## 7. Privacy and overshadowing

Upper-level windows on the side elevations are small, with high sills or obscure glazing. The main
living areas face the rear garden. Shadow diagrams show that the neighbours' rear living areas and
private open space keep at least three hours of sun in mid-winter.

## 8. Stormwater

Roof water drains to the rainwater tank, with overflow by gravity to the kerb in Corella Rise, as shown
on the civil engineer's stormwater concept plan.

## 9. Waste

Construction waste will be sorted on site and taken to a licensed recycling facility. During
occupation, the household bins are stored in the side passage behind the front building line.

## 10. Landscape

New planting to the front garden uses low-water species. The rear garden is lawn with a paved terrace.
The landscaped area exceeds the DCP minimum.

## 11. Conclusion

The proposal is a well-designed dwelling house that complies with the LEP standards and the DCP
controls and has acceptable impacts on the neighbours and the street. It is suitable for approval.
