# Site plan

Marlowe Hart Architects Pty Ltd
Project: New house, 22 Marrakai Close, Caringbah South NSW 2229
Client: Hamish and Rosa Delaney
Scale: 1:200 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Lot boundaries, dimensions and north point
- Outline of the existing house, garage and shed, shown dashed, to be demolished
- Footprint of the new house with the double garage
- New driveway and vehicle crossing to Marrakai Close
- Front, side and rear setbacks
- Rainwater tank location
- Brush box on the council verge and the spotted gum and jacaranda at the rear, retained, with
  protection fencing shown

## Site data

- Site area 619.8 m2
- Double garage
- Landscaped area 43% of the site
- Front setback 7.0 m; side setbacks 1.2 m north and 1.5 m south; rear setback 12.5 m
- Driveway 3.0 m wide in concrete from the crossing to the garage, at the northern end of the frontage
- Rainwater tank 5,000 L behind the laundry, against the southern side wall
