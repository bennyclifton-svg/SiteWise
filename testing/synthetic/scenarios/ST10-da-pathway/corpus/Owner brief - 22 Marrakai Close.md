# Owner's brief: new house at 22 Marrakai Close

## Project description

We want to build a new two-storey detached family house on our land in Caringbah South.

This is a new house: we will knock down the existing house and build a new two-storey home in its
place. The site contains an existing building (a dwelling, detached garage and shed), all to be
demolished. The existing dwelling was built in 1962. It has served us well for eight years, but it is
cold in winter, the rooms are small and it no longer suits us and our two children.

Site: 22 Marrakai Close, Caringbah South NSW 2229

The lot is about 620 m2, with a wide frontage to the street and a long back garden. A detail survey
has been ordered and we will pass it on when it arrives.

We will move out of the house before demolition, so the site will be empty from the day the builder
starts until handover. We plan to rent nearby so the children can stay at the same school.

We are the sole owners and the only decision makers. Either of us can give instructions, and we will
reply to questions within a few days.

## What we want

- Two storeys, with an internal stair from the entry hall
- 4 bedrooms: the main bedroom upstairs with an ensuite and walk-in robe, two children's bedrooms
  upstairs, and a guest bedroom downstairs that can double as a study
- 3 bathrooms plus a powder room
- An open kitchen, family and dining area facing the back garden, with a walk-in pantry
- A separate living room at the front
- A laundry with direct access to the drying court
- Double garage (2 spaces) under the main roof, with internal access to the house
- Target gross floor area about 300 m2
- A rainwater tank for the garden and the toilets
- Ducted air-conditioning, zoned upstairs and downstairs

We like a calm, modern house with plenty of daylight and good cross-ventilation, and a strong link
between the kitchen and the garden.

## Scope, budget and quality

We want a high-quality, architect-designed home with durable, high-end finishes. We would rather have
fewer, better rooms than a bigger house with cheap finishes.

Construction budget: $1,520,000 excluding GST, including demolition.

This figure is for the builder's contract only. We have set aside a separate amount for consultant
fees, certifier and authority fees, and furniture.

## Contract and procurement

A development application will be lodged for demolition of the existing dwelling and construction of
a new two-storey dwelling. Ines told us at our first meeting that a second storey of the
size we want is unlikely to meet the Housing Code side setback on a lot this width, so complying
development is probably not available. We would like our architect to prepare and lodge the
application with Sutherland Shire Council and see it through to approval.

Architect-designed house, select tender to three builders; an architect-led traditional lump-sum
tender, with the architect recommending builders who do this kind of work well and helping us compare
the prices.

The builder will be engaged under the HIA NSW Lump Sum building contract.

Demolition and asbestos removal are included in the builder's contract.

No staged construction: one builder and one contract cover the demolition and the new house.

## Services and sustainability

We want an all-electric house with no gas supply: induction cooktop and heat-pump hot water.

No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target beyond BASIX. We do want the house to stay comfortable
without running the air-conditioning all year, so orientation and shading matter to us.

We do not need any Connecting with Country input for this lot.

No basement or cellar.

We are not planning multiple buildings: just the one house.
