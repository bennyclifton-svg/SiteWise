## Introduction

Thank you for asking us to propose for the design of your new home. We have read your brief of
2 February 2026 and walked the site with you on 26 February. This proposal sets out our services,
our fee for each stage and the terms we work on.

The architect's services run from concept design through to lodging the development application on
the NSW Planning Portal, and then the construction certificate documentation and the tender to three
builders. We will coordinate the town planner's statement of environmental effects, the landscape plan
and the other consultants' input into the application, and work with the certifier you appoint for the
construction certificate.

## Scope of services

**Stage 1: Concept design.** Site analysis on the survey of the existing site, two concept options,
one preferred option developed with you, and a preliminary check against council's LEP and DCP
controls.

**Stage 2: Design development.** Plans, elevations and sections developed to a fixed layout; selection
of the main materials and finishes; coordination of the structural, civil and BASIX input.

**Stage 3: DA and CC documentation, demolition plan and lodgement.** The full drawing set for the
development application, including the demolition plan for the existing house, garage and shed;
coordination of the town planner's statement of environmental effects, the landscape plan and the
hazardous materials report; the BASIX certificate and stamped plans from the assessor; and lodgement
with Sutherland Shire Council through the NSW Planning Portal. We will respond to council's requests
for information during assessment. After consent, the construction certificate drawings, coordinated
with the structural and civil engineers' documents and the conditions of consent, for your certifier.

**Stage 4: Tender.** Tender documents and a schedule of finishes, issue to three builders you have
approved, responses to tender queries, a tender comparison and a recommendation.
