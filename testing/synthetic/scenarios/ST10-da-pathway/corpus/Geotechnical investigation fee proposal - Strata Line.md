## Purpose

You have asked for a site classification to AS 2870 for the design of the slab and footings of a new
two-storey house. The existing house will still be standing when we visit, so the fieldwork has to
be placed in the open parts of the lot.

## Scope

- Review of the survey and the published geological map, and a short investigation plan.
- Dial-before-you-dig search and service location at each borehole position.
- Three boreholes by a small track-mounted rig, placed clear of the existing house: one in the front
  yard and two in the back yard, each to refusal or 4 m, whichever is shallower.
- Dynamic cone penetrometer probes beside each borehole.
- Laboratory shrink-swell and moisture content testing on two clay samples.
- A report with the subsurface profile, the site classification, footing and slab recommendations
  and earthworks advice.
