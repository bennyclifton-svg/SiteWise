# Fee proposal: civil and stormwater engineering

Dear Ines,

Thank you for the survey and the concept plans. We are pleased to offer our fee for the civil and
stormwater engineering for the new house.

## Background

Roof and surface water from the existing house discharges by gravity to the kerb in Marrakai Close
through an old line and kerb outlet. The new house will discharge to the kerb by gravity in the same
way, and the old line and kerb outlet are to be replaced as part of the new work.

No OSD is required for a replacement dwelling of similar impervious area. There is no existing
on-site detention on the lot.

No stormwater quality treatment or MUSIC modelling is required for a single dwelling. The site is
outside the drinking water catchment, so no NorBE assessment applies.

The civil scope includes no public road works; the builder rebuilds the vehicle crossing to council's
standard drawing under council's approval.
