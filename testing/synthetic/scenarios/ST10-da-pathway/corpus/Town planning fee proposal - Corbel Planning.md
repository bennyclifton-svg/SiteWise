## Our understanding

Hamish and Rosa Delaney propose to demolish the existing dwelling, detached garage and shed at
22 Marrakai Close and build a new two-storey dwelling with a double garage. The upper floor sits
closer to the northern side boundary than the Housing Code allows, so the work cannot proceed as
complying development and a development application is needed for both the demolition and the new
dwelling.

The architect lodges the DA on the NSW Planning Portal. Our role is to prepare the statement of
environmental effects that accompanies it and to support the application through council's
assessment.

## Scope

**Statement of environmental effects.** A statement covering demolition of the existing dwelling and
the new dwelling, prepared from the architect's drawings and the consultants' reports, including:

- a description of the site, its context and the proposal
- a compliance table against the Sutherland Shire LEP and the DCP
- a written justification of the upper floor side setback variation against the objectives of the
  DCP control
- an assessment of the matters for consideration under section 4.15 of the Act
- demolition and waste management, privacy and overshadowing

**Responses to council requests during assessment.** Written responses to council's requests for
further information, and attendance at one meeting with the assessing officer if needed.
