## Introduction

Survey of the existing building and outbuildings before demolition. The existing building is a
single-storey dwelling of brick veneer with fibro to the rear section, with a detached garage and a
garden shed. The house was built in 1962, and its materials are typical of that period.

The survey was destructive: linings were cut, floor coverings lifted and the roof space and sub-floor
entered so that hidden materials could be found. Areas we could not reach are listed under
Limitations.

This survey covers building materials only; it is not a contaminated land assessment.

## Findings

Bonded asbestos cement is present in the eaves linings, the bathroom and laundry wall linings, the
external walls of the rear section and the garage cladding. The vinyl floor tiles in the kitchen have
an asbestos backing. All of these are in fair condition and are not a risk while they are left
undisturbed.

No friable asbestos was identified; all asbestos-containing materials are bonded.

A search of the LFAI register returned no listing, and no loose-fill insulation was seen in the roof
space.

No asbestos-containing material was observed in soil around the house, the garage or the shed.

The paint on the window frames contains lead above the threshold. Other painted surfaces sampled did
not.

No PCB capacitors were found in the light fittings.

Dividing fences are timber paling and are retained.

## Recommendations

1. Include this report and its asbestos register in the tender documents so the builders can price
   the removal.
2. All asbestos-containing materials are to be removed by a licensed asbestos removalist before
   demolition, with a clearance certificate issued by a licensed asbestos assessor before demolition
   proceeds.
3. Lead paint on the window frames is to be handled under the builder's demolition work plan, with
   dust controls and the frames disposed of as lead waste.
4. The demolition work plan is to include an unexpected-finds procedure: stop work, isolate the area
   and call in an assessor if any suspect material is found that is not in the register.

## Limitations

Wall cavities were sampled at representative points only. The underside of the concrete slab under
the bathroom could not be reached. Any material found later is to be treated as asbestos until it is
sampled.
