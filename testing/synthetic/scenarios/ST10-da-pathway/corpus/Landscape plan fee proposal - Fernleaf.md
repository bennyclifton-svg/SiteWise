## Our understanding

A new two-storey house is proposed on the lot, under a development application to Sutherland Shire
Council. Council's DCP requires a landscape plan with the application, showing the landscaped area,
deep soil zones and the planting for the front and rear gardens. Thank you for the survey and the
concept sketches.

## Scope

**DA landscape plan.** A landscape plan to the DCP for lodgement with the development application:

- landscaped area and deep soil calculations, coordinated with the architect's site plan
- front garden, driveway edges and rear garden layout, with paving, turf and garden beds
- a planting schedule of mostly local native species, with pot sizes and numbers
- the retained trees shown with protection fencing, as set out in the survey
- the rainwater tank, drying court and side paths

**Landscape documentation for the CC.** Updating the plan to the conditions of consent, with
construction details for edging, paving and garden beds, and a short specification for the builder's
landscaper.
