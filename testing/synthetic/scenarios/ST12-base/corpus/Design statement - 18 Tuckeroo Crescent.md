# Design statement: secondary dwelling

Saltbush Building Design Pty Ltd
Project: 18 Tuckeroo Crescent, Gymea NSW 2227 (Lot 32 DP 237114)
Client: Graham and Lena Whitfield
Issued for: complying development certificate

## Project description

The secondary dwelling is a Class 1a building under the NCC.

The secondary dwelling is a new detached building; the works do not alter the existing building on the
lot, the main house.

- Secondary dwelling gross floor area: 60 m2 (main house unchanged)
- Garage spaces: 0 (no garage or car space; the occupant parks on the street)

The secondary dwelling is a single-storey building with 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom. The laundry is a
European laundry within the bathroom.

The proposal meets the complying development standards and a CDC will be sought from a registered
certifier under the Housing Code.

## Site description and context

The site is a standard suburban lot with no environmental sensitivities such as bushland, creeks or
threatened species. The rear yard is level lawn, reached from the street by the driveway on the eastern
side of the house.

The flat is about 7 m clear of the main house and at least 900 mm from every boundary. It has a 3.0 m
rear setback and a 0.9 m western side setback. The flat's private open space is a paved courtyard on its
northern side, screened from the main house by a fence and garden planting.

## Design

The secondary dwelling is detached and free-standing. The open kitchen and living area faces north onto
the courtyard through sliding doors. The two bedrooms sit at the southern end, either side of the
bathroom, each with built-in robes.

The rear yard is clear lawn; no demolition is required. As the flat is detached, no separating wall is
needed. This is a secondary dwelling, not a dual occupancy, and it cannot be subdivided.

A path runs from the street along the driveway to the flat's entry, so the occupant does not pass
through the main house.

## Materials

Built on site in timber frame by the builder, on a slab designed by the structural engineer.

- Walls: fibre cement weatherboard cladding, painted
- Roof: metal skillion roof falling to the south
- Windows: powder-coated aluminium, with the glazing set out in the BASIX certificate
- Insulation, hot water and lighting as committed in the BASIX certificate

Climate zone 5. The courtyard glazing faces north under a 600 mm eave to keep out the summer sun.

## Services

The electrician's maximum demand calculation confirms the existing supply is adequate. A sub-main runs
in a trench from the main house's switchboard to a sub-board in the flat.

Water is branched from the house supply after the meter. Drainage connects to the house drain by
gravity. Roof water is piped to the stormwater line as shown on the stormwater plan.

## Trees

No trees on or next to the site are affected by the works. No neighbouring or street trees are affected
by the works. No tree removal or pruning is proposed.

## Planning

As complying development, no clause 4.6 variation is sought or possible. As complying development, the
application is not referred to the local planning panel. No public exhibition or community
consultation; the certifier notifies the neighbours before determining the CDC.
