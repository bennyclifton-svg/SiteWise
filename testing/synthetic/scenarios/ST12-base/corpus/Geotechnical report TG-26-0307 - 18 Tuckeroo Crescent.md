## 1. Introduction

This report presents the results of a geotechnical investigation for a new single-storey granny flat in
the rear yard of the property, carried out in accordance with our proposal TG-P-0307. Its purpose is to
classify the site to AS 2870 and to recommend footings and a slab for the flat.

## 2. Fieldwork

Fieldwork was carried out on 24 March 2026. Two boreholes (BH1 and BH2) were drilled by hand auger
within the proposed footprint, about 8 m apart. A dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) test was carried out
beside each borehole. The equipment was brought down the side driveway. Underground services were
checked before drilling. Borehole locations are shown on Figure 1.

## 3. Subsurface conditions

The profile was consistent across the two boreholes:

| Depth (m) | Material |
|---|---|
| 0.0 to 0.2 | Topsoil: dark brown sandy silt with roots, moist |
| 0.2 to 1.6 | Silty clay: orange brown and grey, medium plasticity, stiff to very stiff |
| 1.6 to 2.5 | Sandstone: extremely to highly weathered, recovered as clayey sand |

Both boreholes were terminated at 2.5 m. DCP blow counts increased steadily with depth, with refusal
in the weathered sandstone in BH2 at 2.3 m.

No groundwater was encountered in the boreholes to 2.5 m. Groundwater levels can change after rain.

## 4. Laboratory testing

Shrink-swell tests were carried out on two undisturbed samples of the silty clay:

| Sample | Depth (m) | Shrink-swell index (%) |
|---|---|---|
| BH1 | 0.5 to 0.7 | 2.1 |
| BH2 | 0.8 to 1.0 | 2.4 |

## 5. Site classification

On the soil profile and the laboratory results, and with a characteristic surface movement estimated
at 25 mm:

Site classification: Class M in accordance with AS 2870.

The classification assumes the site is maintained as described in CSIRO Building Technology File 18,
with surface water kept away from the building.

## 6. Footing and slab recommendations

A stiffened raft or a waffle slab designed to AS 2870 for a Class M site is suitable. Edge beams and
internal beams should be founded in the stiff silty clay below the topsoil, for which an allowable
bearing pressure of 100 kPa may be adopted.

No basement is proposed; the flat is a slab on ground.

Edge beam excavations along the western side, 900 mm from the boundary fence, are shallow and do not
undermine the fence or the neighbour's footings. No excavation is proposed next to the neighbouring
dwellings; the slab edge beams are shallow.

## 7. Earthworks

Excavate to a depth of 200 mm to strip topsoil under the slab platform and for 0.5 m beyond its
edge. Any fill placed to level the platform should be compacted in layers
of not more than 200 mm to at least 98% of standard maximum dry density, and tested.

## 8. Site drainage

Finished ground should fall away from the slab by at least 50 mm over the first 1 m. Do not let water
pond against the edge beams. Downpipes should be connected to the stormwater system before the roof is
finished.

## 9. Limitations

This report is based on two boreholes. Conditions between and beyond them may differ. We should be
told if the conditions found during construction differ from those described here.
