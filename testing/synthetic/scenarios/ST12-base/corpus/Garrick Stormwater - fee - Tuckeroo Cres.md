Dear Mia,

Thank you for the drawings and the survey. We are pleased to offer our services for the stormwater
design of the new secondary dwelling.

SCOPE

Roof water from the flat will be piped by gravity to the existing stormwater line and on to the kerb
in Tuckeroo Crescent. We will size the gutters, downpipes and pipework, confirm the discharge point,
and show the levels on a stormwater plan for the certifier.

The development is exempt from on-site detention under council's stormwater policy. There is no
existing on-site detention on the lot, and none is needed. No stormwater quality treatment or MUSIC
modelling is required for a secondary dwelling. The site is outside the drinking water catchment, so no
NorBE assessment applies. No public road works are required.
