## Scope

A BASIX certificate is needed for the new dwelling before the complying development certificate can be
issued. We will:

- model the flat's thermal performance using the NatHERS thermal method, from the concept drawings;
- complete the energy and water sections, including the hot water system, lighting and fixtures;
- issue the BASIX certificate and mark the commitments on the CDC plans with the designer.

The site is in NCC climate zone 5.

If the design changes before the CDC is lodged, we will revise the model and issue one revised
certificate.
