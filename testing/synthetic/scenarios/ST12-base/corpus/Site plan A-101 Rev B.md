# Site plan

Saltbush Building Design Pty Ltd
Project: Secondary dwelling, 18 Tuckeroo Crescent, Gymea NSW 2227
Client: Graham and Lena Whitfield
Scale: 1:200 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Lot boundaries, dimensions and north point
- Main house outline and the new secondary dwelling footprint
- Setbacks to the boundaries and to the main house
