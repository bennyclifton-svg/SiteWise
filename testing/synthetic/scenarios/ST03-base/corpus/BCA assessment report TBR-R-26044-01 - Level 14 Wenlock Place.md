# BCA assessment report

Project: Avenor head office fit-out, Level 14, 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney NSW 2060
Client: Avenor Capital Partners Pty Ltd
Prepared for: Larchmont Project Services Pty Ltd

## Purpose

This report assesses the concept layout of the Level 14 fit-out by Corrigan Hale
Interiors against the Deemed-to-Satisfy provisions of NCC 2022 Volume One. This report supports the
complying development certificate application.

## Building description

The office levels are a Class 5 building. The building has a rise in storeys of 24 (24 storeys
above ground). The building is of Type A construction. The building is sprinklered throughout to
AS 2118.1. The existing building was constructed in 2008. The site is in NCC climate zone 5.

The works are confined to Level 14, 1,200 m2 NLA.

## Egress

Egress is via the two base-building fire stairs in the core. The fit-out layout keeps the paths of
travel from every work point, office and meeting room to the exits within the deemed-to-satisfy
distances, and the distance between alternative exits is maintained. Doors on the path of travel
swing in the direction of egress where required and have hardware that opens without a key from the
tenancy side.

The layout complies with the DTS provisions and no performance solution is proposed.

## Fire safety measures

The fit-out alters the following base-building measures:

- sprinkler heads relocated and additional heads added to suit the new partitions and bulkheads
- smoke detectors relocated and added in the enclosed rooms
- EWIS speakers relocated and added so that the tone is audible in every room
- emergency lighting and exit signs extended to the new layout

These measures will be listed on the fire safety schedule for the CDC, and the altered measures
certified at completion for the final fire safety certificate.

There are no special hazards and no dangerous goods storage on the floor. There are no vulnerable
occupants: office staff do not need assisted evacuation. The fit-out involves no facade, cladding,
timber or structural work that would need a fire performance solution.

## Energy efficiency

Section J applies to the new work: the lighting power density and lighting controls, and any new
air-conditioning plant, must meet the DTS provisions. BASIX is not applicable to a Class 5 building.

## Approvals

This report supports the complying development certificate application. The Design and Building
Practitioners Act does not apply to this Class 5 fit-out.

## Conclusion

Subject to the matters above being shown in the CDC documentation, the fit-out is capable of
complying with the DTS provisions of NCC 2022.
