## Our understanding

The tenant is fitting out the whole of Level 14 of Wenlock Place, an occupied office tower, as its
head office. The interior designer and the services engineers will document the works, supported
by a BCA assessment report and an access report. You have asked us to assess and determine the
complying development certificate and to act as principal certifier through to the occupation
certificate.

We offer the following certification services for the fit-out of Level 14.

| | **Total** | **$17,000** |
