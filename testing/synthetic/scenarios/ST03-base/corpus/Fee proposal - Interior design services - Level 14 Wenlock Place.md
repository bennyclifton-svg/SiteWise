## Our understanding

Avenor Capital Partners is fitting out the whole of Level 14 at Wenlock Place as its head office. The
floor is handed over in base-building condition. We will act as design lead and coordinate the
services engineers, BCA and access consultants and the certifier, and we will prepare the
submissions to the landlord for concept and detailed design approval.

progresses.
