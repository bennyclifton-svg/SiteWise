## Our understanding

Avenor Capital Partners is fitting out the whole of Level 14 of Wenlock Place as its head office,
with a fit-out construction budget set in the project brief. You need cost advice through design, a
pre-tender estimate to check the documents against the budget, and an evaluation of the lump-sum
tenders. We will also prepare a total project budget that brings together the tenant's other costs.

## Scope and fee

| Item | Description | Fee (ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Concept cost plan including the total project budget (FF&E, IT, AV, relocation and landlord charges) | $4,500 |
| 2 | Design development cost plan | $5,000 |
| 3 | Pre-tender estimate | $4,000 |
| 4 | Tender evaluation | $3,000 |
| | **Total** | **$16,500** |

Total fee $16,500 excluding GST.

After-hours and isolation costs are priced in each estimate, so the cost plans reflect the
landlord's building rules for an occupied tower.

Each estimate will be issued in elemental format with a summary against the budget, a list of
assumptions and exclusions, and a recommended contingency. We will meet the design team at each
cost plan issue to agree savings where the estimate exceeds the budget.

## Terms

Fees exclude GST. Each item is invoiced on delivery, payable within 14 days.
