# Owner's brief: dual occupancy at 18 Wattlebird Street

## Project description

The project comprises demolition of the existing house and construction of an attached dual occupancy (two attached two-storey townhouses) with Torrens title subdivision, giving us 2 dwellings on proposed Lots 1 and 2.

The site contains an existing building (a single-storey dwelling and garage), to be demolished. The existing dwelling was built in 1958 and has had very little done to it since. We have owned it for six years and let it out for most of that time. Both dwellings are new builds on the cleared lot.

The house is let until the lease ends on 31 October 2026 and will be empty before demolition starts, so the site will be vacant for the whole of the works.

We are the sole owners and the only decision makers. Either of us can give instructions, and we will answer questions within a few days. We plan to keep one dwelling to live in and sell the other once the subdivision is registered.

## What we want

- 4 bedrooms in each dwelling, with the main bedroom upstairs at the back
- Open-plan living and dining on the ground floor of each, opening onto a private rear courtyard
- A garage for each dwelling, entered from the front
- Good storage, a separate laundry and a study nook in each
- Both dwellings to look good from the street without being identical

No further dwellings are proposed. We want the pair to suit a family each, not to squeeze the most out of the land.

## Scope, budget and quality

Site area about 700 m2. Target gross floor area about 460 m2 in total.

Construction budget for both dwellings: $1,650,000 excluding GST. That figure is for the building work, including demolition and site works, and does not include consultants' fees, council fees or our own costs.

Mid-range finishes: good quality and durable, nothing high end. We would rather spend on insulation, windows and a solid build than on fancy fittings.

No basement or cellar.

## Planning and contract

We will seek a complying development certificate for an attached dual occupancy under the Low Rise Housing Diversity Code. The subdivision follows under its own complying development certificate once the dual occupancy is approved. We expect our architect to prepare and lodge the CDC application with a registered certifier.

Architect-led traditional lump-sum tender to three or four residential builders. The builder will be engaged under the HIA NSW Lump Sum building contract, and we would like the architect to help us through the tender and the contract signing.

No staged construction: one builder, one contract, both dwellings built together. We are not planning multiple buildings: the dwellings are attached under one roof.

## Services and sustainability

Both dwellings will be all-electric, with no natural gas connection. We want heat-pump hot water and a BASIX rainwater tank for each dwelling.

No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target beyond BASIX. We do not need any Connecting with Country input for this lot.
