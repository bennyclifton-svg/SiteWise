## Purpose and method

This report accompanies our detail, level and boundary survey of the property, carried out on 17
February 2026. Levels are on Australian Height Datum (AHD), derived from a permanent mark in
Wattlebird Street. Features were located by total station and are shown on the survey plan to the
accuracy normal for a detail survey at 1:200.

## Site description

Site area by survey: 700.4 m2.

The lot is rectangular, about 41.2 m deep. Full-width street frontage of 17.0 m with unrestricted vehicle access from Wattlebird Street.

The land falls gently from the rear boundary to the street, by about 0.8 m over the depth of the
lot. Levels range from about RL 98.6 at the rear boundary to RL 97.8 at the front boundary.

## Boundaries and easements

The boundaries were identified against the deposited plan and the fence positions were compared with
the title dimensions. Boundary fences are generally within 0.1 m of the true boundaries. No easements
are shown on the title or the deposited plan.

## Existing structures

The site contains an existing building: a single-storey brick dwelling with a detached fibro garage.

- Floor level of the house: RL 98.45 (front entry) to RL 98.52 (rear door)
- Garage slab: RL 98.10

## Services

Services were located from visible features and the Before You Dig Australia plans. Underground
services should be confirmed on site before any work.

- Sewer: The house drains to the Sydney Water sewer main in the street; the junction is at the front boundary. No Sydney Water assets are within the property; the sewer main is in the street.
- Water: Water main in Wattlebird Street; the house is connected to it. The meter is at the front boundary.
- Gas: There is no gas main in Wattlebird Street.
- Electricity: overhead supply from a pole on the verge to the front of the house.
- Telecommunications: NBN pit at the front boundary.
- Stormwater: There is no existing on-site detention on the lot; roof water drains by gravity to the kerb.

## Limitations

This survey shows the site as it was on the date of the survey. Boundary dimensions are from the
title and the deposited plan; no boundary marks were placed. The survey plan should be read with this
report.
