Thank you for meeting us on site and for your brief of 2 February 2026. We are pleased to offer our
architectural services for the demolition of the existing house, the new attached dual occupancy under

The architect's services run from concept design through to lodging the CDC application on the NSW Planning Portal, and then continue with the construction documentation and the tender to residential builders.

| Stage 3: Construction documentation | Detailed construction drawings and details, specification and finishes schedules for the builder and tender, coordinated with the engineers | $38,000 |
| Stage 4: Tender | Tender documents, tender queries, tender analysis and a recommendation report | $7,000 |
| **Total** | | **$85,000** |

Total fee $85,000 excluding GST. All fees exclude GST.
