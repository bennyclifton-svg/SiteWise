## Scope

Thank you for the opportunity to submit this proposal. We understand that an AS 2870 site
classification is needed across the footprint of the new attached two-storey townhouses, for the
structural engineer's slab and footing design.

We propose four boreholes, each with a dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) test, located within the new
footprint and clear of the house. The boreholes are drilled with a small track-mounted rig to about
3 m or refusal. Access to the front and rear yards will be arranged through the owners' managing agent.
