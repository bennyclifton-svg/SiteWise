## Introduction

Thank you for the development brief of 2 February 2026. We are pleased to offer our architectural
services for the new six-storey residential flat building of 64 apartments over two basement levels.
This proposal covers the design from concept through full construction documentation for a
traditional lump-sum procurement. Our documentation stages are Stage 4: tender documents for a
lump-sum tender under AS 4000; Stage 5: for-construction documents. We remain engaged by the
developer through construction; no novation. Design support during construction is priced as an
option.

## Scope of services

**Stage 1: Concept design and yield study.** Site analysis, planning envelope study against the
Ryde controls and the Apartment Design Guide, yield and mix testing, two massing options and one
preferred concept with an area schedule. One presentation to you and the pre-DA meeting with
council.

**Stage 2: DA documentation, design verification statement and SEPP Housing Chapter 4 design report.**
Developed plans, elevations, sections, shadow diagrams, view and solar analysis, ADG compliance
table, schedule of finishes and the architectural input to the statement of environmental effects.
Coordination of the DA consultant reports.

The town planner lodges the DA on the NSW Planning Portal; we provide the drawings and the design
verification statement.

**Stage 3: DA assessment, responses to council and the planning panel.** Responses to council
requests for information, amended drawings where needed, and attendance at the panel meeting.

**Stage 4: Tender documents.** Full construction documentation for the lump-sum tender: general
arrangement plans, elevations and sections, wall types, detailed sections and details, door, window,
hardware and finishes schedules, the architectural specification, and coordination of the
consultant documents into one tender set. Responses to tenderer questions and addenda, and our input
to the tender assessment.

**Stage 5: For-construction documents, regulated designs and design compliance declarations.**
Incorporation of tender clarifications and construction certificate comments, the issue of the
for-construction set, and the architectural regulated designs and design compliance declarations
under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020.
