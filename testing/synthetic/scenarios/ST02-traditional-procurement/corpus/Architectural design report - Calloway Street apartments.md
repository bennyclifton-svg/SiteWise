Project: Calloway Street apartments, 18-22 Calloway Street, West Ryde NSW 2114
Client: Tallis Grove Developments Pty Ltd
Purpose: design report for the development application, addressing Chapter 4 of the Housing SEPP and
the Apartment Design Guide

## Project description

NCC building classification: Class 2 apartments over a Class 7a basement car park.

The building is six storeys above two basement levels. Gross floor area 6,184 m2.

64 apartments: 14 one-bedroom, 38 two-bedroom and 12 three-bedroom. Every apartment has a private
balcony or ground floor terrace, and 70% of apartments receive at least two hours of sun to living
rooms and balconies in midwinter.

Two basement levels with 71 car spaces, reached by a single ramp from Calloway Street. The basement
also holds bicycle parking, storage cages, the bin room and the plant rooms.

## Site and context

The site is a standard urban infill site with no environmental sensitivities such as bushland,
waterways or habitat. The neighbouring properties are zoned R4 and are a mix of older dwellings and
walk-up apartment blocks, several of them likely to be redeveloped under the same controls.

The building is set back from the side and rear boundaries in line with the ADG separation
distances, and the upper level is set back further from the street to reduce its apparent scale.

## Built form and facade

Facade system: face brick and rendered masonry with standard aluminium windows; no curtain wall or
rainscreen. The brick base, in a warm red blend, relates to the older dwellings in the street. The
upper levels are rendered in a light colour with recessed balconies and metal balustrades.

A rooftop communal open space with shade structures, planting and seating faces north and east.

## Services

The building is sprinkler protected throughout.

All-electric building: induction cooktops and heat-pump hot water; no natural gas supply. The car park
has EV-ready circuits to each space, sized for future chargers.

The site is in NCC climate zone 5. The design meets the BASIX energy, water and thermal comfort
targets, with cross ventilation to 64% of the apartments.

The basement has drained cavity basement walls with sub-floor drainage to a sump.

## Landscape and public domain

Deep soil zones of 8% of the site area are provided along the rear
boundary.

No trees on or next to the site are affected by the works. The new vehicle crossing is placed clear
of the three existing brush box street trees, which are retained. New street trees will be planted;
no existing street or neighbouring trees are affected. No trees are pruned or removed.

## Access and waste

The basement serves cars only; no heavy vehicle access is needed and waste is collected from the kerb.
Bins are moved from the basement bin room to the kerb by the building manager on collection days.

## Delivery

The works are delivered in one stage; the construction certificate is issued in two parts: Stage 1
CC (excavation and shoring) and Stage 2 CC (building).
