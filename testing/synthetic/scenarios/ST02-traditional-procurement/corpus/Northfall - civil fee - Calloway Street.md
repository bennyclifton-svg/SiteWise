# Fee proposal: civil engineering

Dear Priya,

Thank you for the brief and the survey. We are pleased to offer our fee for the civil and stormwater
engineering for the residential flat building at Calloway Street, through to the documents for the
lump-sum tender and the final design for construction.

## Background

The site falls to the street and drains to the kerb in Calloway Street. The new building will
discharge by gravity to council's system in the street. We expect an OSD tank under the driveway near
the frontage, and a pump-out system for the basement ramp and car park floor drainage.

On-site detention is required under council's stormwater policy. MUSIC modelling is required to show
the pollutant reduction targets are met. The site is outside the drinking water catchment, so no
NorBE assessment applies.

Works within the road reserve: new footpath, kerb and gutter and vehicle crossing under a s138
consent. We will prepare the road works drawings for council's approval.
