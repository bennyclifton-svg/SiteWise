## Introduction

This report presents the results of a geotechnical investigation for a proposed six-storey
residential flat building over two basement levels. It was carried out in accordance with our
proposal BRG-P-1152. We were given the detail survey by Corrigan Lane Surveyors (CLS-26-0217) and the
architect's concept plans.

## Fieldwork

Six boreholes (BH1 to BH6) were drilled between 23 and 26 March 2026 with a track-mounted rig, by
auger through soil and NMLC coring in rock, to depths of 11.6 m to 12.2 m. Standpipes were installed
in BH2 and BH5 and read weekly until 23 April 2026. The logs and core photographs are in Appendix A.

## Subsurface profile

## Groundwater

No groundwater was encountered in the boreholes to the depth investigated, and both standpipes stayed
dry over four weeks. Minor seepage may occur at the soil and rock interface after heavy rain.

## The proposed basement

Two basement levels excavated to a depth of about 7 m. Bulk excavation will extend through the soil
and shale into the sandstone.

Excavation extends to within 1 m of the northern boundary and the neighbouring dwelling. The basement
is set back between 2.5 m and 4 m from the southern boundary and about 6 m from the rear boundary,
where the deep soil zone is.

## Retention

- A contiguous piled wall, with piles socketed into the sandstone below bulk excavation level, is
  recommended for the full perimeter.
- Temporary ground anchors will be needed until the basement slabs are built. Anchors under
  neighbouring lots need the owners' consent; where consent is not given, internal propping is
  required.
- The sandstone below the pile toes may be cut vertically, subject to inspection by a geotechnical
  engineer as each 1.5 m lift is exposed.

## Drainage

A drained basement is suitable: a sub-floor drainage layer below the lowest slab and drainage behind
the shotcrete infill panels between piles, both discharging to a sump.

## Further recommendations

- Dilapidation surveys of the neighbouring dwellings on the northern, southern and western boundaries
  before excavation starts.
- Vibration limits for rock excavation near the northern boundary, with monitoring.
- Excavation inspections by a geotechnical engineer at the pile toes and each lift of rock face.
