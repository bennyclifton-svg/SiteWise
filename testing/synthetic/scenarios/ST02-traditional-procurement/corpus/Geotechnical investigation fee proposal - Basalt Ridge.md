## Purpose

We understand that a six-storey residential flat building with two basement levels is proposed
across the three lots. The investigation will give the subsurface profile, rock strength and design
parameters for the basement retention, footings and slab, and the site classification.

## Proposed investigation

- Six cored boreholes to 12 m below existing ground, drilled by a track-mounted rig in the rear yards
  and driveways of the three dwellings, with rock coring once rock is reached
- Two standpipes installed in selected boreholes, with water level readings over four weeks
- Laboratory testing of soil and rock samples: moisture content, Atterberg limits, point load index
  and aggressivity
- A geotechnical report with the borehole logs, the subsurface profile and retention parameters

We will need access to the three dwellings' yards, coordinated through your property manager.
