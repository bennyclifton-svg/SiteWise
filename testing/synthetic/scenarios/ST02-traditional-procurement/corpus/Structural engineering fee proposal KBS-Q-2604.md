## Our understanding

The project is a six-storey residential flat building of 64 apartments over two basement levels of
car parking. We have reviewed the development brief and the architect's preliminary massing. We
understand we are engaged by Tallis Grove Developments through construction, and that the builder
will price a full set of construction documents.

We expect a reinforced concrete frame with post-tensioned flat plate slabs on a regular column grid,
with the lift and stair core providing lateral stability. Columns run straight from the basement to
the roof; no transfer structure. The apartment layouts above can be arranged around the car park
grid, and we will work with the architect at concept to keep it that way.

The basement will need perimeter retention. Pending the geotechnical report, we have allowed for a
contiguous piled wall with temporary anchors, and a suspended or on-ground
basement slab as the ground conditions allow.
