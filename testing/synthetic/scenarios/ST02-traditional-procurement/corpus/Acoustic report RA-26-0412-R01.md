## Introduction

This report supports the development application for a six-storey residential flat building of 64
apartments over two basement levels. It assesses external noise intrusion, noise from the building's
mechanical plant, and sound insulation between apartments. It was prepared from the architect's
drawings issued for coordination on 25 May 2026.

## Road traffic noise assessment

An unattended noise logger was placed at the Calloway Street frontage from 12 to 19 May 2026, with
attended measurements on 12 and 19 May. Calloway Street is a narrow local street carrying mainly
residents' cars.

Road traffic noise from the local street is low and standard glazing achieves the internal criteria.
Measured levels were LAeq(15 hour) 54 dB(A) by day and LAeq(9 hour) 47 dB(A) at night at the
frontage. With standard 6.38 mm laminated glazing and windows closed, the internal levels meet the
Apartment Design Guide and the City of Ryde DCP criteria in bedrooms and living rooms.

## Mechanical plant

The rooftop air-conditioning condensers and the basement car park exhaust fans meet the noise policy
criteria at the nearest neighbour, provided that:

- the condensers are inside a screened enclosure with a solid lower section 1.5 m high
- the car park exhaust discharge is fitted with an attenuator and faces away from the northern
  neighbour
- plant selections are checked by an acoustic consultant before the construction certificate

## Internal sound insulation

Walls and floors between apartments are to meet NCC 2022 Volume One Part F7 sound insulation
requirements. The masonry and concrete construction shown can achieve the required ratings, with
acoustic underlay to hard floor finishes and discontinuous construction where wet areas adjoin
habitable rooms of another apartment.

## Recommendations

1. Standard laminated glazing as described is adequate for all facades.
2. Mechanical plant screening and attenuation as above.
3. Sound insulation details to be confirmed at construction certificate stage.
4. Acoustic review of the final plant selections.
