## 1. Purpose

We understand a new two-storey house with a slab on ground is proposed on the vacant lot. The
structural engineer needs an AS 2870 site classification and footing advice for the slab design and
the construction certificate.

## 2. Proposed work

- Review of the survey, the published geology map and our records for nearby sites
- Dial Before You Dig search and a services scan at each borehole location
- Three boreholes drilled with a track-mounted rig to 3.0 m or refusal, logged by an engineer
- Dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) tests next to each borehole
- Undisturbed samples for laboratory shrink-swell testing
- A report with the subsurface profile, AS 2870 site classification, footing and slab advice, and
  earthworks and site drainage advice

## 5. Assumptions
