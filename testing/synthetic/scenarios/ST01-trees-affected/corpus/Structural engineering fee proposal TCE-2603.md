## Our understanding

The project is a new two-storey house with an attached double garage, about 300 m2, with brick veneer
to the ground floor, lightweight cladding above and a metal roof. We expect a stiffened raft slab
designed to AS 2870, timber wall and floor framing, and steel beams where openings are wide. No
transfer structures are expected for a two-storey timber-framed house.

## Scope

### Concept and DA structural advice

Review of the concept plans, advice on spans, beam depths and slab type, and a brief structural note
for the DA set if council asks for one.

### Detailed design and CC documentation

Slab and footing design to AS 2870 using the geotechnical report, steel beam and post design,
framing review against AS 1684, bracing and tie-down review, structural drawings and notes for the
construction certificate.

### Design certificate for the CC

A design certificate for the construction certificate, issued with the structural drawings.

### Site inspections

Slab reinforcement and framing inspections during construction, if you need them, with a short
inspection report and certificate after each visit.
