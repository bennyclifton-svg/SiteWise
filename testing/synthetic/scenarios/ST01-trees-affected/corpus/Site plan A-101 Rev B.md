# Site plan

Harbourline Architects Pty Ltd
Project: New house, 27 Corella Rise, Denistone NSW 2114
Client: Dan and Mai Nguyen
Scale: 1:200 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Lot boundaries, dimensions and north point
- Footprint of the new two-storey house with the double garage
- Driveway and vehicle crossing to Corella Rise
- Front, side and rear setbacks
- Rainwater tank location
- Existing blue gum near the rear boundary, to be retained, with its canopy spread
- Neighbour's jacaranda on No. 25 near the northern boundary, with its TPZ and SRZ
- Tree protection zones, protection fencing and the pier-and-beam rear terrace edge from
  arboricultural impact assessment CLA-AIA-0927

## Site data

- Site area 650.2 m2
- Double garage
- Landscaped area 315 m2 (48% of the site)
- Front setback 7.5 m; side setbacks 1.2 m ground floor; first floor 1.5 m south, 1.8 m north; rear setback 11.0 m
- Driveway 3.0 m wide in concrete to the garage
- Rainwater tank 5,000 L moved to the rear of the garage, behind the side gate
