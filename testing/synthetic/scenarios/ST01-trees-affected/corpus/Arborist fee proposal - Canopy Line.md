## 1. Background

Thank you for sending the survey and the current site plan. We understand a new two-storey house with
a double garage is proposed on the vacant lot, and that the development application is being prepared
now. Council will want an arboricultural impact assessment with the application because two trees
are close to the proposed works. From the survey and site plan Rev A:

- The driveway cut encroaches into the tree protection zone of the neighbour's jacaranda at No. 25.
- The rear slab edge is within the structural root zone of the on-site blue gum.

Both trees are to be retained. Our role is to confirm the extent of each encroachment, advise on
design changes that keep the trees viable, and set out the protection measures for the builder.

## 2. Scope of services

**Stage 1: Arboricultural impact assessment (AS 4970)**

- Site inspection and visual tree assessment (VTA) of both trees from ground level
- Trunk diameter measurements and calculation of the tree protection zone (TPZ) and structural root
  zone (SRZ) for each tree
- Assessment of the encroachments from the house footprint, terrace slab and driveway against the
  plans
- Design recommendations to reduce the root impact
- A report suitable for submission to the City of Ryde with the development application

**Stage 2: Tree protection plan and specification**

- A tree protection plan marked up on the architect's site plan
- Protective fencing, ground protection and trunk protection details
- A specification for work inside the TPZ, including hand digging and the cutting of roots
- Hold points for the builder and the certifier

**Stage 3: Construction supervision hold points**

- Two site visits during construction: one to certify the protection fencing before work starts,
  and one to supervise the driveway cut and the terrace pier holes inside the TPZ
- A short compliance certificate after each visit

## 5. Assumptions and exclusions

- Access to the neighbour's jacaranda is from our client's side of the boundary fence; the tree is
  assessed visually from there
- No climbing inspection, root mapping by ground-penetrating radar or resistograph decay assessment
  is included; if the visual assessment shows these are needed, we will advise and price them
  separately
- Tree work, if any, is by a qualified arborist engaged by the builder
- Council fees and any tree permit fees are excluded
