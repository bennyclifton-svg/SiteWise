# Basement fire pump room: general arrangement

Calder Fire and Hydraulic Engineers Pty Ltd
Project: Sprinkler water supply upgrade, 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060
Client: Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Scale: 1:50 at A1

## Sheet shows

- Electric duty pumpset and diesel standby pumpset on separate skids.
- Jockey pump and the pump controllers on the east wall.
- 150 kL modular tank across the former store and two former car spaces, with access ladder and
  inspection hatch.
- Suction pipework from the tank to both pumpsets.
- Test line with flow meter, returning to the tank.
- Rated enclosure to the enlarged pump room, with a self-closing door.

## General notes

1. Do not scale from this drawing. Use written dimensions.
2. Read with the Calder options report CFH-R-26-019-01.
3. Sprinkler pumpsets and pipework to AS 2118.1 and AS 2941.
4. Set-out of plinths to be confirmed.
