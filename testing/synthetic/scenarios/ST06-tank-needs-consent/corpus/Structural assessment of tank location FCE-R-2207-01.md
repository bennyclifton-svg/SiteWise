# Structural assessment: sprinkler tank location and pump room slab

Prepared by Ferrule Consulting Engineers Pty Ltd for Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Site: 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060
Author: Priya Raman, Principal

## Purpose

This report assesses the existing building's basement slab at the proposed location of the new tank and
pumpsets. The tank is proposed over the existing basement store next to the pump room and the two
adjoining car spaces.

## Existing structure

The building was constructed in 1988. The basement floor is a 200 mm conventionally reinforced slab on
ground. We confirmed the record drawings on site by scanning the slab in the store, the two car spaces
and the pump room in March 2026; bar sizes and spacings match the drawings.

The tank does not sit on or near any transfer slab or transfer beam. The columns nearest the store
run straight down to their footings.

## Tank load

The 150 kL tank's operating load is carried on a new reinforced plinth, which spreads it within the
slab's capacity. No strengthening is needed beneath the store or the two car spaces. The pumpset skids sit on local
thickenings of the slab, detailed on our drawings.

## Core holes

The slab and the store wall are to be scanned before any coring. Core positions for the suction and
test pipework are set clear of the reinforcement in the store wall.

## Scope limits

No basement is built or excavated: the pump room is in the existing basement car park level.
No excavation is involved, and nothing is done near the adjoining boundaries or neighbouring structures.
Groundwater is therefore not encountered and no dewatering is needed.

## Conclusion

The existing slab under the store and the two car spaces can carry the tank on the proposed plinth
without strengthening. We will inspect the
plinth and the thickenings before concrete is placed.
