# Statement of environmental effects: fire pump room, tank and level 3 fit-out

Prepared by Quillon Planning Pty Ltd for Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Site: 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060 (Lot 12 DP 1093476)
Author: Leah Moretti, Director

## Introduction

This statement accompanies a development application for the enlarged fire pump room and fire water storage tank, and the level 3 fit-out.
It describes the site and the proposal, assesses the matters relevant to the application, and
explains why the loss of two car spaces is acceptable. It should be read with the fire services
drawings by Calder Fire and Hydraulic Engineers (FS-101 Revision B) and the level 3 concept design by
Marlowe Interiors.

## Site and proposal

The site is in Zone E2 Commercial Centre under North Sydney Local Environmental Plan 2013. It holds
a 6-storey office building with a single basement car park level, constructed in 1988 and owned
solely by the applicant. Office premises are permitted with consent in the zone, and the proposal
does not change the use of the building.

The proposal has two parts:

- An enlarged fire pump room and a new fire water tank, both within the existing basement. The building's sprinkler water
  supply is upgraded with new duty, standby and jockey pumps drawing from a 150 kL tank. The tank and
  the enlarged pump room take the existing store and two car spaces, with a rated enclosure and
  screening to the car park side.
- The internal refurbishment and fit-out of the vacant level 3 as office space for a new tenant,
  with the sprinkler, detection and warning alterations that go with it.

No change is proposed to the building's height, floor space, external walls or street frontage.

## Car parking

The works remove two car spaces, leaving the building below the number required by the original
consent. The spaces are lost because the tank needs that floor area next to the existing pump room;
no other location in the building can take a tank of this size without displacing more spaces or
tenancy floor area.

Measured against the car parking rates in North Sydney Development Control Plan 2013, the building's
remaining provision is still within the range the plan seeks for office premises in the North Sydney
centre, where the plan applies maximum rates rather than minimums. The site is a short walk from
North Sydney station and from frequent bus services.

The two spaces were allocated to the vacant level 3 tenancy. The incoming tenant has accepted the
reduced allocation in its heads of terms, so no existing tenant loses a space.

In our opinion the loss of two spaces is consistent with the plan's aim of limiting car use in the
centre, and the variation from the original consent is justified.

## Heritage

The site is not a heritage item and is not within a heritage conservation area.

## Impacts

The works are carried out inside an occupied building. All floors except level 3 remain tenanted
during construction, and the fire services works will be programmed around the tenants, with
sprinkler shutdowns only out of hours. Deliveries come through the building's car park entry and
loading dock, booked in advance, with no use of the street for loading.

The pump room and tank are fully enclosed within the building. The diesel pump exhausts through the
existing plant exhaust shaft, clear of tenancies, air intakes and neighbours. There is no change to
the street frontage and no change to the external appearance of the building.

The level 3 fit-out is internal only. Together, the works improve the building's sprinkler water
supply for every floor and allow a vacant floor to be re-let.

## Determination

The application is a modest one with no clause 4.6 variation. We expect it to be determined by
council staff under delegation.

## Conclusion

The proposal upgrades an essential building service and brings a vacant floor back into use, with
the only external effect being the loss of two car spaces in a well-served commercial centre. We
recommend that the application be approved, subject to appropriate conditions.
