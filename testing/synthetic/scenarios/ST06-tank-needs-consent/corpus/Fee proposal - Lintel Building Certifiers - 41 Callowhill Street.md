## Approval pathway

Calder Fire and Hydraulic Engineers have now fixed the position of the new tank. With the enlarged
pump room, two of the basement car spaces will be lost to the new tank, which leaves the building
below the number of spaces required by the original consent.

We attended the pre-lodgement meeting with council on 16 April 2026.
Council advised that the tank and pump house are not complying development and a DA is required; the level 3 fit-out is included in the same development application.

Once consent is granted, we will act as principal certifier and issue the construction certificate
for both the fire services works and the level 3 fit-out. Council's consent is not expected to
require an upgrade of the existing fire safety measures; we will review the conditions when they
issue and advise if that changes.

The development application is expected to be determined by council staff under delegation, not a planning panel.
No clause 4.6 variation is involved.

## The building

The building is Class 5 (office) with a Class 7a basement car park.
This is a Class 5 office building, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act regulated-design obligations do not apply.
