## Background

Thank you for the invitation to propose. We understand that the new fire water tank and the enlarged
pump room will take two car spaces in the building's car park, leaving fewer spaces than the
original consent requires. At the pre-lodgement meeting on 16 April 2026, which we attended with Lintel Building Certifiers
at your request, council set out its position.
Council advised that the tank and pump house are not complying development and a DA is required.
The level 3 fit-out will be included in the same application, so that one consent covers both
packages before the certifier issues the construction certificate.

## Scope

We will prepare the planning case for the application, with the car parking assessment at its centre:
the loss of two spaces measured against the development control plan rates and the site's access to
public transport. We will coordinate the plans and supporting information from Calder Fire and
Hydraulic Engineers and Marlowe Interiors, prepare the application on the Planning Portal, manage
council's requests during assessment, and review the determination and its conditions with you.
