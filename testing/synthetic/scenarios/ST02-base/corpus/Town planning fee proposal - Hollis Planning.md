## The proposal

Tallis Grove proposes a six-storey residential flat building of 64 apartments over two basement
levels on three amalgamated R4 High Density Residential lots. We have reviewed the brief, the
planning certificate and the architect's early massing.

## Planning pathway

The proposal is development that needs consent under Ryde Local Environmental Plan 2014. It will be
assessed against Chapter 4 of the Housing SEPP and the Apartment Design Guide, the Ryde LEP 2014 and
the City of Ryde DCP 2014.

The application will be determined by the Ryde Local Planning Panel, as residential apartment
development to which Chapter 4 of the Housing SEPP applies.

The proposal complies with the height and FSR development standards; no clause 4.6 variation request
is required. We will confirm this against the survey and the architect's area schedule before
lodgement.

The development is not traffic-generating development and is not referred to Transport for NSW. No
intersection modelling or SIDRA analysis is required. No green travel plan is required.

Council will notify the application and place it on public exhibition; no community consultation
beyond that is proposed.

## Scope of services

Preparation and lodgement of the development application and statement of environmental effects, as
follows:

- Planning review of the concept against the Housing SEPP Chapter 4, the ADG, the Ryde LEP 2014 and
  DCP controls
- Preparation for and attendance at the pre-DA meeting with council, with a written summary of
  council's advice
- Statement of environmental effects, with the ADG compliance table prepared with the architect
- Coordination of the DA package and owner's consent

We will lodge the DA on the NSW Planning Portal; the architect does not lodge.

- Monitoring the assessment, responses to council requests for information and submissions
- Review of the draft conditions, preparation for and attendance at the panel meeting
