## Scope

We carried out a detail, level and boundary survey of the three lots on 17 and 18 February 2026.
Levels are on the Australian Height Datum, derived from a permanent mark in Calloway Street.
Boundaries were re-established from the deposited plan and existing survey marks. The survey drawing
CLS-26-0217 sheets 1 to 3 accompanies this report.

## Site description

The three lots together form a near-rectangular parcel with a 54.2 m frontage to Calloway Street.

Site area by survey: 2,403.6 m2

The land falls about 2.5 m from the south-west corner to the street frontage, fairly evenly across
the parcel. Spot levels, contours at 0.25 m intervals and the kerb and footpath levels are shown on
the drawing.

Construction access is constrained: the site fronts a narrow local street with kerbside parking on
both sides, adjoins dwellings on three sides, and all deliveries and cranage must work from the
street frontage. The carriageway is about 7.4 m between kerbs.

## Boundaries and easements

No easements, rights of way or restrictions on the use of land are recorded on the title search
supplied. Fences are generally within 0.15 m of the boundaries; offsets are shown on the drawing.

## Trees

Three brush box street trees stand on the council verge in front of the site. Their positions,
trunk diameters and canopy spreads are shown on the drawing. There are no trees within the lots
other than shrubs and garden beds.

## Services

Services were located from visible fittings and dial-before-you-dig plans. They have not been
proved by potholing.

The three dwellings are connected to the water main in Calloway Street. Each dwelling drains to the
Sydney Water sewer in the street. Connection to the existing water main in the street; no Sydney
Water assets cross the site.

- Overhead electricity from the street poles on the footpath in front of the site, with a service line to each
  dwelling
- NBN pit on the footpath in front of Lot 12
- Kerb outlets for roof water from each lot to Calloway Street

There is no existing on-site detention on any of the three lots.
