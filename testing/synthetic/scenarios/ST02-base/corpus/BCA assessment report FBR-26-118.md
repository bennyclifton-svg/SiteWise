# BCA assessment report

| Report | FBR-26-118 |
|---|---|
| Revision | 1 |
| Date | 08/06/2026 |
| Client | Tallis Grove Developments Pty Ltd |
| Project | Residential flat building, 18-22 Calloway Street, West Ryde NSW 2114 |
| Prepared by | Hannah Ostrowski, Registered Certifier |

## 1. Purpose

This report assesses the architectural drawings by Arden Vale Architects issued for coordination on
25 May 2026 against the
Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of NCC 2022 Volume One. It is for the development application and identifies the
matters to be resolved in the design for the construction certificate.

## 2. Building characteristics

Building classification: Class 2 (apartments) and Class 7a (basement car park).

Rise in storeys: 6

Effective height: about 18.6 m

Type of construction required: Type A.

## 3. Fire resistance and compartmentation

The building is Type A construction under Part C2. Each sole-occupancy unit is separated from the
next and from public corridors by construction with the required FRL. The basement car park is a
separate fire compartment from the residential levels.

## 4. Fire services and equipment

The building is sprinkler protected throughout. The sprinkler system extends through the basement car
park.

- Fire hydrants to AS 2419.1, with a booster assembly at the Calloway Street frontage
- Fire hose reels in the basement car park
- Smoke detection and alarm system to AS 3786 in each apartment, and AS 1670.1 detection in common
  areas
- Portable fire extinguishers to AS 2444
- Emergency lighting and exit signs

## 5. Egress

Two fire-isolated stairs serve every storey. Travel distances on the residential levels and in the
basement comply, subject to the stair locations shown on the DA drawings.

## 6. Occupant and hazard profile

There are no vulnerable occupants needing assisted evacuation. No special fire hazards or dangerous
goods are present. No performance solution is proposed for the facade or structure; the external
walls are non-combustible masonry.

## 7. Design and Building Practitioners Act

The building is a Class 2 building under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020; regulated
designs and design compliance declarations are required.

## 8. Access for people with a disability

An accessible path of travel is available from the street to the lobby and the lifts, and from the
accessible car spaces in the basement. Adaptable apartments are to be shown at construction
certificate stage.

## 9. Conclusion

The DA design can comply with the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of NCC 2022 Volume One, subject to the
matters noted above being resolved in the design for the construction certificate.
