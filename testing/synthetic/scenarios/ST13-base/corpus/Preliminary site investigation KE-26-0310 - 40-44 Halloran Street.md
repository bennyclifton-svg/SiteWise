## Introduction

Halcyon Eastwood Pty Ltd proposes to redevelop the site for a mixed-use development with ground-floor
retail and apartments above. Because the proposal changes the use of part of the land from commercial
to residential, the consent authority must consider whether the land is suitable for that use. This
preliminary site investigation (PSI) was commissioned to inform that consideration. It follows the
NSW EPA Consultants Reporting on Contaminated Land guidelines and the National Environment Protection
(Assessment of Site Contamination) Measure.

## Site history

We reviewed a historical title search, aerial photographs from 1943 to 2024 and council's property
records.

- In the 1943 and 1951 photographs the site is a single dwelling with open yards.
- The shops on Nos. 42 and 44 and the present dwelling on No. 40 appear by 1965 and are unchanged in
  form in every later photograph.
- Council and title records show the shops occupied over that time by retail shops, a newsagent, a
  bakery and a hairdresser.

No service station, dry cleaner or other potentially contaminating activity was identified on the site
or on the adjoining land. Council holds no record of underground storage tanks, and no fill points,
vent pipes or bowsers were seen on site.

## Site inspection

Hannah Wu inspected the site on 2 March 2026. The shops were trading and the dwelling occupied. There
was no staining, odours or stockpiles, no fill mounds, and no sign of stressed vegetation. The rear
car park is sealed asphalt in fair condition.

## Limited sampling

Six shallow test pits were dug through the rear car park and yard to a maximum depth of 1.2 m. Soil
samples were analysed for heavy metals, hydrocarbons, BTEX, PAHs and OCPs, and fill samples were
screened for asbestos. All results are below the residential criteria (HIL A and HSL A) of the NEPM.
No asbestos was detected in the fill.

The fill encountered is a thin layer of sandy clay with brick fragments, over natural clay.

## Conclusions

The PSI found a low risk of contamination and no further investigation is required.

The land is suitable for the proposed residential use. Spoil from the basement excavation is to be
classified before disposal in line with the NSW EPA Waste Classification Guidelines. The shops and
dwelling date from the 1960s. A hazardous building materials survey should be carried out before
demolition; it is outside the scope of this PSI.
