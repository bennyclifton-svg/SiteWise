# Fee proposal: civil and stormwater engineering

Dear Marcus,

Thank you for the opportunity to provide a fee for the civil and stormwater engineering on Halloran
Street. Our understanding of the work is set out below.

## Background

The site drains towards Halloran Street. On-site detention is required under council's stormwater
policy, and we expect the OSD tank to sit under the basement ramp off Wattle Lane, discharging to the
kerb in Halloran Street. There is no existing on-site detention to retain or modify.

Stormwater quality treatment (WSUD) to council's targets, modelled in MUSIC. We expect proprietary
cartridge filters in the OSD tank with a gross pollutant pit upstream.

The basement will need a pump-out system for the ramp and subsoil drainage, sized to council's
requirements.

The site is not in a drinking water catchment, so no neutral or beneficial effect assessment applies.

The civil scope includes no public road works; the only work outside the boundary is the new vehicle
crossing in Wattle Lane and making good the footpath, under council's approval.
