## Site and context

The site is in Zone E1 Local Centre under Ryde Local Environmental Plan 2014, in the Eastwood town
centre. It has a frontage to Halloran Street, the main shopping street, and to Wattle Lane at the
rear. The neighbours on both sides are built to the common boundaries with nil setbacks: a two-storey
shop at No. 38 and a three-storey commercial building at No. 46. The town centre has shops, cafes and
offices at ground level with apartments above.

## The proposal

The development application seeks consent to demolish the existing building on each lot and to
construct a six-storey mixed-use building, being shop-top housing: three ground-floor shops with 42
apartments on the five levels above. The proposal includes:

- two basement levels with 55 car spaces, entered from Wattle Lane;
- three retail tenancies with a total GLA of about 600 m2 fronting Halloran Street;
- 42 apartments: one, two and three bedrooms;
- communal open space on the roof;
- an awning along the Halloran Street frontage.

## Planning controls

**Housing SEPP and the ADG.** State Environmental Planning Policy (Housing) 2021, Chapter 4, applies.
The design verification statement and the ADG compliance table accompany the application. The
proposal meets the ADG criteria for solar access, cross ventilation, apartment sizes and communal open
space.

**Ryde LEP 2014.** The proposal complies with the height and floor space ratio standards; no clause
4.6 variation request is required.

**SEPP (Resilience and Hazards) 2021.** Chapter 4 applies because the proposal introduces residential
use. The PSI found a low risk of contamination and the land is suitable for the proposed residential
use.

## Design review

Council's Urban Design Review Panel saw the scheme at pre-lodgement. Its comments on the street wall
height, the ground-floor shopfronts and the roof communal space have been taken up in the scheme now
submitted.

## Traffic and parking

Parking complies with the Ryde DCP rates for the apartments, visitors and retail. The development
does not generate heavy vehicle movements; waste is collected on site by council's standard collection
vehicle. A loading bay for small delivery vans sits next to the bin rooms off Wattle Lane.

The site fronts a local road; no classified road frontage. The development is not traffic-generating
development and will not be referred to TfNSW. No intersection modelling or SIDRA analysis is
required. A green travel plan is not required.

## Noise

Traffic noise from Halloran Street sets the facade glazing and ventilation in the acoustic report.
Plant on the roof is screened and located to meet the noise criteria at the nearest apartments.

## Trees

No trees on or next to the site, including the two street trees in Halloran Street, are affected by
the works.

## Notification

Council will notify neighbours and place the DA on public exhibition under its community participation
plan; the developer is not running any separate community consultation.

## Subdivision

Strata subdivision of the apartments and the three retail lots will be sought in a separate
application after completion.

## Determination

The application will be determined by the Local Planning Panel.

## Conclusion

The proposal is permitted with consent, complies with the principal development standards, and
suits the town centre. We recommend approval subject to appropriate conditions.
