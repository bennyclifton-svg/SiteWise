Thank you for asking us to prepare and manage a development application for the six-storey
shop-top housing development at 40-44 Halloran Street. The site is zoned E1 Local Centre under Ryde
Local Environmental Plan 2014, and the use is permitted with consent.

## Scope of services

**Pre-lodgement meeting and planning advice.** A planning controls review against the LEP, the Ryde
DCP, the Housing SEPP and the Apartment Design Guide; advice to the design team on height, floor space
ratio, setbacks and parking; and preparation for and attendance at council's pre-lodgement meeting.

**Statement of environmental effects and ADG compliance table.** A full statement of environmental
effects addressing section 4.15 of the Act, with the ADG compliance table prepared with the
architect, and coordination of the consultant reports that council requires.

**DA lodgement and assessment liaison.** We will lodge the development application on the NSW Planning
Portal for Halcyon Eastwood, pay the application fee on your behalf (reimbursed at cost), and manage
council's requests for information and any amended plans through the assessment.

**Local Planning Panel attendance.** The application will be determined by the Local Planning Panel.
We will review council's assessment report and draft conditions, prepare a written response if needed,
and attend the panel meeting with you.
