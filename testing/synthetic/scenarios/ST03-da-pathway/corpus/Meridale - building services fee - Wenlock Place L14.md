# Fee proposal: building services engineering

Dear Daniel,

Thank you for the brief. We are pleased to offer our fee for the building services engineering for
the Avenor fit-out of Level 14.

## Scope

We will design the mechanical, electrical, hydraulic (kitchenette only) and fire services for the
tenancy, working from the base-building systems and the landlord's fit-out guide. Our work covers
the alterations to the VAV air-conditioning and exhaust, the tenant power and lighting, the
kitchenette water and waste, and the alterations to the sprinklers, detection and EWIS. We will
confirm the spare base-building capacity with the landlord's engineers before concept design.

Our scope runs through construction-phase inspections, witness testing and completion certification.
