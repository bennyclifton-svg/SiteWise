## Our understanding

Avenor Capital Partners is fitting out the whole of Level 14 of Wenlock Place as its head office,
with Corrigan Hale Interiors as design lead. Council will need an access report with the
development application, the private certifier will need it again with the construction certificate,
and the certifier will need an access statement at completion. The
sanitary facilities and lifts on the core are base building and outside our review.

## Scope

We will review the fit-out design against the access provisions of the NCC and AS 1428.1, and the
Disability (Access to Premises - Buildings) Standards. Our review covers:

- the accessible path of travel through the tenancy from the shared lifts and the lift lobby
- door circulation spaces and door hardware
- the reception counter and glazing manifestation
- hearing augmentation in the boardroom
- signage
