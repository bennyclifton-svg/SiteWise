# Statement of environmental effects

Prepared by: Clara Ellery, Ellery Town Planning Pty Ltd
Project: Avenor head office fit-out, Level 14, 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney NSW 2060
Applicant: Avenor Capital Partners Pty Ltd
Land: Lot 12 DP 1184520

## The site

The site is Wenlock Place, an existing 24-storey office tower at 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney.
The application relates to Level 14 only, a whole floor of 1,200 m2 NLA. All other floors are
occupied by office tenants and are not affected by the proposal.

The land is zoned E2 Commercial Centre under North Sydney Local Environmental Plan 2013.

## The proposal

This statement accompanies the development application to North Sydney Council. A development
application will be lodged with council for the fit-out of Level 14 as the head office of Avenor
Capital Partners. The proposal comprises:

- new partitions, glazed meeting-room fronts, ceilings, finishes and joinery
- a reception area, boardroom, meeting rooms, offices, open-plan work points and a kitchenette
- alterations to the tenancy's air-conditioning, lighting, power and fire services to suit the
  new layout

The floor remains in office use. There is no change of use, no increase in floor space and no
external works. The building's exterior, common areas and plant are not altered. The application
is accompanied by the DA drawings by Corrigan Hale Interiors, a BCA assessment report and an access
report.

## Planning controls

Office premises are commercial premises, which are permitted with consent in the E2 zone. The
proposal is consistent with the zone objectives, as it maintains employment floor space in the
North Sydney centre.

The fit-out does not alter the building's height or floor space ratio, so the LEP development
standards are not affected. No clause 4.6 variation is sought.

The relevant provisions of North Sydney Development Control Plan 2013 deal mainly with the
building's form and street frontages, which the proposal does not change.

The certifier has advised that the works do not meet all of the standards of the Commercial and
Industrial Alterations Code, so they fall outside the complying development standards. A
development application is therefore made.

## Assessment

The proposal is contained within an existing floor of the building. It has no environmental impact
beyond the building: it does not change the building's appearance or the amenity of neighbouring
properties. Construction will follow the landlord's building rules, with noisy works and
deliveries outside business hours and deliveries through the building's loading dock.

Council will notify the application in accordance with its participation plan. Given the internal
nature of the works, we do not expect submissions.

The application will be determined by council staff under delegation and is not referred to the
local planning panel.

## Conclusion

The proposal is permissible with consent, is consistent with the zone objectives and the
applicable controls, and has no adverse environmental impact. We recommend that council grant
consent.
