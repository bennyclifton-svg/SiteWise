## Our understanding

Avenor Capital Partners is fitting out the whole of Level 14 of Wenlock Place, an existing office
tower, as its head office. The certifier has advised that the works do not meet the complying
development standards, so a development application is to be lodged with North Sydney Council. The
works are internal to the tenancy, with no change of use and no external works.

You have asked us to prepare the statement of environmental effects, lodge the application on the
NSW Planning Portal and manage it through to determination. Corrigan Hale Interiors will provide the
DA drawings, and the BCA and access consultants will provide their reports for lodgement.

## Scope and fee

| Item | Description | Fee (ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review of planning controls and pre-lodgement advice | $2,800 |
| 2 | Statement of environmental effects and lodgement | $7,200 |
| 3 | Responses to council requests | $2,000 |
| | **Total** | **$12,000** |

Total fee $12,000 excluding GST.

Item 1 covers a review of North Sydney Local Environmental Plan 2013 and North Sydney Development
Control Plan 2013 as they apply to the fit-out, and a short advice note on the lodgement package.
Item 2, Statement of environmental effects and lodgement of the development application, includes
preparing the lodgement package with the owner's consent from the landlord and the cost estimate
the Portal requires. Item 3 covers responses to council's requests for further information and a
review of the draft conditions before determination.

## Exclusions

- council DA fees and any development contributions
- NSW Planning Portal fees
- reports by other consultants
- applications to modify the consent after determination

## Terms

Fees exclude GST. We invoice on completion of each item, payable within 14 days. This offer is open
for 60 days.
