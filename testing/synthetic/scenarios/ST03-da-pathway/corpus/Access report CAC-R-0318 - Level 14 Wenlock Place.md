# Access report

Prepared by: Sunil Rao, Clearpath Access Consultants Pty Ltd
Project: Avenor head office fit-out, Level 14, 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney
Client: Avenor Capital Partners Pty Ltd

## Purpose

This report reviews the concept layout of the Level 14 tenancy by Corrigan Hale
Interiors against the access provisions of NCC 2022, AS 1428.1 and the Disability (Access to
Premises - Buildings) Standards. This report supports the development application and the
construction certificate.

## Review

**Path of travel.** An accessible path of travel runs from the lift lobby through reception to the
work areas, meeting rooms, boardroom and kitchenette. Corridor widths allow wheelchair passing at the
turning points shown on the plan.

**Doors.** Door circulation spaces are provided on both sides of the doors on the accessible path,
with lever handles at the required height and luminance contrast between the door leaf and the
frame.

**Glazing.** The glazed meeting-room fronts carry a contrasting manifestation band across the full
width of the glass.

**Reception counter.** The reception counter includes a lowered section with knee clearance for a
seated visitor.

**Hearing augmentation.** A hearing augmentation system is provided in the boardroom, with signage
at the entry.

**Signage.** Room signage includes raised tactile characters and Braille where required.

## Base building

Accessible sanitary facilities are base building on the core and outside the works. The shared lifts
are base building.

## Conclusion

Subject to the items above being documented, the layout is capable of complying with the access
provisions. We will inspect the completed works and issue an access statement.
