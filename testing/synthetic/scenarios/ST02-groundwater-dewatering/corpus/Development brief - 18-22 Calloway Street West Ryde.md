# Development brief: residential flat building, 18-22 Calloway Street, West Ryde

Prepared by Daniel Okafor, Development Manager, Tallis Grove Developments Pty Ltd, for the design team.

## The project

A new six-storey residential flat building of 64 apartments is proposed on our land in West Ryde,
a short walk from the local shops and services. This is a new building on an
amalgamated site: we have bought the three adjoining lots and will develop them together as one
parcel.

64 apartments: a mix of one-, two- and three-bedroom apartments. Our current target mix is
14 one-bedroom, 38 two-bedroom and 12 three-bedroom apartments, which suits the West Ryde market
and the Apartment Design Guide mix. We are open to the architect's advice on small changes to the mix
if it improves solar access or cross ventilation.

Average apartment size about 80 m2 internal. We want generous living rooms and usable balconies
rather than extra bedrooms squeezed into the same area.

Six storeys above two basement levels. Two basement levels of car parking will hold about 70 cars,
with bicycle parking, storage cages and the plant and bin rooms. One driveway from Calloway Street
is all we want on the frontage.

The amalgamated site area is about 2,400 m2. Target gross floor area about 6,200 m2. Please confirm
both against the survey and the planning controls at the concept stage.

## The site

The three lots sit side by side on the western side of Calloway Street and have been bought over
the last eighteen months. The three existing dwellings were built in 1962. They are rented on
periodic leases that we will end in an orderly way.

The existing dwellings will be demolished; no existing building is retained or altered.

We will have vacant possession of all three lots before demolition; the site is vacant for the whole
of the works.

Tallis Grove Developments Pty Ltd is the sole owner of the three lots and the only decision maker.
Instructions to the design team come from me or from our Managing Director.

## Quality, budget and program

Mid-range quality finishes pitched at owner-occupiers and investors. We want durable, low-maintenance
materials, good acoustic separation between apartments and kitchens and bathrooms that will still
look current in ten years. We do not want premium finishes that the West Ryde market will not pay
for.

Construction budget: $27,600,000 excluding GST. This covers the building works, the basement and the
public domain works on the frontage. It excludes land, consultant fees, authority fees and finance
costs, which we carry separately.

Target: lodge the development application by 31 August 2026. Our financier wants to see a determined
DA before we commit to the construction contract, so the design program should work back from that
date.

## Approvals and procurement

A development application will be lodged with the City of Ryde. We expect it to be assessed under the
Housing SEPP and the Ryde planning controls and determined by the local planning panel.

Design and construct under AS 4902, with the architect and engineering consultants novated to the
contractor after DA. We will appoint the architect, structural engineer and civil engineer now and
take the design to a D&C tender package. The successful contractor then takes over their
appointments and completes the design.

The builder will be engaged under AS 4902 General conditions of contract for design and construct,
with our own amendments to be prepared by our solicitors.

The building is a Class 2 building under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020; regulated
designs and design compliance declarations are required. Each consultant should say who prepares
the regulated designs after novation.

No staged construction: the works are delivered in one stage. We will take one occupation
certificate for the whole building.

Strata subdivision on completion, with the apartments sold off the plan.

## Services and sustainability

All-electric building: induction cooktops and heat-pump hot water; no natural gas supply. We want
the car park wired so that each space can take an EV charger later.

No Green Star, NABERS or net zero target beyond BASIX. Meet the BASIX targets well and keep running
costs low for owners.

No Connecting with Country input is required for this site.

There are not multiple buildings: a single residential flat building is proposed, with one lobby and
one core.
