## 1. Introduction

Thank you for the opportunity to propose our services for your new home. This proposal follows
your brief of 3 February 2026 and our meeting on site. We have allowed for a two-storey, four-bedroom
house of about 300 m2 with a double garage, designed for a construction budget of $1,450,000
excluding GST.

The architect's services run from concept design through to lodgement of the application for a
complying development certificate with the certifier on the NSW Planning Portal, then construction
documentation and tender.
Contract administration during construction is offered as an option.

This is a Class 1a house, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act registered-design obligations
do not apply.

## 2. Scope by stage

### Stage 1: Concept design

Site analysis, review of planning controls, two concept options, a preferred scheme with plans,
elevations and a 3D massing view, and an outline materials palette. Two design meetings.

### Stage 2: CDC documentation and lodgement with the certifier

CDC drawings, a Housing Code compliance table, coordination of the survey, stormwater concept and
BASIX certificate, preparation of the application on the NSW Planning Portal, and responses to the
certifier's requests for information during assessment.

### Stage 3: Construction documentation

Construction drawings, door and window schedules, finishes schedule, a written specification, and
coordination with the structural and civil engineers to suit the certifier's conditions.

### Stage 4: Tender

Tender documents, a tender list of three or four residential builders agreed with you, answers to
tenderer questions, a tender comparison and a recommendation.
