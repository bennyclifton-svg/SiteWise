# Architectural design statement

Harbourline Architects Pty Ltd
Project: New house for Dan and Mai Nguyen, 27 Corella Rise, Denistone NSW 2114

## 1. Site analysis

The lot is a rectangular 650.2 m2 site with a 16.5 m frontage to Corella Rise and a gentle fall to
the rear. It is vacant and grassed. The rear garden faces north-east, which gives the main living
spaces good winter sun. Neighbouring houses sit back behind front gardens.
The lot is a standard suburban lot with no environmental sensitivities such as bushland, waterways or
contamination. No demolition is required.

## 2. Built form

The house is a simple two-storey form set behind a front garden, with the upper level stepped in
from the side boundaries to reduce bulk. The double garage projects slightly forward of the entry,
under a lower roof that ties into the main roof. The kitchen, family and dining area
opens to a covered terrace and the rear garden.

Building classification: Class 1a dwelling with a Class 10a attached garage.

## 3. Room schedule

- Two storeys
- Gross floor area 298 m2
- Four bedrooms, the main with ensuite
- Three bathrooms (main, ensuite, upstairs family) and a ground-floor powder room
- Double garage
- Kitchen with walk-in pantry, open family and dining
- Separate front living room
- Study near the entry
- Laundry with external access

The guest bedroom on the ground floor has the main bathroom next to it, so Mai's mother can stay
without using the stairs.

## 4. Materials

- Ground floor: face brick veneer in a pale blend
- First floor: lightweight fibre cement cladding with a painted finish
- Roof: standing-seam metal roof in a light colour at a low pitch
- Windows: aluminium frames with double glazing to the living areas and bedrooms
- Front fence: low masonry piers with timber infill

## 5. BASIX and energy

The house is all-electric, with an induction cooktop, a heat-pump hot water system and ducted
air-conditioning. A rainwater tank serves the toilets, laundry and garden. Eaves and external shading
to the north and west windows reduce summer heat. The NatHERS assessment and BASIX certificate are
being prepared by the energy assessor for the application. Climate zone 5.

## 6. Planning compliance

As complying development, no clause 4.6 variation is sought or possible.
The house is within the 8.5 m Housing Code height limit and the gross floor area is well
below the Housing Code maximum for a lot of this size.

The front, side and rear setbacks, landscaped area and private open space meet the Housing Code
development standards. The design complies with the Housing Code so the certifier can approve it in about 20 days.

As complying development, the application is not referred to the local planning panel.
No public exhibition or community consultation; the certifier gives the neighbour notice before work.

## 7. Landscape and trees

The front garden is planted with low shrubs and groundcovers. Raised garden beds along the side
boundary give the family a place for vegetables and herbs. The rear garden is mostly lawn, with the
covered terrace and a paved path to the clothes line.

No trees on or next to the site are affected by the works; a large blue gum near the rear boundary is
retained outside the works. No neighbouring or street trees are affected by the works.

## 8. Summary

The proposal is a modest, well-planned family house that suits its street, meets the owners' brief
and complies with the main planning controls.
