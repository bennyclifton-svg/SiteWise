# Owner's brief: new house at 27 Corella Rise

## About us

We are Dan and Mai Nguyen. We have two children in primary school and Mai's mother stays with us for
part of each year. We bought the land at Corella Rise last year because it is close to the children's
school, the station and Mai's work at Macquarie Park. This brief sets out what we want so that our
architect can start on a concept design.

We are the sole owners and the only decision makers. Either of us can give
instructions, and we will answer questions within a few days.

## The site

Site: 27 Corella Rise, Denistone NSW 2114

Site area about 650 m2. The lot is vacant and clear, with a wide frontage to the street. We have
ordered a detail survey and will pass it on when it arrives.

## The house

A new two-storey detached family house. It will be a new build on a vacant lot.

What we need:

- Two storeys
- Target gross floor area about 300 m2
- 4 bedrooms: the main bedroom upstairs with an ensuite and walk-in robe, two children's bedrooms
  upstairs, and a guest bedroom downstairs for Mai's mother
- 3 bathrooms plus a powder room
- An open kitchen, family and dining area facing the back garden, with a walk-in pantry
- A separate living room at the front that can be closed off
- A study near the entry
- A laundry with direct access to the drying area outside
- Double garage (2 spaces) under the main roof, with internal access to the house
- Good storage throughout

No basement; slab on ground at natural level. Built in a single stage. One dwelling only; no
secondary dwelling.

We like a calm, modern house with plenty of daylight, good cross-ventilation and a strong link
between the kitchen and the garden. We do not want a house that looks like every other display home.

## Quality and budget

We want a high-quality, architect-designed home with durable, high-end finishes. We would rather have
fewer, better rooms than a bigger house with cheap finishes.

Construction budget: $1,450,000 excluding GST.

This figure is for the builder's contract only. We have set aside a separate amount for consultant
fees, council fees and furniture.

## How we want to build

We want an architect to design the house and see it through approvals and tender. We have been told
a house like ours can meet the Housing Code, so we would like it approved by a registered certifier.

Approval: Complying development certificate under the Housing Code.

Architect-led traditional lump-sum tender to three or four residential builders. We would like the
architect to recommend builders who do this kind of work well, and to help us compare the prices.

We expect to sign the HIA NSW Lump Sum Contract with the builder.

We do not need any Connecting with Country input for this lot.

## Services

- All-electric home: induction cooktop and heat-pump hot water
- A rainwater tank for garden watering and the toilets
- Ducted air-conditioning with zones for upstairs and downstairs
- Data points in the study and the living areas
- An electric vehicle charger in the garage

No sustainability rating target beyond BASIX. We do want the house to be comfortable without running
the air-conditioning all year, so orientation and shading matter to us.

## Next steps

Please come back to us with a fee proposal for the design, approvals and tender stages, and a list of
the other consultants you think we will need.

Dan and Mai Nguyen
