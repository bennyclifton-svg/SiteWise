## 1. Introduction

This report presents the results of a geotechnical investigation for a proposed new two-storey house
at the above site. The work was done in accordance with our proposal RG-P-0388. Its purpose is to
classify the site under AS 2870 and give advice for footing and slab design.

We understand the house will be timber framed with brick veneer to the ground floor, on a slab on
ground. No basement is proposed; the house is a slab on ground.

## 2. Fieldwork

Fieldwork was carried out on 31 March 2026. Three boreholes (BH1 to BH3) were drilled with a
track-mounted rig to 3.0 m at the locations shown on Figure 1. Dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) tests
were done next to each borehole to 1.5 m. Undisturbed tube samples were taken for laboratory testing.
The boreholes were logged by an engineer and backfilled with spoil.

No groundwater was encountered in the boreholes to 3.0 m.

## 3. Subsurface profile

The profile was consistent across the three boreholes:

| Depth (m) | Material |
|---|---|
| 0.0 to 0.2-0.3 | Topsoil: silty clay, dark brown, with roots |
| 0.2-0.3 to 1.8-2.1 | Silty clay: stiff to very stiff, orange-brown and grey, medium plasticity |
| 1.8-2.1 to 3.0 | Shale: extremely weathered, very low strength, grey |

DCP results show stiff clay from about 0.3 m, with refusal on shale in BH2 at 2.4 m.

## 4. Laboratory testing

Three shrink-swell tests were carried out on the clay from BH1 to BH3.

| Sample | Depth (m) | Shrink-swell index Iss (%) |
|---|---|---|
| BH1 | 0.6 to 0.8 | 2.1 |
| BH2 | 0.5 to 0.7 | 2.4 |
| BH3 | 0.7 to 0.9 | 1.9 |

Based on these results, a characteristic surface movement of 30 mm to 35 mm was estimated for the
site.

## 5. Site classification

Site classification: Class M in accordance with AS 2870.

This classification assumes the site is maintained as set out in AS 2870 Appendix B and CSIRO
Building Technology File 18. We understand the frame will be H2 treated pine with termite
management to AS 3660.1; termite protection is outside the scope of this report.

## 6. Footing and slab recommendations

- A stiffened raft slab designed to AS 2870 for a Class M site is suitable.
- Edge and internal beams should be founded in the natural stiff silty clay below the topsoil, at
  least 300 mm into natural clay. An allowable bearing pressure of 100 kPa may be used.
- Any isolated pad footings for posts should be founded in natural clay at similar depth.
- A footing inspection by a geotechnical engineer is recommended before concrete is placed.

## 7. Earthworks

- Strip the building platform and excavate to a depth of 300 mm to remove topsoil under the slab
  platform. Stockpile topsoil for reuse in landscaping.
- Proof roll the exposed clay and remove any soft areas.
- Place any fill in layers no thicker than 200 mm, compacted to at least 98% standard dry density.
- No excavation is proposed within the zone of influence of neighbouring footings.

## 8. Site drainage

Keep the ground around the house graded away from the slab for at least 1 m. Connect downpipes to
the stormwater system as soon as the roof is on. Do not let water pond next to the slab during or
after construction.

## 9. Limitations

This report is based on three boreholes. Conditions between and beyond the boreholes may differ. We
should be contacted if conditions differ from those described during construction.
