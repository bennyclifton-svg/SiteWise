## Background

The project is a new two-storey house of about 300 m2 with an attached double garage. The site is in NCC climate zone 5. The house will need a BASIX certificate for the
complying development certificate, with the thermal comfort section met by a NatHERS assessment.

## Scope

1. NatHERS thermal assessment of the CDC plans using accredited software, with advice on glazing,
   insulation and shading if the first run does not meet the BASIX thermal comfort targets.
2. Stamped NatHERS plans and the NatHERS certificate.
3. BASIX certificate covering water, energy and thermal comfort, prepared from the information the
   owners and architect give us on fixtures, hot water, cooling and heating, and the rainwater tank.
4. One revision of the assessment and certificate to match the construction documentation.
