Following up on your question at the site meeting about when building could start. The honest answer
is that it depends mostly on how long the certifier takes to issue the complying development
certificate, and then on the tender. Because we are designing to the Housing Code, a certifier can
usually issue a house CDC within a few weeks of lodgement, and a tender with three or four
builders plus a contract usually takes another six to eight weeks after the construction drawings
are done.
