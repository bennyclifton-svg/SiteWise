# Site plan

Saltbush Building Design Pty Ltd
Project: Secondary dwelling, 18 Tuckeroo Crescent, Gymea NSW 2227
Client: Graham and Lena Whitfield
Scale: 1:200 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Lot boundaries, dimensions and north point
- Main house outline and the new secondary dwelling footprint
- Setbacks to the boundaries and to the main house
- Private open space and courtyard
- Entry path from the street along the driveway
- Sydney Water sewer main along the rear boundary, with its zone of influence hatched
- Piers along the flat's rear edge, founded below the zone of influence
- Route of the house drain to the sewer main
- New stormwater line from the flat to the existing system
- Sub-main trench from the main house to the flat
- Clothes line beside the eastern fence

## Site data

- Site area 600.0 m2
- Secondary dwelling GFA 60 m2
- Rear setback 3.0 m; western side setback 0.9 m
- About 7 m between the flat and the main house
