# Detail and boundary survey report

Client: Graham and Lena Whitfield
Property: 18 Tuckeroo Crescent, Gymea NSW 2227 (Lot 32 DP 237114)
Purpose: detail, level and boundary survey for the design of a secondary dwelling

## Site description

The survey was carried out on 17 February 2026. Levels are on the Australian Height Datum (AHD),
derived from a nearby permanent mark. Boundaries were re-established from the deposited plan and
existing survey marks.

Site area by survey: 600.0 m2. The lot is rectangular, with a 15.24 m frontage to Tuckeroo Crescent
and a depth of 39.37 m.

The backyard is generally level, with a fall of about 200 mm across the yard towards the rear eastern
corner. Lawn covers most of the rear yard.

Construction access is constrained: the only way to the backyard is the side driveway past the occupied
house, through a double gate. A 3 m driveway along the eastern side of the house gives truck access to
the rear yard through a double gate; it is the only way in, as there is no rear lane, and it is also the
family's parking.

## Boundaries and easements

No easements are shown on the title or found on site. Timber paling fences stand on or near the side
and rear boundaries, within 100 mm of the true boundary at every point measured. The front boundary
has a low brick fence about 150 mm inside the boundary.

## Services

The sewer diagram shows a Sydney Water main running along the rear boundary. It is a 150 mm main about
1.0 m inside the rear boundary and about 2.0 m deep, with a maintenance hole near the south-western
corner of the lot. The cover level of the maintenance hole was picked up and is shown on the plan.

The house drains by gravity to the Sydney Water sewer main along the rear boundary; the house drain
runs down the western side of the yard. The house drain inspection opening was located about 1.5 m behind the rear wall of the house, and the gully beside the
laundry door. Both are shown on the plan with invert levels.

Water main in Tuckeroo Crescent; the house is connected to it. The meter is at the front boundary,
beside the driveway.

The house has an overhead electricity supply from the street pole to a point of attachment on the front
wall. There is no gas connection to the house.

There is no existing on-site detention on the lot; roof water drains by gravity to the kerb.

## Limitations

Underground services were located from visible fittings only. Their routes should be confirmed by a
locator before any digging. This report is to be read with survey plan BHS-26-0119 sheets 1 and 2.
