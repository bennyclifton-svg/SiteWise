## Purpose

Saltbush Building Design has asked us to propose for a site classification of the footprint of the new
granny flat in the rear yard. The classification to AS 2870 is needed by the structural engineer to
design the slab and footings, and by the certifier for the complying development certificate.

## Scope

1. Investigation plan and fieldwork. We will drill two boreholes within the flat's footprint, with
   dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) tests beside each. We expect to use a hand auger; if the ground is
   too stiff, a small track-mounted rig will be brought down the side driveway. The owners are to
   arrange access past the house on the day and to have the double gate open.
2. Laboratory reactivity testing. Shrink-swell tests on samples of the clay from both boreholes.
3. Report. A geotechnical report with the borehole logs, the AS 2870 site classification and
   recommendations for the footings and slab.

We will locate underground services before drilling, using the survey plan and a dial-before-you-dig
enquiry.
