## Background

We understand the development will have one basement level with bulk excavation to about 3.8 m below
the street. The investigation needs to give the structural engineer the parameters for the retention
system and footings, and to describe the subsurface profile for the DA and the D&C tenderers.

## Scope

- Dial Before You Dig search and service location by a specialist locator.
- Four cored boreholes to 9 m depth, or refusal, using a track-mounted rig: two through the rear car park of Nos. 42 and 44 and two in the rear yard of No. 40 after access is
  arranged with the occupants.
- Logging of soil and rock, standard penetration testing in soil, and rock coring to classify the
  shale.
- Laboratory testing: moisture content, Atterberg limits, point load strength index and aggressivity.
- A geotechnical report with the subsurface profile, excavation and retention parameters, footing
  design parameters and construction recommendations.
