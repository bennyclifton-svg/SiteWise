## Background

We understand the project to be a three-storey reinforced concrete building of ground-floor retail and
18 apartments over one basement level of car parking. We expect post-tensioned slabs on a regular
column grid to the residential levels, with conventionally reinforced basement slabs and walls.

A transfer slab at level 1 picks up the residential columns over the retail and car-park grid, so the
apartment layouts are not bound by the parking bays below.

Bulk excavation to the eastern boundary adjoining the two-storey shop at No. 38 needs piled retention
with temporary anchors. Anchors under neighbouring land will need the owners' consent, which the
contractor will obtain. Retention to the other boundaries will follow the geotechnical report.

## Scope

**Concept and DA structural advice.** Structural scheme, column grid and slab depths to set floor to
floor heights, and a structural statement for the DA.

**Design development.** Design of the retention system and the transfer slab, preliminary sizing of
all members, and coordination with the architect and services engineers.

**D&C tender documentation.** Structural drawings and a performance specification for the D&C tender,
and responses to tenderer queries.

Our designs will be prepared as regulated designs under the Design and Building Practitioners Act
2020 where the Act requires it.
