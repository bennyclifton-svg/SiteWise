## Site description

The site is three adjoining lots on the northern side of Halloran Street. Site area by survey:
1,802.4 m2 (three lots). The site has a frontage of 40.2 m to Halloran Street and the same to Wattle
Lane at the rear.

The ground falls about 2.3 m from Wattle Lane down to the Halloran Street boundary, fairly evenly
across all three lots. The rear of Nos. 42 and 44 is a sealed car park; the rear of No. 40 is a
grassed yard.

## Existing structures

There is an existing building on each lot: single-storey shops on Nos. 42 and 44 and a dwelling on
No. 40.

## Boundaries and easements

Boundaries were redefined from the deposited plan and the occupation of the neighbours. There are no
easements, rights of carriageway or restrictions on the title of any of the three lots.

The parapet flashing of No. 38 overhangs the common boundary by up to 40 mm. This is a minor
encroachment and is shown on sheet 2.

## Trees

Two council plane trees stand on the Halloran Street footpath in front of Nos. 42 and 44. Their
positions, trunk diameters and canopy spreads are shown on sheet 1. There are no trees within the
site.

## Services

Services were located from Dial Before You Dig plans and visible surface features. They have not been
proven by potholing.

- Sewer: Each lot drains to the Sydney Water sewer main in the rear lane.
- Water: Each lot is connected to mains water from Halloran Street. Meters are at the front boundary
  of each lot.
- Stormwater: roof water from each lot discharges to the kerb in Halloran Street. There is no existing
  on-site detention on any of the lots.
- Electricity: overhead supply from the pole in Wattle Lane to each lot.
- Telecommunications: an NBN pit on the Halloran Street footpath at No. 42.
- Gas: gas meters on each shop, supplied from Halloran Street.

No Sydney Water assets are within the site.

## Limitations

This survey shows the site as it stood on the date of fieldwork, 17 February 2026. Underground
services should be located on site before any digging starts.
