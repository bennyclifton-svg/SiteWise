## Project understanding

The project is a three-storey shop-top housing building of 18 apartments over three ground-floor shops,
with one basement level of car parking, on a consolidated site of about 1,800 m2 in the Eastwood town centre.
The retail is three shell tenancies. The apartments must meet the Apartment Design Guide, and the
scheme will go through council's urban design review before the application is determined.

The facade is face brick and fibre cement cladding with aluminium windows; there is no curtain wall or
unitised facade system.

## Scope of services

**Concept design and urban design review.** Site analysis, massing options, a preferred concept, and
presentation to council's urban design review meeting, with one round of changes from its comments.

**DA documentation.** DA drawings, shadow diagrams, a photomontage, ADG compliance tables and the
design verification statement required for residential apartment development. Our DA documentation
is issued to the town planner, who will lodge the development application. We will respond to
council's requests for information on architectural matters.

**Design development.** Coordinated plans, sections, elevations and key details with the structural,
civil and services engineers, a finishes schedule and an updated area schedule.

**D&C tender documentation.** A design and construct tender set: drawings, an outline specification
and a performance brief for the contractor's design, and responses to tenderer queries.

After the D&C tender our appointment will be novated to the successful design and construct
contractor.
