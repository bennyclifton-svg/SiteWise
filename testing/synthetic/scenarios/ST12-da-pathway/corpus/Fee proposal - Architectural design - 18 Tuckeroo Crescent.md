## Introduction

Thank you for asking us to propose for the design of the granny flat in your backyard. We are
accredited building designers, not architects, and most of our work is new homes, secondary dwellings
and small additions in the Sutherland Shire. This proposal sets out our scope and fees by stage, based on
your brief of 2 February 2026 and the survey by Brindle & Hart.

This is a Class 1a dwelling, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act registered-design obligations
do not apply. Our drawings will still be prepared to the standard a builder needs to price and build
from.

## Scope of services

### Stage 1: Concept design

- Site analysis on the survey: setbacks, orientation, the house drain and services
- Placing the flat clear of the house drain and inside the setbacks
- Concept floor plan, elevations and a site plan
- One round of changes after we meet to review the concept

### Stage 2: DA documentation and lodgement with council, including the statement of environmental effects

- DA drawings: site plan, floor plan, elevations, sections and a roof plan
- Site plan showing setbacks and the services routes from the main house
- Shadow diagrams
- The statement of environmental effects
- BASIX commitments marked on the drawings, coordinated with your BASIX assessor
- Coordination of the structural, stormwater and geotechnical input
- Responses to council's requests for information during assessment

The designer will lodge the development application with council on the NSW Planning Portal.

### Stage 3: Construction certificate drawings and specification for quotes

- Construction certificate drawings developed from the approved DA set and the consent conditions
- Finishes and fixtures schedule, chosen from builders' standard ranges
- A short specification so that three builders quote on the same basis
