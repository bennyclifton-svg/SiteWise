# Owner's brief: secondary dwelling at 18 Tuckeroo Crescent

## Project description

The project comprises a new detached secondary dwelling (granny flat) in the backyard of our family home
at Tuckeroo Crescent. We have lived here for nineteen years and the backyard has far more lawn than we
use.

It will be a single-storey flat with 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom, with a European laundry in the bathroom.
We are thinking of about 60 m2 of floor area, which is what our designer tells us fits comfortably on
the lot.

The flat is for our daughter, who is starting work locally and wants a place of her own close to
home. In a few years it may be rented out, so it should work as a self-contained home for someone who
is not family.

Please make this clear to the builders: the owners will continue to live in the main house throughout
the works, so we need the side gate shut at night, the driveway clear by evening and the noise kept to
normal hours.

We are the sole owners and the only decision makers. Either of us can give instructions, and we will
reply to questions within a day or two.

We want the flat about 1.5 m from the rear fence, to keep a large lawn between the house and the flat.
Our designer has told us the flat sits closer to the rear boundary than the complying development
standards allow; the flat exceeds the CDC rear setback standard, so we will lodge a DA with council.

## What we want

- An open kitchen and living area that opens to a small private courtyard
- Built-in robes in both bedrooms
- Split-system air-conditioning in the living area and the main bedroom
- A path from the street to the flat's front door, so a visitor does not have to come through our house
- Somewhere out of sight for the bins and a clothes line

The flat needs no garage or car space. Our daughter will park on the street.

## Scope, budget and quality

Standard project-home finishes from the builder's range: durable and easy to keep, nothing custom.
We are happy to choose from a builder's standard colour and fixture selections.

Construction budget: $210,000 excluding GST.

That figure is for the builder only. Design, consultant and certifier fees are budgeted separately.

## Contract and procurement

We will engage our own designer and consultants, then take lump sum quotes from three licensed builders.
We would like our designer to help us compare the quotes.

The builder will be engaged under the HIA NSW Residential Building Contract for New Dwellings for a
fixed lump sum price.

No staged construction: one builder, one contract, one continuous job. We are not planning multiple
buildings: just the one secondary dwelling.

## Services and sustainability

There is no gas supply to the property, and the flat will be all-electric. We would like an induction
cooktop and heat-pump hot water.

No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target beyond BASIX. We do want the flat to be comfortable in
summer without the air-conditioning running all day, so window shading matters to us.

We do not need any Connecting with Country input for this lot.

No basement or cellar.
