# Project brief: Avenor head office fit-out, Level 14 Wenlock Place

Prepared by Daniel Okafor, Project Director, Larchmont Project Services Pty Ltd, for the tenant's
design team and the landlord's representative.

## Project

The project is the fit-out of an existing commercial office floor for the tenant's head office.
Avenor Capital Partners is consolidating its Sydney staff from two leased floors into one floor at
Wenlock Place under a new ten-year lease.

This is a tenant fit-out of an existing office floor; there is no building work outside the tenancy.

Tenancy: whole of Level 14, 1,200 m2 NLA.

Avenor will be the single tenancy on the floor.

Site: Level 14, 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney NSW 2060

Client: Avenor Capital Partners Pty Ltd

Level 14 still holds the previous tenant's fit-out. It will be stripped out back to base building:
the landlord has agreed that the incoming tenant demolishes the existing offices, partitions and
finishes as part of its works. Behind them are the base-building suspended ceiling grid and
lighting, VAV air-conditioning, sprinklers, smoke detection and EWIS, and a tenant distribution
board, which stay. The amenities on the core are base building and are maintained by the landlord.

## Accommodation

The design team should check the following against the floor plate and the core:

- open-plan work points for about 140 staff, with a mix of fixed desks and shared settings
- six enclosed offices for the executive team
- four meeting rooms of six to ten people, and two small focus rooms
- a boardroom for 18 people, divisible for staff presentations
- a reception and waiting area at the lift lobby, with a branded feature wall
- a kitchenette and breakout space with a sink, dishwasher and boiling water unit
- a comms room for the tenant's network equipment
- a print area, lockers and general storage

Final numbers will come from the workplace survey in March.

## Budget and quality

Fit-out construction budget: $2,400,000 excluding GST. The figure covers the fit-out contract only.
Consultant fees, landlord charges, relocation and the tenant's own purchases sit in a separate
project budget held by Avenor.

The fit-out contract excludes tenant-supplied items. Tenant-supplied items: loose furniture and
workstations, AV equipment, IT active equipment.

A high-quality A-grade head-office fit-out with premium finishes and custom joinery. The reception,
boardroom and breakout space are client-facing and should carry the Avenor brand; the work areas
should be calm, well lit and acoustically comfortable.

## Approvals and procurement

The fit-out is complying development; the certifier will issue a CDC. As complying development there
is no clause 4.6 variation and no planning panel. The landlord's concept and detailed design
approvals run alongside the CDC.

Traditional lump-sum tender on fully documented design: the interior designer and the services
engineers document the works, and three to five fit-out contractors from the landlord's approved list
tender a lump sum. The interior designer leads the design team.

The services engineers' scope runs through construction-phase inspections, witness testing and
completion certification.

The fit-out contractor will be engaged under AS 4000-1997 with the tenant's fit-out amendments.

There will be no staged construction: the whole floor is handed to the contractor at once.

## Building and site

All other floors of the building remain occupied by tenants throughout the works. Noisy works and
deliveries are restricted to after hours and weekends under the landlord's building rules. Tenderers
must allow for this in their price and program.

Environmental sensitivity is standard: an internal fit-out in a North Sydney office tower with no
ecological or waterway constraints.

For the avoidance of doubt:

- No community consultation is required for an internal fit-out.
- No Connecting with Country input is required for an internal fit-out.
- The works are in one building; there are not multiple buildings.
- The works involve no basement work of any kind.
- There is no excavation, so no adjoining or neighbouring property is affected.
- No transfer structures and no structural alterations are involved.
- The works are inside the building; no trees are affected.
- There are no works within the public road reserve; deliveries use the building's loading dock.
- No on-site detention is required and no existing OSD is modified.

## Lease

The agreement for lease sets out the landlord's and the tenant's works. At lease expiry the tenant
must make good the premises to base-building condition. The design team should prefer partitions,
joinery and services that can be removed cleanly at that time.

The tenant is not targeting a Green Star Interiors or NABERS tenancy rating.
