# Demolition plan - Level 14

Corrigan Hale Interiors Pty Ltd
Project: Avenor head office fit-out, Level 14 Wenlock Place, 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney NSW 2060
Client: Avenor Capital Partners Pty Ltd
Scale: 1:100 at A1
Issue: For landlord concept approval

## Sheet shows

- Previous tenant's partitions and doors to be removed, shown dashed
- Previous tenant's plasterboard ceilings and bulkheads to be removed, shown dashed
- Previous tenant's joinery and carpet to be removed
- Base-building ceiling grid, lights and diffusers to be retained and protected
- Previous tenant's power, data and services to be disconnected and made safe
- Base-building core, risers and lifts shown for information

## General notes

1. Do not scale from this drawing. Use written dimensions.
2. Services to be made safe by licensed trades before removal starts. Fire and BMS isolations by the
   landlord's nominated contractors, restored every night.
3. Waste out through the goods lift after hours, booked with building management.
4. Read with the general arrangement plan and the services drawings.
