# Base building information and tenant fit-out guide

This guide is issued to incoming tenants and their design teams under the agreement for lease. It
describes the base building, the condition of the floor at handover, and the rules every
fit-out contractor works to. Fit-out enquiries go to the building management office at
fitouts.wenlockplace@example.com.

## The building

Wenlock Place is a 24-storey A-grade office tower at 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney. The existing
building was constructed in 2008. The office levels are a Class 5 building. The building is
sprinklered throughout.

The tower was approved under DA 2004/0617 and its occupation certificate was issued in 2008. The
building holds a 5.5 star NABERS Energy base building rating.

Typical office floor plate: 1,200 m2. Each floor has a central core with six passenger lifts, a
goods lift serving every level, risers on the north and south faces of the core, and the base-building
amenities.

## Level 14 handover

Level 14 still contains the previous tenant's fit-out. The landlord has agreed that the incoming
tenant strips it out back to base building as part of its fit-out works, keeping the following
base-building elements in place and protected:

- suspended ceiling grid with base-building tiles and lighting
- VAV air-conditioning with perimeter and interior zones and base-building diffusers
- sprinklers to the base-building layout
- smoke detection and EWIS speakers to the base-building layout
- a tenant distribution board in the north riser, metered by the landlord

The previous tenant's partitions, ceilings, joinery, floor finishes and tenant services above the
base-building elements are the incoming tenant's to remove.

The amenities on the core are base building and remain the landlord's responsibility.

## Services

There is no gas supply to the office floors.

Tenant water and waste points are provided in the core on each level for a kitchenette.

The base-building air-conditioning has spare capacity for typical office densities. After-hours
air-conditioning is available on request through the building management system and is charged at
the published rate.

Alterations to the fire services and the building management system must be carried out by the
landlord's nominated fire and BMS contractors, engaged by the tenant's fit-out contractor.

No Sydney Water assets are within the tenancy.

## Building rules

Levels 2 to 13 and 15 to 24 are occupied by other tenants and stay open throughout the fit-out.

Noisy works, hot works and deliveries are permitted only after hours (6 pm to 7 am) and on weekends;
the goods lift and loading dock must be booked.

Bookings are made through the building management office at least 48 hours ahead. Hot works need a
permit issued by building management for each shift. Any fire or BMS isolation must be booked and
must be restored every night before the contractor leaves site. Contractors sign in at the loading
dock and attend the building induction before starting work.

## Approvals

The landlord's engineers review the tenant's concept design and detailed design before any work
starts, and the landlord's approval is required for each submission. The fit-out contractor must be
approved by the landlord before it is engaged. Certification and statutory approvals for the fit-out
are the tenant's responsibility, and copies must be given to building management before work
starts on the floor.
