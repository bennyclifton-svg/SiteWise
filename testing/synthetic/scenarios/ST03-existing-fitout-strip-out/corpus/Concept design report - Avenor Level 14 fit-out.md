# Concept design report

Prepared by: Corrigan Hale Interiors Pty Ltd
Project: Avenor head office fit-out, Level 14, 211 Wenlock Terrace, North Sydney
Client: Avenor Capital Partners Pty Ltd

## Tenancy

The tenancy is the whole of Level 14, 1,200 m2 NLA. The previous tenant's fit-out will be stripped
out back to base building: remove the existing partitions, plasterboard ceilings and bulkheads,
kitchenette joinery and carpet, and make safe the old tenant services. The base-building bathrooms on the core are not in scope.

## Layout

The test fit was refined with the Avenor leadership team in three workshops. The concept layout
places client-facing spaces at the lift lobby and work areas on the north and east faces, where
the daylight is best:

- open-plan work points for 140 staff in four neighbourhoods
- six enclosed offices on the south face
- four meeting rooms and two focus rooms
- a boardroom for 18 with an operable wall
- reception and waiting at the lift lobby
- a kitchenette and breakout space next to the boardroom for client events
- a comms room on the south side of the floor
- a print area, lockers and general storage

## Interiors

- plasterboard partitions, with acoustic partitions to the offices, meeting rooms and boardroom
- glazed meeting-room fronts with manifestation
- the base-building ceiling grid altered to suit the partitions, with the removed tiles and lights
  stored for the landlord
- plasterboard bulkheads at reception and the breakout space
- solid-core veneered doors with stainless steel hardware
- reception desk, kitchenette and storage joinery in veneer and stone
- carpet tiles to the work areas and vinyl to the kitchenette and print area
- painting throughout
- acoustic wall panels in the boardroom and meeting rooms
- signage and the Avenor brand wall at reception

## Services

- relocate sprinkler heads and add heads to suit the new rooms; relocate and add detectors and EWIS
  speakers; extend emergency lighting and exit signs
- re-zone the VAV air-conditioning to the enclosed rooms, with new flexible duct and diffusers;
  exhaust to the kitchenette and print room; new BMS points
- kitchenette water and waste connected to the tenant points in the core
- a tenant sub-board from the base-building distribution board; power to the work points from power
  poles
- data cabling to every work point, comms room fit-out and access control on the entry doors
- AV conduits and backboxes to the boardroom and meeting rooms

## Quality

A high-quality A-grade head-office fit-out with premium finishes. The custom joinery and the glazed
meeting-room fronts are long-lead items and will need early orders by the contractor.
