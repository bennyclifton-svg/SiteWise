# Statement of environmental effects: facade remediation

Ridgeway Planning Pty Ltd
Prepared by: Ben Hartigan, Director
Site: Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114 (SP 87654)

## Introduction

This statement accompanies a development application for the change to the external materials and
appearance of Tideway Apartments. It describes the site and the proposed works, assesses them against
the planning controls and considers their likely impacts. It should be read with the architectural
drawings and photomontages by Brightline Architects.

## Site and proposal

The site is in Zone R4 High Density Residential under Ryde Local Environmental Plan 2014. It contains an
8-storey residential flat building of 56 apartments, with frontages to Tideway Crescent and Bennelong
Lane.

The works replace the combustible cladding on the balcony spandrels, balcony blades and upper setback
walls with a ventilated rainscreen of solid aluminium panels, in a new colour scheme of warm grey and
bronze. The works respond to a fire safety order given by council on 19 January 2026.

## Planning controls

| Control | Requirement | Proposal | Complies |
|---|---|---|---|
| LEP zone objectives | Residential character and amenity | Residential use unchanged | Yes |
| Height of buildings | No change sought | Existing height retained | Yes |
| Floor space ratio | No change sought | No additional floor area | Yes |
| DCP facade articulation | Articulated, high-quality facades | Panel layout follows the existing balcony rhythm | Yes |
| DCP materials and colours | Durable, low-maintenance materials | Solid aluminium panels, two colours | Yes |

There is no change to height or floor space ratio. No clause 4.6 variation request is needed.

## Heritage

The site is not a heritage item and is not within a heritage conservation area.

## Impacts

**Appearance.** The new panels keep the building's horizontal banding and give it a calmer, more
consistent colour scheme. The change improves the building's presentation to both streets.

**Construction impacts.** The building stays occupied during the works. A construction management plan
will set working hours, noise limits, resident access to the entrances and car park, and the handling
of removed materials.

**Footpath hoarding.** The scaffold and an overhead hoarding will stand over the council footpath on
both frontages for the duration of the works, with pedestrian passage maintained beneath. Separate
council approval will be sought for the hoarding.

## Excluded works

Repainting the rendered podium walls in the existing colour is exempt development and is not part of
this application.

## Determination

The application will be determined by council staff under delegation, not by a planning panel. No
community consultation is proposed beyond council's notification.

## Conclusion

The works remove a fire risk, are consistent with the zone objectives and the DCP, and have acceptable
impacts. We recommend that council approves the application.
