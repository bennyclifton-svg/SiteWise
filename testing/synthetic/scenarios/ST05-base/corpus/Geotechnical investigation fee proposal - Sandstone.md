## Purpose

You have asked for a site classification to AS 2870 for the footprint of the proposed rear addition,
and for the depth and type of the existing footings at the rear of the house, so the structural
engineer can design footings that suit both.

## Scope

1. Review of the survey, the concept plan and published geological mapping.
2. Fieldwork: two hand-auger boreholes within the addition footprint, and one test pit against an
   existing footing at the rear wall to record its depth, width and founding material.
3. Laboratory shrink-swell testing of clay samples to assess reactivity.
4. A report with the subsurface profile, an AS 2870 site classification and footing recommendations.
