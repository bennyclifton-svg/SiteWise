# Owner's brief: alterations and additions at 41 Tallowwood Avenue

## Project description

The project comprises alterations and additions to our family house at 41 Tallowwood Avenue. We
have lived here for nine years and want to stay in the street, so we would rather improve the house
than move.

The works are alterations and additions to an existing building (a single-storey brick dwelling).
The house was built in 1958 and still has most of its original layout, with a skillion and a laundry
lean-to added at the back in the 1980s.

What we have in mind is a single-storey rear addition (new kitchen, living and dining) plus
alterations to the existing house. The house stays single storey: a single-storey rear addition, with
no change to the front of the house as seen from the street.

We will move out of the house for the duration of the main works. The owners will vacate the house for the duration of the works.
We have family nearby and expect to rent for about six months, so the builder will have the whole
house to work in.

We are the sole owners and the only decision makers. Either of us can give instructions, and we will
answer questions within a few days.

## What we want

- A new kitchen, living and dining area across the back of the house, opening onto the garden, with
  plenty of morning light and a covered outdoor area
- A walk-in pantry off the kitchen
- The existing bathroom and laundry redone, with the laundry closer to the back door
- Two internal walls taken out between the hallway, the old kitchen and the back room, so the house
  opens up from the front door through to the garden
- The front rooms kept much as they are, with the floors sanded and the walls painted
- Better storage in the hall and the new living area

We want the new part to sit comfortably with the old house: face brick to match where possible, and
a roof that does not tower over the neighbours.

## Scope, budget and quality

Mid-range finishes: good quality and durable, nothing high end. We would rather spend on good
windows, insulation and a well-built kitchen than on expensive tiles and fittings.

Construction budget: $640,000 excluding GST.

This is for the builder's contract only. Consultant fees, council fees, rent while we are out and
furniture are budgeted separately.

No basement or cellar. There are not multiple buildings: just the one house, with no granny flat or
studio in the garden.

## Contract and procurement

We want an architect to design the work and take it through approvals and tender. A development
application will be lodged with council.

Architect-led traditional lump-sum tender to three or four licensed builders. We would like the
architect to suggest builders who are used to alterations work on older brick houses.

The builder will be engaged under the HIA NSW Lump Sum building contract.

No staged construction: one builder, one contract, one continuous job. We would like the builder to
have the house for the whole period rather than us moving back in part-way through.

## Services and sustainability

- The house has natural gas from the street main, and we want to keep a gas cooktop
- Heat-pump hot water in place of the old gas storage unit
- Ducted or split air-conditioning to the new living area
- More power points and a data point in the kitchen and living area
- Better lighting throughout

No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target beyond BASIX. We do want the new living area to stay
comfortable in summer, so shading and cross-ventilation matter to us.

We do not need any Connecting with Country input for this lot.
