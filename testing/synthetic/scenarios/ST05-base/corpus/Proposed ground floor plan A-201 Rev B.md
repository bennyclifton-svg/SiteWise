# Proposed ground floor plan

Kestrel Hill Architects Pty Ltd
Project: Alterations and additions, 41 Tallowwood Avenue, Concord NSW 2137
Client: Tomas and Leah Ferreira
Scale: 1:100 at A3

## Sheet shows

- Existing walls to be retained, existing walls to be demolished (dashed), and new walls
- Demolition outline: rear skillion, laundry lean-to and two internal walls
- New rear opening and the removed internal walls on steel beams (refer to structural)
- New kitchen, living and dining, with the bathroom rebuilt
- Laundry moved next to the back door
- Paved terrace and rear deck, with the steps down to the garden moved to the eastern end
- Side setbacks to the addition and the side path

## Area schedule

- Existing house 140 m2
- Addition 55 m2
- Total after the works 195 m2
