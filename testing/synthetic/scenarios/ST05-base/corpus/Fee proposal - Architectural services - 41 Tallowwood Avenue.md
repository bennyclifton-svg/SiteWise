## 1. Introduction

Thank you for asking us to propose our services for the alterations and additions to your house.
This proposal follows your brief of 9 February 2026 and our visit to the house. We have allowed for
a rear kitchen, living and dining addition, the bathroom and laundry redone, and two internal walls
removed.

The architect's services run from concept design through to DA lodgement on the NSW Planning Portal,
then construction documentation and tender. Contract administration during construction is offered
as an option.

This is a Class 1a house, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act registered-design obligations
do not apply.

## 2. Scope by stage

### Stage 1: Measure-up and concept design

Measure-up and drawings of the existing house, a review of the planning controls, two concept
options, a preferred scheme with plans, elevations and a 3D view, and an outline materials palette.
Two design meetings.

### Stage 2: DA documentation and lodgement

DA drawings, shadow diagrams and the statement of environmental effects, coordination of the survey,
stormwater concept and BASIX certificate, preparation of the application on the NSW Planning Portal,
and responses to council requests for information during assessment.

### Stage 3: Construction documentation

One set of construction drawings for the construction certificate and tender, door and window
schedules, a finishes schedule and a written specification, coordinated with the structural and
civil engineers.

### Stage 4: Tender

Tender documents, a tender list of three or four builders agreed with you, replies to tenderer
questions, a tender comparison and a recommendation.
