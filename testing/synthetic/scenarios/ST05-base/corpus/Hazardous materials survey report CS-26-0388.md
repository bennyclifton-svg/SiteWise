## Introduction

Survey of the areas of the existing building affected by the proposed alterations and additions,
carried out on 8 April 2026 in line with the SafeWork NSW code of practice on how to manage and
control asbestos in the workplace. The house was built in 1958 and was re-roofed in 1996.

Areas surveyed:

- Rear skillion and laundry lean-to (to be demolished)
- Bathroom and laundry (to be stripped out)
- Kitchen
- The two internal walls to be removed
- Roof space above the rear rooms
- Subfloor, accessed through the side access hatch
- Eaves at the rear and the sides

The front rooms were inspected visually only, as no work is proposed there.

Samples were analysed by a NATA-accredited laboratory by polarised light microscopy with dispersion
staining.

## Findings

Samples 1-6 of the eaves and wet-area linings: no asbestos detected; the fibre-cement eaves date from the 1996 re-roof.
The bathroom and laundry linings are a later replacement sheet of the same type.

No asbestos-containing materials were detected in any sample.

A lead paint test of the painted window frames in the kitchen and the skillion returned results
below the threshold for lead-containing paint.

The roof space has glasswool batts in good condition. We searched the NSW loose-fill asbestos
insulation register for the address. The register search returned no listing for the property.

No other hazardous materials were found in the areas surveyed.

## Recommendations

1. Include this report and its register in the tender documents so that builders can price the
   demolition and strip-out on the same basis.
2. The builder should have an unexpected-finds procedure. If suspect material is uncovered during the
   works, for example inside wall cavities or under floor coverings not accessible at the time of the
   survey, stop work in that area and have it sampled before continuing.
3. Keep this register on site during the works.

Limitations: the survey covered accessible areas only. Inaccessible areas such as sealed cavities
were not opened.
