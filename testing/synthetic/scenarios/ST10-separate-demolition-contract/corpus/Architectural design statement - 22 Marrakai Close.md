## Project description

The project is a new family house for Hamish and Rosa Delaney, on the site of their existing home.

Building classification: Class 1a dwelling with an attached Class 10a garage.

The new house is two storeys. Gross floor area 302 m2.

- Four bedrooms, the main with ensuite and walk-in robe; three bedrooms upstairs and a guest bedroom
  on the ground floor
- Three bathrooms (main, ensuite, upstairs family) and a ground-floor powder room
- Open kitchen, family and dining facing the back garden, with a walk-in pantry
- Front living room, laundry and drying court
- Double garage under the main roof, with internal access

## Site description and context

The site is a standard suburban lot with no environmental sensitivities such as bushland, waterways
or contamination. It has a 15.85 m frontage to Marrakai Close and falls about 1.1 m from the street
to the rear boundary. The street is made up of detached houses of mixed age.

## Demolition

The existing house, detached garage, shed, slabs, paths and old driveway are demolished. A demolition
hazardous materials survey has been carried out (Clearfield Hazmat Services, report CHS-26-0733). A
licensed asbestos removalist removes the asbestos-containing materials first, and demolition proceeds
only after a clearance certificate is issued.

The existing natural gas service is abolished and the meter removed before demolition. The
electricity supply is disconnected at the same time, and a temporary supply is set up for the builder
once the site is handed over.

Demolition and asbestos removal are by a separately engaged licensed demolisher under its own contract
with the owners, working under the same complying development certificate; the site will be handed to
the builder clear and level.

## Design

The house is set back from the street in line with its neighbours, with the garage set behind the
front wall so that the entry and the front living room face the street. The ground floor opens to the
back garden through wide sliding doors under a deep eave. An internal stair rises from the entry hall
to the bedrooms upstairs.

The ground floor is face brickwork veneer in a pale blend, with lightweight cladding to the upper
floor and a low-pitched metal roof. Windows are aluminium-framed with double glazing to the west.

The new house is all-electric with no gas supply. Hot water is by heat pump and cooking is on an
induction cooktop. A 5,000 L rainwater tank supplies the toilets, laundry and garden.

Climate zone 5. The BASIX commitments are being prepared with the NatHERS assessor, Solace Thermal
Assessments, and are met through insulation, glazing and shading rather than a large air-conditioning
plant.

## Trees

The old garage beside the rear trees is demolished from the driveway side, outside their tree
protection zones; the trees are retained and protected with fencing. No trees are affected; all works
are outside tree protection zones. No neighbouring or street trees are affected by the works.

## Planning

The design meets the Housing Code standards, and the demolition and new dwelling will be approved by
a complying development certificate.

As complying development, no clause 4.6 variation is sought or possible.

The registered certifier determines the application; there is no council or local planning panel
determination.

There is no public exhibition or community consultation; the certifier notifies the neighbours of the
application.
