## Purpose and method

We carried out a detail, level and boundary survey of the property on 16 February 2026 at the
request of the owners, for the design of a new house. Boundaries were re-established from the
deposited plan and the survey marks found in Marrakai Close. Levels are on the Australian Height
Datum (AHD), from SSM 41872 in the footpath outside No. 18. The survey is shown on our drawing
BV-26-0144 sheets 1 and 2, issued with this report in PDF and DWG.

## Site description

Site area by survey: 619.8 m2.

The lot has a full-width 15.85 m street frontage with an existing driveway and vehicle crossing, and
unrestricted vehicle access. The lot is rectangular, about 39.1 m deep, and falls about 1.1 m from
the street to the rear boundary, evenly and without steps. The front yard is lawn with garden edging.

## Boundaries and easements

The boundary dimensions agree with the deposited plan within survey tolerance. No easements or
rights of carriageway are noted on the title or the deposited plan.

The old timber paling fences on the side and rear boundaries sit up to 0.2 m off the true
boundaries. They are shown on sheet 1 with offsets to the boundary at each corner.

## Services

The house drains to the Sydney Water sewer main in the street; the junction is near the front
boundary.

Water main in Marrakai Close; the house is connected to it, with the meter at the front boundary.

Electricity is supplied overhead from the street pole outside No. 20 to a point on the front wall.

A gas meter is on the northern side wall of the house, supplied from the street.

An NBN pit sits in the footpath near the north-western corner of the lot.

Roof water discharges through an old stormwater line to a kerb outlet in Marrakai Close. The line was
not traced beyond the pits that could be opened.

No Sydney Water assets are within the property. There is no existing on-site detention system on the
lot.
