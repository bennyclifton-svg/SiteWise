## Introduction

Thank you for asking us to propose for the design of your new home. We have read your brief of
2 February 2026 and walked the site with you on 26 February. This proposal sets out our services,
our fee for each stage and the terms we work on.

The architect's services run from concept design through to lodging the complying development
certificate application on the NSW Planning Portal, and then the tender to three builders. We will
work with the certifier you appoint and coordinate the other consultants' input into the application.

## Scope of services

**Stage 1: Concept design.** Site analysis on the survey of the existing site, two concept options,
one preferred option developed with you, and a preliminary check against the Housing Code standards.

**Stage 2: Design development.** Plans, elevations and sections developed to a fixed layout; selection
of the main materials and finishes; coordination of the structural, civil and BASIX input.

**Stage 3: CDC documentation, demolition plan and lodgement.** The full drawing set for the
complying development certificate, including the demolition plan for the existing house, garage and
shed; coordination of the hazardous materials report; the BASIX certificate and stamped plans from the
assessor; and lodgement with your certifier through the NSW Planning Portal. We will respond to the
certifier's requests for information.

**Stage 4: Tender.** Tender documents and a schedule of finishes, issue to three builders you have
approved, responses to tender queries, a tender comparison and a recommendation. This stage also
includes a separate request for quotation to three licensed demolishers for your own demolition
contract, with the hazardous materials report attached, and a comparison of the quotations.

## Exclusions

- Interior design and loose furniture selection
- Printing beyond electronic issue
