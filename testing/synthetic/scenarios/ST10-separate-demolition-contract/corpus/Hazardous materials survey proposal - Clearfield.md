## Background

The house, detached garage and shed at the site are to be demolished for a new house. Under the work
health and safety regulations an asbestos register is needed before demolition, and the survey
must be intrusive enough to find materials hidden in the structure. We will do the survey before the
owners move out, and will keep the sampling to places that can be patched neatly.

## Scope

- A demolition survey of the house, garage and shed, with destructive sampling of wall and ceiling
  linings, eaves, floor coverings, roof spaces, sub-floor areas and the garage and shed cladding.
- Paint sampling of the window and door frames for lead.
- Inspection of light fittings for PCB capacitors.
- Analysis of up to ten samples by a NATA-accredited laboratory.
- A report with the asbestos register, photographs, sample results, and a removal scope written so it
  can go into the demolition quotation documents and the demolisher's demolition work plan.
