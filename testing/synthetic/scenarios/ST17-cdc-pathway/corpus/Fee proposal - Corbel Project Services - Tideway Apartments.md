## Our understanding

Council's fire safety order requires the owners corporation to remove the PE-core aluminium composite
panels and the EPS render from the building and replace them with a compliant external wall by
17 December 2027. The building stays fully occupied, so the work must be planned around residents'
access, safety and quiet enjoyment. Every major commitment needs a committee or general meeting
resolution, and the program must allow for notice periods, levies and the certifier's assessment of
the CDC application.

## Scope of services

- Liaison with council's fire safety officer on the order, including the three-monthly progress
  reports.
- Procurement of the consultant team and management of their appointments.
- A program to the compliance date, updated monthly.
- Monthly reports to the strata committee, and papers and attendance at general meetings.
- A resident communication plan, notices and a resident contact point during construction.
- Tender management with the quantity surveyor and architect, and a tender recommendation.
- We will act as superintendent under AS 4000-1997 General conditions of contract, with special
  conditions, from contract award to the end of the defects liability period.
