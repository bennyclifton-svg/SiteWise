## Scope

The new cladding will be carried on brackets fixed into the slab edges and columns of the existing
building. Our role is to confirm that the structure can take those fixings, design and certify them,
and inspect them during construction. No excavation, basement or ground works are included, so no
geotechnical input is required.

## Exclusions

- Design of the cladding subframe, which the facade engineer will review and the builder's installer
  will design.
- Repairs to the slab edges, which will be priced by the builder against our provisional
  specification.
