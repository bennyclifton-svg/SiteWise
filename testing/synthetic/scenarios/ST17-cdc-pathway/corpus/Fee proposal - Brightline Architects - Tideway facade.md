## Introduction

Thank you for inviting Brightline Architects to propose for the facade of Tideway Apartments. We have
read the owners' brief, council's fire safety order and the 2025 cladding audit, and walked the
perimeter of the building.

The job is to give the building a new external wall that complies, looks considered and can be built
around 56 households who stay at home throughout. We will lead the design team, work closely with the
facade, fire and structural engineers, and keep the committee's choices clear and few.

## Our team

Mei Lin Chau, registered architect and registered design practitioner, will lead the project and sign
the regulated designs. She will be supported by a project architect and a documenter.

Stage 5 includes the architectural regulated designs and design compliance declarations: the
remediation is building work under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020. The facade and
structural engineers will provide their own regulated designs and declarations, which we will
coordinate.
