# Combustible external wall cladding audit

Kestrel Fire Engineering Pty Ltd
Unit 7, 2 Rhodes Street, West Ryde NSW 2114
Prepared by Tomasz Wrobel, fire safety engineer

Property: Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114
Owner: Strata Plan 87654, managed by Harbourline Strata Management for the owners corporation

## Building

Tideway Apartments is an 8-storey residential flat building of 56 apartments.

- NCC building classification: Class 2
- The building was constructed in 2011.
- The building has a rise in storeys of 8.
- The building is not sprinkler protected.
- The building is wholly residential above the basement car park.
- Balconies have glass balustrades, which are retained.

The audit covers the external walls of the existing building. Areas inside the apartments are outside its
scope.

## Method

We carried out a visual survey of all four elevations from the ground and from the balconies of
selected apartments, with the building manager. We took 12 samples of the external wall materials
where the surface finish suggested a composite or foam-based product, and sent them for laboratory
combustibility testing. Each sample point was sealed on the day.

## Findings

1. Polyethylene-core (PE-core) aluminium composite panels (ACP) are fixed to the balcony spandrels and
   blades on all four elevations.
2. An expanded polystyrene (EPS) render system forms the level 7 and 8 setback walls.
3. A combustible foil sarking is installed behind the ACP.
4. The 2020 hazardous materials survey found no asbestos in the facade.
5. No services are fixed to or through the cladding.

## Risk

Our assessment identifies the external wall cladding as a facade fire performance issue
requiring rectification. The PE-core ACP and the EPS render are continuous over several storeys, and
the building has no automatic suppression to limit a fire that spreads up the external walls.

## Recommendation

We recommend full removal of the PE-core ACP, the EPS render system and the combustible sarking, and
their replacement with a compliant external wall. We recommend the owners corporation provides a copy
of this report to council.
