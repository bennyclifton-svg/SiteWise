## Background

Thank you for asking us to propose for the next steps after our preliminary site investigation
(report KE-26-0310, 10 March 2026). The land is potentially contaminated: the site was a service station until 1992 and underground tanks may remain; a dry cleaner operated in the ground-floor shop at No. 42.

Our limited sampling found hydrocarbons above the residential criteria near the former tanks at the
rear of No. 40. Council will need to be satisfied that the land is, or can be made, suitable for
residential use before the development application is determined. This proposal covers the
investigation, the remediation action plan and the validation that will support a site audit
statement.

## Scope

**Detailed site investigation.** A ground-penetrating radar sweep of the former service station area
to locate any remaining tanks and lines; twelve soil bores across the site to natural material, with
the density increased around the former tank pit; soil vapour sampling from four sub-slab points
around the former dry cleaner at No. 42; laboratory analysis for hydrocarbons, BTEX, PAHs, heavy
metals and chlorinated solvents; and a detailed site investigation report to the NSW EPA reporting
guidelines, with a conceptual site model and the extent of impact.

**Remediation action plan.** A remediation action plan setting out the preferred remediation
strategy (likely to be tank removal and excavation of impacted soil, which fits the basement
excavation), the remediation criteria, the sequence of works, contingencies and the validation plan.
It will be prepared for review by the site auditor and provided to council before the development application is determined.

**Remediation validation.** Attendance during tank removal and excavation of impacted soil,
validation sampling of the excavation walls and floor, tracking of the disposal dockets, and a
validation report confirming that the remediation criteria have been met.
