# Development brief: mixed-use building at 40-44 Halloran Street, Eastwood

Prepared by Marcus Lee, Development Manager, Halcyon Eastwood Pty Ltd
marcus.lee@example.com

This brief sets out what we want from the design team for our Halloran Street site. It is the
starting point for the consultant appointments and will be updated as the design develops.

## The project

Halcyon Eastwood Pty Ltd will develop a new six-storey mixed-use building: ground-floor retail with
42 apartments on the five levels above.

This is a new building: each existing building on the site is to be demolished and the new
mixed-use building built in their place.

The site contains an existing building on each lot (two single-storey shops and a dwelling).
The existing shops were built in 1965.

The consolidated site is about 1,800 m2, with a frontage to Halloran Street and a rear lane behind.

Halcyon Eastwood Pty Ltd is the sole owner of all three lots and the only decision maker. Approvals
of design, budget and programme come through me.

The existing tenants will have left before demolition starts, so the site is vacant for the whole of
the works.

## Accommodation

Six storeys: ground-floor retail with five residential levels above, over two basement levels of car
parking.

The ground floor is a one-level retail podium; the five residential levels sit above it.

- 42 apartments (a mix of one, two and three bedrooms), designed to the Apartment Design Guide.
- About 600 m2 of retail GLA in three shell tenancies. The tenancies are delivered as shell and core
  only, with no fit-out; incoming tenants fit out under their own leases.
- Total gross floor area about 5,500 m2.
- 55 car parking spaces in the basement, shared between residents, visitors and retail staff.
- Communal open space on the roof for residents.

We want efficient, saleable apartment layouts and a ground floor that gives the main street an
active, attractive shopfront.

## Site and access

Construction access is constrained: a busy main-street frontage, nil-setback neighbours on both sides
and the rear lane as the only vehicle access. The design team should think about how the builder will
get in and out of the site early, as it will affect the basement ramp and the structure.

There are no ecological or habitat constraints on the site, and no Aboriginal sites are recorded on
it. No Connecting with Country input is required for this site.

## Approvals and procurement

A development application will be submitted to the City of Ryde and determined by the Local Planning
Panel.

The project will be procured as a design and construct contract with novation of the design team. The
builder will be engaged under AS 4902 general conditions of contract for design and construct, as a
lump sum. Consultants appointed now should allow for novation to the successful contractor after the
tender.

The building contains a Class 2 part, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 applies:
regulated designs and design compliance declarations are required. Consultants must be registered
where the Act requires it.

The building will be built in one continuous stage under one contract; there is no staged construction
or staged handover. This is one building; we are not planning multiple buildings.

The apartments will be sold off the plan, and the developer will lodge the strata building bond before
the occupation certificate.

## Budget

Construction budget: $24,500,000 excluding GST

This covers the construction contract only. Consultant fees, authority charges, contributions,
finance and marketing sit in the developer's own budget.

## Services and sustainability

A natural gas supply will serve the retail tenancies only; the apartments are all-electric.

No Green Star, NABERS or net zero target: BASIX for the apartments and Section J for the retail only.
We expect practical, low-maintenance services that a future owners corporation can run cheaply.
