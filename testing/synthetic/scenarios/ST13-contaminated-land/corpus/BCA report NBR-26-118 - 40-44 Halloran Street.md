## Purpose

This report assesses the DA design against the Deemed-to-Satisfy Provisions of the National
Construction Code 2022, Volume One. It is to support the development application, and to identify
matters the design team must resolve before the construction certificate. It is not a construction
certificate assessment.

## Building description

Building classification: Class 6 (ground-floor retail), Class 2 (levels 1-5 residential), Class 7a
(basement car park).

The building has a rise in storeys of 6. The two basement levels are not counted as storeys in the
rise, as they are below the ground floor and do not have more than 1 m above finished ground.

Effective height: 18.9 m. The effective height is less than 25 m. With a rise in storeys of 6,
Table C2D2 requires Type A construction.

The site is in NCC climate zone 5.

## Fire resistance and separation

The Class 6, Class 7a and Class 2 parts are to be separated by fire-resisting floors and walls in
accordance with Part C3, with the ground-floor slab and the level 1 slab providing the separation
between parts. Fire-rated shafts are required for services passing through the separating floors.

The building is sprinklered throughout. The system is to comply with AS 2118.1, with the car park
designed for its own hazard class.

Fire hydrants are to comply with AS 2419.1, with a booster assembly at the Halloran Street frontage.
Hose reels are required to the Class 6 and Class 7a parts. Smoke detection and an occupant warning
system to AS 1670.1 are required throughout.

## Access and egress

The residential levels are served by two fire-isolated stairs discharging at ground level, one to
Halloran Street and one to Wattle Lane. Each retail tenancy has its own exit direct to Halloran
Street, separate from the residential exits. Travel distances in the car park comply with Part D2.
Access for people with a disability is to comply with Part D4 and AS 1428.1.

## Sound and energy

Sound insulation between apartments, and between the apartments and the retail below, is to comply
with Part F7. The acoustic consultant should confirm the floor and wall systems.

BASIX applies to the apartments. Section J applies to the retail tenancies and common areas outside
the BASIX scope.

## Fire engineering

No vulnerable occupants requiring assisted evacuation are expected in any part of the building.

No special fire hazards or dangerous goods storage are proposed.

The fire safety strategy is deemed-to-satisfy throughout; no performance solutions are proposed for
the facade, cladding or structure.

## Regulatory

The building contains a Class 2 part, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 applies:
regulated designs and design compliance declarations are required.

## Conclusion

Subject to the matters above, the design is capable of complying with the NCC 2022 Deemed-to-Satisfy
Provisions.
