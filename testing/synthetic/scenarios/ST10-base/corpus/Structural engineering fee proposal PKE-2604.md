## Our understanding

The existing house, garage and shed on the lot are to be demolished and a new two-storey house with a
double garage constructed in their place. We understand the house will be a slab on ground with timber wall
and roof framing, face brickwork veneer to the ground floor and lightweight cladding above.

No transfer structures are needed for a two-storey timber-framed house. Loads will come down through
stacked walls and a small number of beams over the larger openings.

## Scope

- Slab and footing design to AS 2870, based on the geotechnical report. The old footings and any fill
  under the existing house are to be removed after demolition, and the design will allow for the
  ground under the old house once it is inspected.
- Review of the framing layout, with design of beams, lintels and any members outside the span
  tables.
- Tie-down and bracing design for the wind classification of the site.
- Structural drawings and a design certificate for the complying development certificate, and
  responses to the certifier's queries.
