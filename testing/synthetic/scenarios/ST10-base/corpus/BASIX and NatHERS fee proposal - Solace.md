## Scope

We will model the new dwelling in accredited NatHERS software from your CDC drawings, recommend
insulation, glazing and shading that meet the thermal comfort targets, and issue the NatHERS
certificate and stamped plans. We will then complete the BASIX certificate for a new dwelling, using
the water, energy and thermal comfort commitments agreed with you and the owners, for lodgement with
the complying development certificate application.

The site is in NCC climate zone 5. We will use the climate file for the site's postcode.

One revision is included for the construction documentation, so that the certificate matches the
drawings the builder prices and builds from.
