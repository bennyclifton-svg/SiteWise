# Hazardous materials survey: balcony build-ups and soffits

Fibrecheck Hazardous Materials Pty Ltd
Kurrajong Apartments, 41 Kurrajong Parade, Hurstville NSW 2220
Prepared by Hana Kowalczyk, Senior Hazardous Materials Consultant

## Purpose and scope

The owners corporation is renewing the waterproofing of all 42 balconies. The survey covers the
balconies of the existing building: the soffits, the tile bedding, the original bituminous membrane
and the mastic sealants at the door sills, outlets and balustrade posts. Its purpose is to identify
which of these materials contain asbestos before they are stripped.

The building was constructed in 1986. The common property asbestos register lists bonded fibre
cement sheet in the balcony soffits.

This survey covers building materials only; it is not a contaminated land assessment.

## Method

We attended the four balconies opened up by Parapet Building Consultants on 24 and 25 March 2026
(Balconies 207, 303, 502 and 605). At each balcony we inspected the soffit of the balcony above, the
exposed build-up and the sealants, and took samples of each suspect material. Twelve samples were
analysed by a NATA-accredited laboratory by polarised light microscopy with dispersion staining.

## Results

| Material | Location | Samples | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fibre cement soffit sheet | Soffits of Balconies 207, 303, 502 and 605 | 4 | Chrysotile asbestos detected |
| Bituminous membrane | Build-up of Balconies 207, 303, 502 and 605 | 4 | No asbestos detected |
| Mastic sealant | Door sills and balustrade post bases | 4 | No asbestos detected |

1. **Soffits.** The bonded fibre cement soffit sheets contain asbestos, as the register records. The
   sheets are in fair condition and are not friable, but several are water-stained around the slab
   edges. They are fixed with the same detail on every level, so we treat the soffits of all 42
   balconies as asbestos-containing.
2. **Membrane.** No asbestos was detected in the original bituminous membrane in any of the four
   samples.
3. **Sealants.** No asbestos was detected in the mastic sealants at the door sills or the post bases.

## Recommendations

1. We recommend asbestos remediation of the soffits: the bonded fibre cement soffit sheets of all 42 balconies are
   removed by a licensed asbestos removalist before or with the strip-out of the balcony above.
2. The removalist prepares an asbestos removal control plan, which we review before removal starts.
3. The removalist gives SafeWork NSW the required notice before removal starts.
4. Each work area is isolated from residents until it is cleared. A licensed assessor or competent
   person inspects each area after removal and issues a clearance certificate before membrane works
   start on that balcony.
5. The asbestos register is updated as the soffits are removed.
6. If any material not sampled here is found on opening up and is suspected to contain asbestos,
   work in that area stops until it is sampled.

## Limitations

The survey is limited to the balcony build-ups, soffits and sealants at the four opened balconies,
and the results are extended to the other balconies on the basis that they share the same
construction. Other parts of the building were not surveyed. Results apply to the samples taken.
