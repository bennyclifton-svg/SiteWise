## The building

Kurrajong Apartments is a 6-storey residential flat building of 42 apartments. The building was
constructed in 1986. It has a reinforced concrete frame and floor slabs, and every apartment has a
tiled balcony with an aluminium-framed glass balustrade. The building has no basement; residents park
on a separate at-grade car park at the rear.

The building is a strata scheme of 42 lots; the owners corporation acts through its strata committee,
with Northgate Strata Management as its strata managing agent. The committee meets monthly, and any
spending above the committee's limit is put to a general meeting of owners.

## The problem

All 42 balconies leak. Owners and residents report water at the balcony door sills, and water dripping
from the balcony slab edges and soffits onto the balconies below. The problem has been reported on every
level and on all elevations, and short-term sealant repairs over the last three years have not stopped
it. The committee has resolved to fix the cause rather than keep patching.

The owners corporation seeks a remedial building consultant to investigate and rectify the leaking
balcony waterproofing of the existing building.

## Scope of services

The consultant investigates and documents the works, which are then tendered to remedial builders for a
lump sum contract that the consultant administers. We expect the services to include:

1. Investigation: visual inspection of all balconies, leak mapping against our leak log, water testing
   and opening-up at a small number of representative balconies, and a review of the records we hold.
2. A defect report setting out the causes, the options for repair, a recommended option and an order of
   cost for each option, written so that the committee can put it to owners.
3. A remedial specification and details suitable for tender, including any regulated design and
   declarations the work needs.
4. Tender: preparing the tender documents, inviting remedial builders, answering queries, assessing the
   tenders and making a recommendation to the committee.
5. Contract administration: acting as superintendent, inspecting the work at its hold points,
   assessing progress claims and variations, and certifying completion.

Please name any other consultant you need, such as a structural engineer, and say whether you will
engage them or the owners corporation should.

## Constraints

The building will remain fully occupied throughout the works. Residents must keep access to their
apartments, and the work on any one balcony should be as short as practical.

The committee approves each step before the next one starts. In particular, the consultant must not
proceed to the specification until the committee has accepted the defect report, and the owners must
approve the contract and the levy to fund it at a general meeting before any contract is signed.
