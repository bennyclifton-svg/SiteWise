## Our understanding

All 42 balconies at Kurrajong Apartments leak at the door sills and slab edges, and sealant repairs
have not stopped them. The owners corporation wants the cause found and fixed under one contract with
a remedial builder, with the building occupied throughout. We have walked the building with your
building manager and looked at six balconies.

## Methodology

We will inspect every balcony, map the leaks against your leak log, and open up four representative
balconies to see the membrane, the falls, the outlets and the balustrade fixings. Our report will set
out the causes, the options and an order of cost for each, with a recommendation the committee can put
to owners. We then specify the recommended option, tender it to remedial builders on a lump sum, and
administer the contract to completion, inspecting each hold point before work is covered up.

Stage 2 includes the regulated design and the design compliance declaration for the waterproofing: the
works are building work under the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020. Joel Marchetti is a
registered design practitioner and will sign the declaration.
