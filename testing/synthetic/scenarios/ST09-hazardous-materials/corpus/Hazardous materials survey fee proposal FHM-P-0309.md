## Background

Thank you for the invitation to propose. We understand the owners corporation is renewing the
waterproofing of all 42 balconies, and that Parapet Building Consultants will open up four
representative balconies in late March. The common property asbestos register lists bonded fibre
cement sheet in the balcony soffits, and the composition of the original bituminous membrane and the
mastic sealants is not known. Before the build-ups are stripped, the owners corporation and the
builder need to know which materials contain asbestos and how they are to be removed.

## Scope

1. **Survey.** Attend the four balconies while they are opened up. Inspect the soffits, the tile
   bedding, the original membrane and the sealants at the door sills, outlets and balustrade posts,
   and take samples of each suspect material. Sampling is co-ordinated with Parapet so that each
   balcony is opened only once.
2. **Laboratory analysis.** Analysis of up to 12 samples by a NATA-accredited laboratory, with
   results reported in our survey report and the asbestos register updated for the balconies.
3. **Asbestos removal control plan.** Review the asbestos removal control plan submitted by the
   builder's licensed removalist, and comment in writing before removal starts.
4. **Clearance.** Visual clearance inspection of each work area after asbestos removal, with a
   clearance certificate issued before membrane works start on that balcony.

## Assumptions and exclusions

- Access to the balconies is arranged by the strata manager and Parapet Building Consultants.
- Opening up and making good are by others.
- Air monitoring during removal is not included and can be quoted once the builder's programme is
  known.
- Our survey is limited to the balcony build-ups, soffits and sealants. Other parts of the building
  are not surveyed.
