# Site and subdivision plan

Fernleigh Architects Pty Ltd
Project: Multi dwelling housing and community title subdivision, 18 Wattlebird Street, Carlingford NSW 2118
Client: Marko and Ana Kovac
Scale: 1:200 at A3
Issue: For DA

## Sheet shows

- Existing lot boundary (Lot 9 DP 236154) and the existing house and garage to be demolished, dashed
- Proposed lot boundaries on the separating wall lines, dividing the site into proposed Lots 1 to 4
  and the association property lot
- Building footprints of Townhouse 1 (Lot 1) to Townhouse 4 (Lot 4) in one attached row, with single
  garages off the common driveway
- Front setback 6.0 m to Townhouse 1; upper floor set back 1.5 m from the northern side boundary
- Common driveway along the southern boundary (association property), with a single vehicle crossing
- OSD tank under the common driveway
- Bin store behind the front fence line, beside the driveway; mailboxes at the driveway entry
- Rainwater tank for each townhouse, in the rear courtyard
- Front fence line amended: low open fence with planting behind
- Private open space and rear courtyards
