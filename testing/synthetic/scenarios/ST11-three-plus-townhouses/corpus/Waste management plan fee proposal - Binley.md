## Introduction

Thank you for asking us to quote. City of Parramatta requires a waste management plan with the
development application for the townhouses. The plan must cover the waste from demolition and
construction as well as the ongoing collection once the townhouses are occupied, and it must show
where the bins are stored and how they reach the kerb.
