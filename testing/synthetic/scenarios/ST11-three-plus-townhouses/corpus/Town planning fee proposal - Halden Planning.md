## Introduction

Thank you for inviting us to submit a proposal. We understand that a single development application
will be made to the City of Parramatta for the demolition of the existing house, multi dwelling
housing of 4 attached townhouses and the community title subdivision of the lot. Our role is to confirm the planning controls
early, prepare the statement of environmental effects and support the application through
assessment.

## Scope and fees

| Stage | Services | Fee (ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Controls review: the Housing SEPP's low-rise housing provisions, LEP minimum lot size and frontage for the multi dwelling housing and the subdivision, and the DCP controls that apply; short advice to the architect before the design is fixed | $2,500 |
| Stage 2 | Statement of environmental effects, including the DCP compliance table and the section 4.15 assessment | $6,500 |
| Stage 3 | Assessment support and responses to council's requests for information | $2,000 |
| | **Total** | **$11,000** |

Total $11,000 excluding GST. All fees are exclusive of GST.

The architect lodges the DA on the Planning Portal; we will provide the statement and supporting
material to the architect for lodgement.

## Exclusions

- Attendance at a planning panel meeting: excluded, as the application is not expected to go to a
  panel
- Appeals to the Land and Environment Court
- Council application fees and contributions
- Reports by other consultants

## Terms

Each stage is invoiced on completion. Any additional work will be quoted and agreed with you
before it starts. Payment is due within 14 days. This offer is valid for 60 days.
