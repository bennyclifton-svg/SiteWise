## Introduction

This report presents the results of a geotechnical investigation for the proposed attached row of
two-storey townhouses at the site. Its purpose is to classify the site under AS 2870 and to give
advice on footings and earthworks. The work was carried out in line with our proposal BRG-P-0655.

## Fieldwork

Fieldwork was carried out on 14 April 2026. Four boreholes (BH1 to BH4) were drilled with a
track-mounted rig in the front and rear yards, within the footprint of the new dwellings and clear of
the house. A dynamic cone penetrometer (DCP) test was carried out next to each borehole. The
boreholes were logged by our engineer, sampled and backfilled.

## Subsurface profile

| Depth (m) | Material |
|---|---|
| 0.0 to 0.2-0.4 | Fill and topsoil: silty clay with some gravel, dark brown |
| 0.2-0.4 to 1.6-2.2 | Silty clay: stiff to very stiff, medium to high plasticity, brown and grey |
| 1.6-2.2 to 3.0 | Weathered shale: extremely to highly weathered, very low to low strength |

All boreholes were ended at 3.0 m in weathered shale. No groundwater was seen during drilling.

## Laboratory testing

Two shrink-swell tests were carried out on undisturbed samples of the silty clay, giving shrink-swell
indices of 2.1% and 2.6%. Using these results and the soil profile, the characteristic surface
movement is estimated at about 30 mm.

## Site classification

Site classification: Class M in accordance with AS 2870.

The fill and the old house footings are not suitable to found on. Once they are removed and the
platform is prepared as set out below, the classification applies to the new slab areas.

## Footing advice

- Waffle or stiffened raft slabs designed to AS 2870 for a Class M site are suitable for all the
  townhouses.
- Provide a thickened footing under each separating wall between the townhouses, founded in the
  stiff silty clay.
- An allowable bearing pressure of 100 kPa may be used for the thickened footings and edge beams in
  the stiff silty clay.
- Groundwater is not expected above the base of the footing excavations, and no dewatering is needed.

## Earthworks

After demolition, remove the old footings, the garage slab, paths and fill from within the new building areas.
Excavate to a depth of 300 mm to remove topsoil and the old garage slab under the new slab platforms,
then proof roll the exposed clay and replace any soft areas with compacted fill.

No basement is proposed; the townhouses are slabs on ground. No excavation is proposed next to the neighbouring dwellings; the slabs sit on a shallow cut and fill platform. Cuts and fills of up to
about 0.4 m are expected across the lot.

Place any fill in layers no thicker than 200 mm, compacted to at least 98% standard maximum dry
density, under the supervision of a geotechnical engineer.

## Site drainage

Keep the clay around the slabs at a stable moisture content. Grade paved and landscaped areas away
from the slab edges, connect all downpipes to the stormwater system before the roof is sheeted, and
keep garden watering away from the slab edges.

## Limitations

The subsurface conditions between and beyond the boreholes may differ from those described. We should
be told if conditions found during construction differ from this report.
