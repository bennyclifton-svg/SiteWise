# Owner's brief: townhouses at 18 Wattlebird Street

## Project description

The project comprises demolition of the existing house and multi dwelling housing comprising 4 two-storey townhouses, with community title subdivision into 4 lots with a common driveway. That gives us 4 dwellings on proposed Lots 1 to 4, with the driveway and bin area held as association property.

The site contains an existing building (a single-storey dwelling and garage), to be demolished. The existing dwelling was built in 1958 and has had very little done to it since. We have owned it for six years and let it out for most of that time. All the townhouses are new builds on the cleared lot.

The house is let until the lease ends on 31 October 2026 and will be empty before demolition starts, so the site will be vacant for the whole of the works.

We are the sole owners and the only decision makers. Either of us can give instructions, and we will answer questions within a few days. We plan to keep one townhouse to live in and sell the others once the community plan is registered.

## What we want

- Two bedrooms in each townhouse, both upstairs
- Open-plan living and dining on the ground floor, opening onto a small private rear courtyard
- A single garage for each townhouse, entered off the common driveway
- Good storage and a separate laundry in each
- A row that looks good from the street and from the driveway, without being monotonous

We understand the townhouses are permissible in the R2 zone under the Housing SEPP's low-rise housing provisions, and we would like the planner to confirm this early. We want each townhouse to suit a couple or a small family, built to last rather than squeezed for the most return.

## Scope, budget and quality

Site area about 700 m2. Target gross floor area about 460 m2 in total.

Construction budget for the 4 townhouses: $1,650,000 excluding GST. That figure is for the building work, including demolition and site works, and does not include consultants' fees, council fees or our own costs.

Mid-range finishes: good quality and durable, nothing high end. We would rather spend on insulation, windows and a solid build than on fancy fittings.

No basement or cellar.

## Planning and contract

A development application will be lodged with council for the townhouses and subdivision. We expect our architect to prepare and lodge it, with a town planner's help on the planning report.

Architect-led traditional lump-sum tender to three or four residential builders. The builder will be engaged under the HIA NSW Lump Sum building contract, and we would like the architect to help us through the tender and the contract signing.

No staged construction: one builder, one contract, all 4 townhouses built together. We are not planning multiple buildings: the townhouses are attached in one row under one roof.

## Services and sustainability

All the townhouses will be all-electric, with no natural gas connection. We want heat-pump hot water and a BASIX rainwater tank for each dwelling.

No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target beyond BASIX. We do not need any Connecting with Country input for this lot.
