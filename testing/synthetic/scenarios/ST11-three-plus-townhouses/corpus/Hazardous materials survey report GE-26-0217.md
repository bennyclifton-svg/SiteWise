## Introduction

Survey of the existing building and garage before demolition, carried out on 7 April 2026. The existing dwelling was built in 1958. It is a brick house with a tiled roof, timber floors on a
subfloor, and fibre-cement eaves. The garage is a separate fibro structure with a metal roof.

The whole house, the roof space, the subfloor, the eaves and the garage were surveyed. Access was
arranged through the owners' managing agent. The survey was carried out under the Work Health and
Safety Regulation 2017 (NSW) and the Code of Practice: How to manage and control asbestos in the
workplace.

## Sampling

| Sample | Location | Material | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Eaves, front and sides | Fibre-cement sheet | Chrysotile asbestos detected |
| S2 | Bathroom walls | Fibre-cement wall lining | Chrysotile and amosite asbestos detected |
| S3 | Laundry walls | Fibre-cement wall lining | Chrysotile asbestos detected |
| S4 | Garage walls | Fibre-cement sheet | Chrysotile asbestos detected |
| S5 | Hallway ceiling | Plasterboard | No asbestos detected |
| S6 | Laundry floor | Vinyl sheet and backing | No asbestos detected |
| S7 | Window frames, all elevations | Paint | Lead 1.8% w/w |
| S8 | Living room walls | Paint | Lead below 0.1% w/w |

Samples S1 to S6 were analysed by polarised light microscopy with dispersion staining at a
NATA-accredited laboratory. Paint samples were analysed for lead content.

## Findings

Asbestos. Bonded asbestos-containing fibre-cement sheeting is present in the eaves, the bathroom and
laundry wall linings, and the garage walls. It is in fair condition, with no significant damage, and
is a low risk while it stays undisturbed. It will be disturbed by demolition and must be removed
first.

Lead paint. Paint on the window frames contains lead above the 0.1% threshold in AS 4361.2. Paint on
the internal walls sampled is below the threshold.

Loose-fill asbestos insulation. We searched the NSW Loose-Fill Asbestos Insulation Register for the
address. The register search returned no listing for the property. No loose-fill insulation was seen
in the roof space, which has glasswool batts over the ceiling.

## Recommendations

1. Remove all bonded asbestos-containing material by a licensed asbestos removalist before
   demolition starts.
2. The removalist is to notify SafeWork NSW at least five days before the removal work starts.
3. After removal, a licensed asbestos assessor is to inspect and issue a clearance certificate
   before demolition proceeds.
4. Manage the lead-painted window frames during demolition in line with AS 4361.2, with dust
   control and disposal to a facility licensed to accept the waste.
5. Include this register and report in the demolition and tender documents so that builders can
   price the removal.
