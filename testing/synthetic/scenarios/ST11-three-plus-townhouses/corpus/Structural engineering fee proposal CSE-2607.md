## Our understanding

We have reviewed the concept drawings for the row of 4 attached two-storey townhouses with attached
single garages. The ground floor is brick on a concrete slab, and the upper floors and roof are framed in
timber. No transfer structures are required; the timber-framed upper floors bear on the ground-floor walls and beams.
