## Purpose

We understand the project is a slab-on-ground warehouse of about 7,500 m2 with a 500 m2 office, a
heavy-duty hardstand and a car park, on a benched estate lot. No basement is proposed, so the
investigation targets footing, slab and pavement design only.

## Scope of investigation

- Eight boreholes drilled to 6 m with a truck-mounted rig across the warehouse footprint and the
  office.
- Twelve test pits across the hardstand and car park, with dynamic cone penetrometer testing.
- Laboratory testing: CBR, shrink-swell index (reactivity), Atterberg limits and soil salinity.
- A geotechnical report with the subsurface profile, site classification for the office slab, footing
  design parameters, subgrade preparation for the industrial slab, and pavement design CBR values.
