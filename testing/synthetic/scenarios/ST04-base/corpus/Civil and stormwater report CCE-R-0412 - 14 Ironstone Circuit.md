# Civil engineering and stormwater management report

Cardinal Civil Engineering Pty Ltd
ABN 36 780 903 496
Prepared for: Redgum Logistics Estates Pty Ltd
Project: Warehouse and office, 14 Ironstone Circuit, Erskine Park NSW 2759
Author: Aisha Karimi, Senior Civil Engineer (aisha.karimi@example.com)

This report describes the civil and stormwater design submitted with the development application. It
should be read with our civil drawings C-001 to C-020 and the architectural site plan.

## Bulk earthworks

The lot is a benched pad. Earthworks are limited to trimming the pad to the design levels for the
warehouse, hardstand and car park, with cut and fill balanced on site. The warehouse floor level is set
at RL 46.20, about 0.3 m above the finished hardstand at the docks, which is graded to fall away from
the dock face.

## Pavements

The hardstand is a heavy-duty jointed concrete pavement designed for B-double axle loads on a subgrade
CBR of 3%, as recommended in the geotechnical report. The car park is an asphalt pavement on a granular
base.

## Stormwater drainage

Roof water is collected by box gutters and downpipes and piped, with the pavement drainage, to the
estate drainage system in the drainage easement along the eastern boundary.

On-site detention is required under council's stormwater policy; a 540 m3 OSD tank is provided under
the car park. The tank limits post-development discharge to the pre-development rates for storms up
to the 1% AEP event.

## Water quality

Stormwater quality targets are demonstrated with a MUSIC model. Treatment comprises a gross pollutant
trap at the inlet to the OSD tank and proprietary cartridge filters in a chamber beside it.

The gross pollutant trap and filters treat hydrocarbon contamination washed from the hardstand. The
model shows reductions in gross pollutants, total suspended solids, phosphorus and nitrogen that meet
council's targets.

## Works outside the lot

No works in the public road other than the two heavy-duty vehicle crossings and making good the
footpath. The crossings are designed to council's heavy-duty vehicle crossing standard.

## Erosion and sediment control

During construction, a temporary sediment basin at the eastern end of the lot, sediment fences and a
stabilised site entry will control runoff, in line with the Blue Book (Managing Urban Stormwater: Soils
and Construction).
