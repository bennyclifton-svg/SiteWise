## Site description

The land comprises one lot. Site area by survey: 16,012 m2

The lot has a frontage of 96 m to Ironstone Circuit and is roughly rectangular, with a depth of about
167 m. It is a benched pad left by the estate's bulk earthworks, with a gentle fall of about 1.4 m from
west to east. Batters at the rear and side boundaries are grassed and stable.

## Structures

There is no existing building or structure on the lot; the pad is bare benched earth. The estate's
boundary fencing (1.8 m chain wire) runs along the side and rear boundaries.

## Boundaries and easements

Boundary marks were found or replaced at all corners. Measured dimensions agree with the deposited plan
within survey tolerance.

A drainage easement 3 m wide runs along the eastern boundary in favour of the estate. No encroachments
onto or from the lot were found.

## Vegetation

There are no trees on the lot, so no tree is affected by the works. Ground cover is grass and
hydromulch on the batters only.
