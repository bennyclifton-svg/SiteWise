## Background

We understand the warehouse to be a single-storey steel portal frame with a 12 m clear height,
enclosed by off-site precast concrete panels to the dock face, the front and the boundaries, with
metal cladding above the panels. The office is a single-storey structure at the front of the
warehouse.

The portal frame and panels are founded on pad footings. The warehouse floor is an industrial slab on
ground designed for racking leg loads and forklift wheel loads, with joints laid out to suit the
tenant's aisle layout.

There are no transfer slabs or transfer beams; the portal frame bears directly on pad footings.

There is no excavation next to any boundary or neighbouring structure beyond footing pads and service
trenches.

## Scope

**Concept and DA structural advice.** Frame spacing and bay layout, preliminary member sizes, panel
heights and joint layout, and a structural statement for the DA.

**Design development.** Design of the portal frame, precast panels and their connections, and the
industrial slab, coordinated with the architect, civil engineer and geotechnical report.

**D&C tender documentation.** Structural drawings and a performance specification for the D&C tender,
and responses to tenderer queries.
