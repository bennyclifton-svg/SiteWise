## Introduction

This report presents the results of a geotechnical investigation for a proposed warehouse of about
7,500 m2 with a 500 m2 office, heavy-duty hardstand and car park. It was carried out in accordance
with our offer SHG-P-0219 of 12 March 2026.

## Fieldwork

Fieldwork was carried out between 30 March and 2 April 2026. It comprised eight boreholes (BH1 to BH8)
drilled to 6 m with a truck-mounted rig across the warehouse and office footprint, and twelve test pits
(TP1 to TP12) across the hardstand and car park. Dynamic cone penetrometer tests were carried out next
to each test pit.

Test pits were excavated to a depth of 3.0 m; no basement is proposed and the building is slab on
ground.

Bore logs for BH1 to BH8 are in Appendix A. Test pit logs are in Appendix B.

## Subsurface conditions

The profile is consistent across the site:

| Unit | Depth (m) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Controlled fill | 0 to 0.5-1.5 | Silty clay fill, placed and compacted by the estate developer under Level 1 supervision |
| Residual clay | to about 3.0 | Stiff to very stiff silty clay, high plasticity |
| Bringelly Shale | from about 3.0 | Extremely weathered, becoming highly weathered and low strength with depth |

The estate developer's Level 1 earthworks certificate was provided and is consistent with the fill
encountered.

No groundwater was encountered in any borehole or test pit during drilling.

## Laboratory testing

Shrink-swell tests on the residual clay gave values consistent with moderate reactivity. Soaked CBR
values on the fill and residual clay ranged from 3% to 6%. Soil salinity testing classified the soils
as moderately saline.

## Recommendations

**Site classification.** The office slab area is classified as site class M (moderately reactive) under
AS 2870, provided the fill remains as certified.

**Footings.** Pad footings for the portal frame and panels may be founded in stiff residual clay with
an allowable bearing pressure of 200 kPa, or in the weathered shale with 600 kPa. Footing excavations
should be inspected by a geotechnical engineer before concrete is placed.

**Industrial slab.** Strip topsoil and soft material, proof roll the subgrade and replace any soft
areas. A design subgrade modulus of 30 kPa/mm may be adopted on the certified fill.

**Pavements.** A design CBR of 3% is recommended for the hardstand and car park pavements.

**Durability.** The soils are moderately saline. Concrete in contact with the ground should be designed
for exposure classification B1 under AS 3600.
