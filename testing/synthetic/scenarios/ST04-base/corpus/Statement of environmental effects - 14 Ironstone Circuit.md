# Statement of environmental effects

Westbourne Planning Pty Ltd
Prepared by Megan Doyle, Director (Registered Planner), megan.doyle@example.com

Project: Warehouse and office, 14 Ironstone Circuit, Erskine Park NSW 2759 (Lot 21 in DP 1289934)

This statement accompanies a development application to Penrith City Council for the construction
and use of a warehouse with an ancillary office, car parking, hardstand and landscaping. It should be
read with the architectural drawings by Marrick Hale Architects and the consultants' reports submitted
with the application.

## Site and context

The land is in Zone E4 General Industrial under Penrith Local Environmental Plan 2010, within the
Erskine Park employment estate. It is a vacant lot of about 1.6 ha, benched by the estate developer,
with a 96 m frontage to Ironstone Circuit. Neighbouring lots hold or are approved for warehouses of a
similar scale.

The estate developer's validation report confirms the lot is suitable for industrial use and is not
contaminated.

## The proposal

Gross floor area: 8,000 m2 (warehouse 7,500 m2, office 500 m2)

- A single-storey warehouse with a clear height of 12 m
- 12 dock doors with levellers, along the southern dock face
- About 6,000 m2 of heavy-duty hardstand
- 40 car spaces at the front of the lot
- A single-storey office facing Ironstone Circuit

The warehouse is enclosed by precast concrete panels with metal cladding above, and the office has an
aluminium-framed frontage.

## Planning controls

The development is a warehouse or distribution centre, permitted with consent in the E4 zone. The
proposal complies with the height and floor space ratio standards; no clause 4.6 variation request is
required. It meets the setback, landscaping and parking controls of the Penrith Development Control
Plan. State Environmental Planning Policy (Resilience and Hazards) 2021 has been considered, and the
site is suitable for the proposed use.

## Traffic and parking

The site is designed for 26 m B-double access to the hardstand and docks. Swept paths for both
crossings are shown in the traffic report by Lanefield Traffic.

Access is from a local industrial road; the site has no classified road frontage.

The development is below the Schedule 3 thresholds, so it will not be referred to TfNSW.

The 40 car spaces meet the DCP rate for the warehouse and office floor areas.

## Stormwater

The s10.7 certificate shows no flood related development controls.

On-site detention and stormwater quality treatment are designed by Cardinal Civil Engineering to
council's stormwater policy and are described in its civil report.

## Lighting

LED floodlights on the building and light poles light the hardstand, aimed away from the street.
Lighting will comply with AS 4282 for the control of obtrusive light.

## Trees and landscape

There are no trees on the lot and no tree is affected by the works. A landscaped setback to Ironstone
Circuit softens the frontage and screens the car park.

## Notification

Council will notify neighbours and place the DA on public exhibition under its community participation
plan; the developer is not running any separate community consultation.

## Determination

The application will be determined by council staff under delegation and is not referred to the
Sydney Western City Planning Panel.

## Conclusion

The development is consistent with the zone objectives and the applicable controls, and its
environmental effects are acceptable. We recommend that consent be granted.
