# Development brief: warehouse and office at 14 Ironstone Circuit, Erskine Park

Prepared by Owen Fitzgerald, Development Manager, Redgum Logistics Estates Pty Ltd
owen.fitzgerald@example.com

This brief tells the design team what we want on our Ironstone Circuit lot. It is the basis for the
consultant appointments and will be updated as the design develops and the tenant's requirements are
confirmed.

## The project

Redgum Logistics Estates Pty Ltd will develop a new industrial warehouse with an ancillary office for a
single logistics tenant.

The brief is for a new single-storey warehouse (Class 7b use) with a single-storey office at the front
of the lot, facing Ironstone Circuit.

This is a new building on a vacant estate lot. There is no existing building on the site.

The lot is vacant and benched by the estate developer, so the site is unoccupied for the whole of the
works.

Redgum Logistics Estates Pty Ltd is the sole owner of the lot and the only decision maker. Design,
budget and program approvals come through me.

## Accommodation

Site area: 16,000 m2 (1.6 ha)

Gross floor area: 8,000 m2 (warehouse 7,500 m2, office 500 m2)

Clear height: 12 m to the underside of the haunch

Dock doors: 12 dock doors, recessed, with levellers and shelters

Heavy-duty concrete hardstand: about 6,000 m2

Office share: about 6% of the gross floor area

Maximum top of storage: 11 m in selective pallet racking

Car parking for 40 cars, located at the front of the lot near the office entry, separated from the
truck circulation.

The office is single-storey at ground level, with open-plan workspace, two meeting rooms, amenities
for warehouse and office staff, and a staff kitchen and lunch room.

## Site

Construction access is unrestricted: a 96 m frontage to a local industrial road, no neighbour built to
a common boundary and no restriction on working hours beyond council's standard hours.

There are no ecological or habitat constraints on the lot, and the AHIMS search found no Aboriginal
sites recorded on it.

The estate developer's validation report confirms the lot is suitable for industrial use and is not
contaminated. A copy will be issued to the design team with the estate's subdivision documents.

No basement is proposed; the building is slab on ground.

The site is designed for 26 m B-double access to the hardstand and docks. Trucks should be able to
enter, turn and leave in a forward direction without using the car park.

No Connecting with Country input is required for this lot.

## Fire and services

The building will be sprinkler protected throughout with ceiling-level ESFR heads.

Power will come from the Endeavour Energy grid through a new kiosk substation; no on-site generation is
planned.

No natural gas supply is needed; the building is all-electric.

No dangerous goods will be stored above minor quantities; the tenant stores general palletised goods
only.

## Approvals and procurement

A development application will be lodged with council. The building does not meet the code's rear setback
standard, so a DA is required.

The DA will be determined by council staff under delegation; it will not go to the Sydney Western City
Planning Panel.

The design complies with the LEP height and floor space ratio standards; no clause 4.6 variation
request is required.

We are not running any community consultation of our own; council notifies the neighbours.

The works will be procured as a design and construct lump sum under AS 4902; the architect, structural
and civil engineers and landscape architect will be novated to the contractor. Consultant appointments
must allow for novation on the terms of our standard deed.

The building has no Class 2, 3 or 9c part, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 does not
apply.

The warehouse will be built in one continuous stage under one contract; there is no staged
construction or staged handover.

This is one building; we are not planning multiple buildings on the lot.

No Green Star or NABERS rating is targeted; NCC Section J compliance only.

## Budget

Construction budget: $14,800,000 excluding GST

The budget covers the warehouse, office, hardstand, car park, landscaping and services connections. It
excludes land, authority contributions, consultant fees and the tenant's own fit-out and racking.
