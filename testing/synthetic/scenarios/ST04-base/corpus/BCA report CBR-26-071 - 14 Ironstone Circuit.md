# BCA assessment report

Clearpath Building Regulations Pty Ltd
ABN 45 465 566 157
Prepared for: Redgum Logistics Estates Pty Ltd
Project: Warehouse and office, 14 Ironstone Circuit, Erskine Park NSW 2759
Author: Sophie Tran, Registered Certifier (sophie.tran@example.com)

This report assesses the architectural DA drawings by Marrick Hale Architects against the
Deemed-to-Satisfy provisions of the National Construction Code 2022, Volume One. It is intended to
accompany the development application and to confirm that the design can comply, with the performance solutions
noted, at construction certificate.

## Building description

Building classification: Class 7b (warehouse) and Class 5 (office)

Rise in storeys: 1

Type of construction required: Type C construction.

The site is in NCC climate zone 6.

## Large isolated building

Floor area 8,000 m2; volume of the single fire compartment about 100,000 m3 (below the C3D4(1) limits
of 18,000 m2 and 108,000 m3)

The building is treated as a large isolated building under C3D4. Perimeter vehicular access at least
6 m wide is provided around the building, within 18 m of it, except across the office frontage,
where it is addressed by a performance solution.

## Fire services

The building will be sprinkler protected throughout with ceiling-level ESFR heads.

Fire hydrants are required to AS 2419.1, with a booster assembly at the main entry, two fire water
tanks and a pump room near the Ironstone Circuit frontage. Fire hose reels are required to AS 2441.
An occupant warning system is required throughout, activated by the sprinkler system.

## Racking

Maximum top of storage: 11 m in selective pallet racking

## Access and egress

Travel distances in the warehouse exceed the Deemed-to-Satisfy limits of D2D5 and are to be
addressed by a performance solution. Exits from the warehouse are distributed along the long walls so
that no exit is more than 60 m from another. The office has two exits to open space. Access for
people with a disability is to be provided to and within the office and to the warehouse amenities.

## Energy

Section J applies. The office envelope, glazing and air-conditioning, and the artificial lighting to
the office and the warehouse, are to comply with the Deemed-to-Satisfy provisions of Part J.

## Fire engineering

No vulnerable occupants requiring assisted evacuation are expected in any part of the building.

No special fire hazards are proposed and no dangerous goods are kept above minor quantities.

No fire performance solution is proposed for the facade, cladding or structure; performance solutions
cover only warehouse travel distances and the perimeter vehicular access.

## Regulatory

The building has no Class 2, 3 or 9c part, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act 2020 does not
apply.
