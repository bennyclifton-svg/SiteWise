## The application

We are pleased to offer our services to prepare and manage a development application to Penrith City
Council for the warehouse and office.

The land is zoned E4 General Industrial, and a warehouse with an ancillary office is permitted with
consent. The building does not meet the rear setback standard of the Industrial and Business Buildings Code, so
complying development is not available and a DA is required. We have reviewed the architect's concept
against Penrith Local Environmental Plan 2010 and the Penrith Development Control Plan and raise no
planning concerns with it.

We will lodge the development application on the NSW Planning Portal for Redgum Logistics Estates,
once the architect's DA documentation and the consultants' reports are complete.

## Scope

**Pre-lodgement meeting and planning advice.** Planning controls review, a pre-lodgement meeting with
council officers and a summary of their advice to the design team.

**Statement of environmental effects.** Assessment of the proposal against the LEP, DCP and relevant
State environmental planning policies, drawing on the architect's drawings and the traffic, civil,
geotechnical and BCA reports.

**DA lodgement and assessment liaison.** Portal lodgement, payment of fees on your behalf, liaison
with the assessing officer, responses to council's requests for information and review of the draft
conditions.
