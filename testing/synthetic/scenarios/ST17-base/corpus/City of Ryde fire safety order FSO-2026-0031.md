# Fire safety order: external wall cladding

CITY OF RYDE
Locked Bag 2069, North Ryde NSW 1670

c/- Harbourline Strata Management Pty Ltd, strata managing agent

Premises: Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114 (SP 87654)

Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979, section 9.34 and Schedule 5, Part 1 (fire safety order)

Council gave the owners corporation notice of its intention to give this order on 3 November 2025.
Council has considered the representations made on behalf of the owners corporation on 1 December 2025
and now gives the order set out below.

## Order

You are ordered to do the following in relation to the premises:

1. Remove the polyethylene-core aluminium composite panels from the balcony spandrels and blades on
   all elevations of the building.
2. Remove the expanded polystyrene render system from the level 7 and level 8 setback walls.
3. Replace the removed materials with an external wall that complies with the National Construction
   Code as in force at the time the replacement is carried out.

This order relates to the external wall cladding only.

## Compliance date

The order must be complied with by 17 December 2027.

## Interim fire safety measures

Until the order is complied with, you must maintain the following interim fire safety measures: no
storage or barbecues on balconies; a hot-works permit system for any work involving heat or flame on or
near the external walls; a monthly inspection of the essential fire safety measures by a competent
fire safety practitioner, with a record kept on site; and a resident information notice, displayed in
the main lobby and given to every lot owner and resident, explaining the order and the interim
measures.

## Progress reports

You must give council a written progress report every three months from the date of this order until
it is complied with. Each report is to describe the work done in the period, the work proposed for the
next period and any change to the likely completion date. Reports are to be sent to council's fire
safety officer, Karen Liu.

## Reasons

The order is given because council is satisfied that the external wall cladding of the premises poses
a risk to the safety of persons in the building in the event of fire. Council relies on the
combustible cladding audit commissioned by the owners corporation in 2025, which identified
polyethylene-core aluminium composite panels and an expanded polystyrene render system on the
external walls, and on council's own inspection of the premises.

## Appeal

You may appeal against this order to the Land and Environment Court within 28 days after the date on
which the order is given.

## Failure to comply

It is an offence to fail to comply with this order. If the order is not complied with, council may
carry out the work required and recover its costs from the owners corporation.
