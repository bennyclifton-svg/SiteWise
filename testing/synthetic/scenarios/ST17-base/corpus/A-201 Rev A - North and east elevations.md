Brightline Architects Pty Ltd
Project: Facade remediation, Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114
Client: The Owners - Strata Plan No. 87654
Scale: 1:100 at A1

## Sheet shows

- North elevation to Tideway Crescent and east elevation to Bennelong Lane, levels 1 to 8

4. Existing windows and glass balustrades are retained and protected throughout the works.
