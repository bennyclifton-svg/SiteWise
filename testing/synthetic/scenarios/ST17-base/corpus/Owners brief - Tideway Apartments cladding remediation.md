# Owners' brief: combustible cladding remediation project team

Prepared by Harbourline Strata Management Pty Ltd, strata managing agent, and approved by the strata
committee on 29 January 2026.

## The building

Tideway Apartments is an 8-storey residential flat building of 56 apartments, constructed in 2011. It
has a reinforced concrete frame and stands on a corner, with frontages to Tideway Crescent and
Bennelong Lane. The building is built to the street boundary on both frontages.

The building is a strata scheme; the owners corporation acts through its strata committee, with
Harbourline Strata Management as its strata managing agent, and every spending decision needs a
committee or general meeting resolution. Owners should expect the project team to report to the
committee each month and to attend the general meetings at which fees, levies and the building
contract are approved.

## The order

The owners corporation is undertaking the remediation of the combustible external wall cladding of the
existing building, as required by council's fire safety order. The City of Ryde gave the order on
19 January 2026, and it must be complied with by 17 December 2027.

The order follows the cladding audit the owners corporation commissioned in 2025. The audit found
polyethylene-core (PE-core) aluminium composite panels on the balcony spandrels and blades and an
expanded polystyrene (EPS) render system on the upper setback walls. The audit report and the order
are attached to this brief.

## Funding

The owners corporation is funding the works through special levies without government assistance.
The first levy, for consultant fees, will be put to a general meeting once the team's fees are known.
A second levy, for the building contract, will follow tenders.

## Approvals

Council has advised that a development application is required for the change to the external
materials and appearance. The team's planner and architect will need to prepare the application
together, and the program should allow for council's assessment period.

## Team sought

The committee seeks proposals from:

- a project manager, to lead the team, manage the program against the compliance date, report to the
  committee and act as superintendent during construction;
- an architect, to design the new facade and prepare the application drawings and tender documents;
- a fire safety engineer;
- a facade engineer;
- a structural engineer, for the fixing of the new cladding to the existing frame;
- a quantity surveyor; and
- a town planner.

A BCA consultant and a principal certifier will be appointed later, once the design is settled.

## Delivery

The consultant team will fully document the works for a lump sum tender to remedial builders. The
committee wants the design settled, priced and approved by owners before a builder is engaged.

The building will remain fully occupied throughout the works. Residents' safety, access to the
entrances and the car park, and noise will need careful management, and residents must be kept
informed.

The building stands on the street boundary on both frontages, so the perimeter scaffold and an
overhead hoarding must stand over the council footpath, with deliveries from the street.

## What to include

Each proposal should set out:

1. your understanding of the project and its risks;
2. your scope of services, from now to the closure of council's order;
3. a fee broken down by phase, with the GST basis stated, and your hourly rates;
4. the people who will do the work, and their registrations; and
5. relevant remediation projects your firm has completed on occupied strata buildings.
