# North and east elevations: proposed cladding

Brightline Architects Pty Ltd
Project: Facade remediation, Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114
Client: The Owners - Strata Plan No. 87654
Scale: 1:100 at A1

## Sheet shows

- North elevation to Tideway Crescent and east elevation to Bennelong Lane, levels 1 to 8
- Panel layout for the balcony spandrels, balcony blades and level 7 and 8 setback walls, with joints
  aligned to the facade engineer's subframe grid
- Colour codes: C1 warm grey to spandrels, C3 light grey to setback walls, C2 bronze to blades
- Cavity barrier lines at each slab edge
- Retained windows, shown hatched

## General notes

1. Do not scale from this drawing. Use written dimensions.
2. Read with the facade engineer's performance specification and the fire engineer's report.
3. Panel joints are set out to the facade engineer's subframe grid. Do not adjust without approval.
4. Existing windows and glass balustrades are retained and protected throughout the works.
