# Slab edge investigation and pull-out test report

Tallow Consulting Engineers Pty Ltd
Prepared for: The Owners - Strata Plan No. 87654, c/- Harbourline Strata Management
Project: Cladding remediation, Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114
Author: Callum Reid, senior structural engineer

## Existing structure

The building was constructed in 2011. The slab edges and columns of the existing building are
reinforced concrete. The floors are flat plates of 200 mm, with edge thickenings at the balconies.

We measured the concrete cover and the edge distance at each opening with a cover meter and confirmed
them by drilling. Cover ranged from 30 mm to 45 mm.

The building has no transfer slabs or transfer beams in the zones where the brackets fix.

## Openings

A contractor engaged for the investigation made six sample openings on four elevations, at levels 2, 4,
5, 6 and 8, by removing a panel and cutting back the sarking.

| Opening | Elevation | Level | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1 | North | 2 | Balcony spandrel |
| O2 | North | 6 | Balcony blade |
| O3 | East | 4 | Balcony spandrel |
| O4 | South | 5 | Balcony spandrel |
| O5 | West | 6 | Balcony blade |
| O6 | West | 8 | Setback wall |

The substrate and fixings were sound in all sample openings. Balcony waterproofing upturns were found
intact in all sample openings. Each opening was closed with a temporary non-combustible board.

## Pull-out tests

We tested 24 anchors, four at each opening, to 1.5 times the working load. All anchors passed with no
measurable movement.

## Recommendations

1. Brackets are to be set out on the facade engineer's subframe grid, with no bracket closer than
   100 mm to a slab edge.
2. Use stainless steel anchors of the type tested, or an approved equal tested on site.
3. Where a slab edge is found damaged during the works, repair it with the provisional method in
   Appendix B: break out to sound concrete, treat the reinforcement and reinstate with a polymer-modified
   repair mortar.

The bracket fixings into the primary structure are part of the regulated design under the Design and
Building Practitioners Act 2020.

## Scope limits

The works are confined to the external walls; no basement or ground works are involved.
No excavation is involved, and nothing is done near the adjoining boundaries or neighbouring
structures.
