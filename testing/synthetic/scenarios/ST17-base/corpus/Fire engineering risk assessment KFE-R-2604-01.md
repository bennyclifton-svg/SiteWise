# Fire engineering risk assessment and interim fire safety strategy

Kestrel Fire Engineering Pty Ltd
Prepared for: The Owners - Strata Plan No. 87654, c/- Harbourline Strata Management
Project: Cladding remediation, Tideway Apartments, 27 Tideway Crescent, Meadowbank NSW 2114
Author: Tomasz Wrobel, fire safety engineer

## Building

- NCC building classification: Class 2. The building is required to be of Type A construction.
- The building has a rise in storeys of 8.
- The building is not sprinkler protected.
- The building will remain fully occupied throughout the works.

## Risk assessment

This assessment follows our 2025 audit (KFE-A-2519) and the laboratory results for the 12 samples. All
of the aluminium composite panel samples have a polyethylene core, the setback wall samples are
expanded polystyrene, and the foil sarking behind the panels is combustible.

The fire engineer's assessment identifies the external wall cladding as a facade fire performance issue
requiring rectification. The PE-core panels run vertically on the balcony spandrels and blades over
several storeys, so a fire on one balcony could spread to the levels above before the fire brigade
arrives.

There are no vulnerable occupants who need assisted evacuation beyond the general resident population.

There are no special fire hazards or dangerous goods in the building.

## Removal extent

All combustible cladding and insulation will be removed and replaced to the deemed-to-satisfy
provisions. This includes the PE-core panels, the EPS render system and the combustible foil sarking.
The replacement wall is to be non-combustible throughout, with cavity barriers at each slab edge.

## Interim strategy

Until the replacement is complete, the owners corporation must keep up the interim measures required by
council's order: the balcony restrictions, the hot-works permit system, the monthly inspections and the
resident information notice.

During the works, the builder must also follow a removal sequence that never leaves exposed EPS
overnight. Each section of EPS render that is opened in a day is to be removed in full and bagged off
the building by the end of that day. Hot works near the existing cladding are not permitted.

## The order

The order relates to the external wall cladding only. No other upgrade of the building's fire safety
measures is required by council, and none is recommended by this assessment.

## Inspections

We will inspect the cavity barriers and slab-edge protection on each elevation before they are closed
up by the new panels. The builder is to give us two working days' notice of each inspection.
