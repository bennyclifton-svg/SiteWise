# Annual fire safety statement and fire safety schedule

Owner: Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Contact: Megan Holt, Asset Manager, megan.holt@example.com
Statement period: the 12 months ending 31 December 2025

Issued under the Environmental Planning and Assessment (Development Certification and Fire Safety)
Regulation 2021.

## Building

This statement covers the existing building at 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060, an office building.

- The building is Class 5 (office) with a Class 7a basement car park.
- The building has a rise in storeys of 6.
- The building is sprinkler protected throughout to AS 2118.1.
- The building has no performance solutions; it was approved to the deemed-to-satisfy provisions.

## Statement

I, Megan Holt, on behalf of the owner of the building, declare that:

1. each essential fire safety measure listed in the schedule below has been assessed by a competent
   fire safety practitioner and was found, when assessed, to be capable of performing to at least the
   standard of performance specified in the current fire safety schedule for the building; and
2. the building has been inspected by a competent fire safety practitioner and was found, when
   inspected, to be in a condition that did not disclose any grounds for a prosecution under the
   Regulation.

Note on the automatic fire sprinkler system: the 2025 annual flow test passed with a thin margin at the
duty point. The owner is planning an upgrade of the water supply.

Date of assessment: 2 December 2025 to 9 December 2025.

## Fire safety schedule

| Essential fire safety measure | Standard of performance |
|---|---|
| Automatic fire sprinkler system | AS 2118.1 |
| Fire hydrants and booster assembly | AS 2419.1 |
| Fire detection and alarm system | AS 1670.1 |
| Emergency warning and intercommunication system (EWIS) | AS 1670.4 |
| Emergency lighting and exit signs | AS/NZS 2293.1 |
| Fire doors to the fire stairs | AS 1905.1 |
| Portable fire extinguishers | AS 2444 |

## Evacuation

The building is evacuated by egress via the fire stairs to the street. The evacuation diagrams on each floor
were reviewed in November 2025 and remain current.

A copy of this statement has been given to the council and to the Commissioner of Fire and Rescue NSW,
and is displayed in the main entrance lobby.
