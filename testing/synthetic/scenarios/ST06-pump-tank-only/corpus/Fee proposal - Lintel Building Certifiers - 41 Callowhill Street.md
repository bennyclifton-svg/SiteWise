## Approval pathway

The works will be approved by one complying development certificate under the Fire Safety Code of the Codes SEPP.
The certificate covers the pumps, the tank, the enlarged pump room and the associated fire service works.

We have discussed the proposed arrangement with Calder Fire and Hydraulic Engineers: new pumpsets and a
tank inside the existing basement pump room and the adjoining store. On that arrangement we
confirmed the tank and pump house meet the Fire Safety Code development standards. The works
proceed as complying development; no consent-authority upgrade applies.

As complying development, no planning panel is involved. No clause 4.6 variation is involved.

Whether a fire safety schedule and a final fire safety certificate are needed for this alteration will
be confirmed at assessment.

The building is Class 5 (office) with a Class 7a basement car park.
