## Our understanding

The building's sprinkler system is boosted by a single old electric pump with no standby. The 2025
annual flow test passed with a thin margin at the duty point, and the latest pressure and flow figures
for the street are lower than those used for the original design. The building is fully occupied and
stays occupied while the work is done, so every shutdown has to be planned around the tenants.

The scope is limited to the fire pump and tank upgrade. The existing sprinkler, detection and warning
layouts on the floors are not altered.

## Scope

We will review the records, test the existing system, report on the options for the upgrade and
recommend one, then design, document and certify the recommended option. We will prepare the fire
services tender package, assess tenders, review the work during construction at agreed hold points
and witness commissioning.
