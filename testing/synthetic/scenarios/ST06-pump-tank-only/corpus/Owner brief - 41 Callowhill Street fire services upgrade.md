# Owner's brief: sprinkler water supply upgrade

This brief invites the consultant team to propose for an upgrade of the sprinkler water supply at
41 Callowhill Street.

## The building

41 Callowhill Street is a 6-storey office building over a single basement car park level, constructed in 1988.
The building is in single ownership. Corvina Property Holdings is the sole owner and the only decision maker
on these works; approvals sit with me as asset manager, within the budget below.

Every floor is leased and occupied. The level 3 lease was renewed in January 2026.
Level 3 remains leased to the existing tenant and is not affected
by these works.

## Why we are acting

The 2025 annual sprinkler flow test passed, but with a thin margin at the duty point. Sydney Water's
recent statement of available pressure and flow also shows lower pressure in the street than the
figures the original design relied on. The fire pump room in the basement holds one old electric pump,
which is near the end of its service life and has no standby.

The upgrade is a voluntary capital works item; no order or notice has been issued. We want to restore a
comfortable margin before it becomes a compliance problem.

## Scope

We are refurbishing the base-building fire services by upgrading the sprinkler water supply of the existing building. The scope is limited to the fire pump and tank upgrade; no tenancy works are included.

The work is a new pumpset arrangement and a fire water tank, both sized by the engineer. We expect the
existing pump room to be enlarged into the adjoining store, but we leave the arrangement to the
engineer's options report.

## Budget

Approved budget: $1,200,000 excluding GST (fire services upgrade).

The budget covers construction. Consultant fees and authority charges are held separately by Corvina.

## Delivery

The consultants will fully document the upgrade for a traditional lump sum tender to fire protection contractors.
The works contract will be AS 4000-1997 General conditions of contract, with special conditions.
The contract is delivered as one construction stage; there is no staged construction or staged occupation.

All floors remain tenanted and in use throughout the works. Any shutdown of the sprinkler system is to
be planned out of hours, with notice to tenants.

The building stands on the street boundary in the North Sydney CBD; every delivery comes through the basement car park entry or the loading dock, booked around the tenants, and there is no on-street loading.

## Expectations

- Mid-range quality: good, durable plant and finishes in the pump room, nothing high end.
- No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target is set for these works.
- This is one building; we are not planning multiple buildings.
- A standard CBD site with no ecological, habitat or Aboriginal heritage constraints.

## Team sought

We are seeking proposals from:

1. A hydraulic and fire services engineer, to lead the water supply upgrade.
2. A structural engineer, for the slab under the new equipment.
3. An electrical engineer.
4. A mechanical engineer.
5. A hazardous materials consultant.
6. A registered certifier.
