## Our understanding

Corvina will deliver a fit-out of the whole of level 3 for an incoming tenant. The tenant needs an
open-plan office, meeting rooms, a comms room, a tea point and a reception. The base-building toilets
on level 3 are not altered. The fit-out is to be mid-range and durable, documented for certification
and for a lump sum tender to fit-out builders.

## Scope

- Confirm the tenant's brief and prepare a test fit of the floor.
- Develop a concept and then a detailed design, coordinated with the fire services, electrical and
  mechanical engineers.
- Document the fit-out for the complying development certificate and for tender.
- Run the tender and recommend a builder.
- Administer the contract and answer site queries through to completion.
