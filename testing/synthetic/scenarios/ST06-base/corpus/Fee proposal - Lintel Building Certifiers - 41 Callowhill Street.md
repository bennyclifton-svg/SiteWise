## Approval pathway

The works will be approved by two complying development certificates under the Codes SEPP: one under the Fire Safety Code and one for the level 3 fit-out.
The first certificate covers the pumps, the tank, the enlarged pump room and the associated fire
service works. The second covers the internal alterations and fit-out of level 3.

We have discussed the proposed arrangement with Calder Fire and Hydraulic Engineers: new pumpsets and a
tank inside the existing basement pump room and the adjoining store. On that arrangement the certifier
confirmed the tank and pump house meet the Fire Safety Code development standards. The works
proceed as complying development; no consent-authority upgrade applies.

As complying development, no planning panel is involved. No clause 4.6 variation is involved.

## The building

The building is Class 5 (office) with a Class 7a basement car park.
This is a Class 5 office building, so the Design and Building Practitioners Act regulated-design obligations do not apply.
