# Owner's brief: sprinkler water supply upgrade and level 3 fit-out

Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd

Prepared by Megan Holt, Asset Manager

This brief invites the consultant team to propose for two packages of work at 41 Callowhill Street:
an upgrade of the building's sprinkler water supply, and the fit-out of the vacant level 3 for an
incoming tenant.

## The building

41 Callowhill Street is a 6-storey office building over a single basement car park level, constructed in 1988.
The building is in single ownership. Corvina Property Holdings is the sole owner and the only decision maker
on these works; approvals sit with me as asset manager, within the budget below.

Levels 1, 2 and 4 to 6 are leased and occupied. Level 3 has been vacant since the previous tenant left
in late 2025, and we have agreed heads of terms with a new tenant for the whole floor.

## Why we are acting

The 2025 annual sprinkler flow test passed, but with a thin margin at the duty point. Sydney Water's
recent statement of available pressure and flow also shows lower pressure in the street than the
figures the original design relied on. The fire pump room in the basement holds one old electric pump,
which is near the end of its service life and has no standby.

The upgrade is a voluntary capital works item; no order or notice has been issued. We want to restore a
comfortable margin before it becomes a compliance problem, and before the new tenant moves in.

## Scope

We are upgrading the sprinkler water supply of the existing building and carrying out a refurbishment and fit-out of the vacant level 3.

- Sprinkler water supply: a new pumpset arrangement and a fire water tank, both sized by the engineer.
  We expect the existing pump room to be enlarged into the adjoining store, but we leave the
  arrangement to the engineer's options report.
- Level 3: a mid-range office fit-out to suit the incoming tenant's brief, with the sprinkler,
  detection and warning system alterations that go with it.

The level 3 fit-out is delivered with the fire services upgrade. The landlord delivers the level 3 fit-out as landlord's works.
Level 3 is occupied only after the fire services upgrade is commissioned.

## Budget

Approved budget: $2,700,000 excluding GST (fire services upgrade $1,200,000; level 3 fit-out $1,500,000).

The budget covers construction. Consultant fees and authority charges are held separately by Corvina.

## Delivery

The consultants will fully document both packages for traditional lump sum tenders: one to fire protection contractors for the upgrade and one to fit-out builders for level 3.
Both works contracts will be AS 4000-1997 General conditions of contract, with special conditions.
Each contract is delivered as one construction stage; there is no staged construction or staged occupation.

All floors except level 3 remain tenanted and in use throughout the works. Any shutdown of the
sprinkler system is to be planned out of hours, with notice to tenants.

The building stands on the street boundary in the North Sydney CBD; every delivery comes through the basement car park entry or the loading dock, booked around the tenants, and there is no on-street loading.

## Expectations

- Mid-range commercial fit-out: good quality and durable, nothing high end.
- No Green Star, NABERS or similar rating target is set for these works.
- This is one building; we are not planning multiple buildings.
- A standard CBD site with no ecological, habitat or Aboriginal heritage constraints.
