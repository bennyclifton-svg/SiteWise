# Fire services options report: sprinkler water supply upgrade

Prepared by Calder Fire and Hydraulic Engineers Pty Ltd for Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Site: 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060

This report sets out the options for upgrading the sprinkler water supply of the existing building at
41 Callowhill Street, the 6-storey office building, and recommends one.

## Existing system

The building is sprinkler protected throughout to AS 2118.1. One electric pump boosts the town main.
The sprinkler system is supplied from Sydney Water's reticulated town water main through the existing fire service connection.
The hydrant system is unaffected and remains town-main fed through the existing booster.
The building has no performance solutions; it was approved to the deemed-to-satisfy provisions.

## Test results

We carried out a flow and pressure test at the sprinkler test line in March 2026 and compared the
results with Sydney Water's statement of available pressure and flow dated 2 February 2026. The main
alone cannot meet the sprinkler demand at the hydraulically most disadvantaged area, and the existing
pump meets it only with a margin that will not survive a further fall in main pressure.

## Options

Option A: a larger single electric pump. This would restore the margin on the current figures, but it
leaves the building with one pump, no standby and full reliance on the main. Rejected.

Option B: an electric duty pumpset, a diesel standby pumpset, a jockey pump and a 150 kL modular fire
water tank, with the pumps drawing from the tank. Recommended.

## Recommended option

The tank fits within the existing basement store; no car spaces or external areas are affected.
The pump room is enlarged into the store, and the tank is assembled in place from panels.
Tank panels are carried in through the basement car park entry; no crane is needed.
No heavy vehicle access is needed: the tank panels arrive by light truck.

The existing fire service connection is retained; Sydney Water confirmed the main is adequate.
The main can also refill the tank within the required time. The
backflow device is upgraded to a testable containment device.

The electrical engineer confirmed the MSB has capacity and a spare supply-side connection.

The diesel pump draws from a bunded day tank in the pump room. The pump room floor drains to the car
park sump to protect the pump room from flooding.

The diesel exhaust discharges through the existing basement plant exhaust shaft, clear of tenancies, air intakes and neighbours; the weekly test run is not audible in the tenancies.

## Compliance

The design complies with the deemed-to-satisfy provisions; no performance solution is proposed.

There are no special fire hazards or dangerous goods in the building; the fire water storage tank and the diesel fuel for the standby pump are part of the sprinkler water supply, not a special hazard.

No structural fire assessment and no facade, cladding or tested-system fire departure is required; no specialist fire systems are needed.

There are no vulnerable occupants needing assisted evacuation.

## Site and authority matters

The works do not build over or next to any sewer main or water main. No public road works or road opening are required.
There is no existing on-site detention. The works do not change the roof or any hard surface, so on-site detention is not required.

## Approvals and delivery

The works will be approved by two complying development certificates under the Codes SEPP: one under the Fire Safety Code and one for the level 3 fit-out.
Both works packages are to be let as lump sums under AS 4000-1997 General conditions of contract, with special conditions.
Each contract is one construction stage; there is no staged construction.

## Impairment plan

All floors except level 3 remain tenanted and in use throughout the works. The sprinkler system will
be impaired only for the cut-overs, and only out of hours, with tenants and the monitoring service
notified beforehand.

- Stage 1 cut-over: out of hours, with a hire pump connected and a fire watch in place for the whole
  period the system is impaired.
- Stage 2 commissioning: the new pumpsets are run, tested and handed over, and the old pump is
  disconnected.

Before commissioning, the contractor will flood the tank for its hydrostatic test and hold it full for
the period the supplier specifies.

## Level 3 link

The level 3 sprinkler layout will be calculated on the new supply. Its commissioning follows the pump
commissioning, so the level 3 fit-out cannot be completed before the new pumps are running.
