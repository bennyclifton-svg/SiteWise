## Background

Corvina Property Holdings is upgrading the sprinkler water supply at 41 Callowhill Street with new
pumpsets and a tank in an enlarged pump room. We have been asked to assess the existing slab, design
any plinths or strengthening needed under the new equipment, and inspect that work on site.

## Fees

| Item | Description | Fee (ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Existing slab assessment, record drawing review and slab scanning | $7,500 |
| 2 | Tank plinth and slab strengthening design | $9,000 |
| 3 | Core hole and penetration review | $2,500 |
| 4 | Structural design certification | $1,500 |
| 5 | Construction inspections of plinths and strengthening before concrete | $3,500 |
| | **Total $24,000 excluding GST** | **$24,000** |

Fees exclude GST.

Additional inspections beyond those allowed for in item 5: $480 each, excluding GST.

## Notes

1. The structural engineer will confirm the slab for the fire water storage tank once the tank operating load is known.
2. The pump room is in the existing basement; no basement is built or excavated.
3. Slab scanning is carried out out of hours, with the car park bays around the store coned off.
4. We have assumed record drawings are available from the owner's files.

## Exclusions

- Design of the tank itself, which is the supplier's responsibility.
- Hydraulic, electrical and mechanical design.
- Certifier and authority fees.
