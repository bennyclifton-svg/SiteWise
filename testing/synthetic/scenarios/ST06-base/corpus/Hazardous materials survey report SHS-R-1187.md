# Pre-refurbishment hazardous materials survey

Prepared by Sentinel Hazmat Surveys Pty Ltd for Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Site: 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060
Surveyor: Tom Reyes, Senior Consultant

## Scope

This survey covers the areas of the existing building that the works will disturb: the pump room, the
adjoining store, the risers and level 3. The building was constructed in 1988.

## Method

We inspected the areas on 31 March 2026. We took 14 samples of suspect materials and had them analysed
by a NATA-accredited laboratory. Areas we could not reach are listed in the appendix.

## Findings

The 2024 hazardous materials survey found no asbestos in the areas affected by the works. This
re-inspection confirms it: none of the 14 samples contained asbestos.

No lead paint was found in the pump room.

Man-made mineral fibre lagging is fitted to the old pump discharge pipe. It is to be removed with
standard controls: wet methods, a P2 respirator and bagged disposal.

No other hazardous materials were found in the surveyed areas.

## Register

The asbestos register has been updated for the works areas and a copy is attached. The register should
be kept on site and shown to every contractor before work starts.
