# Level 3 fit-out concept design report

Prepared by Marlowe Interiors Pty Ltd for Corvina Property Holdings Pty Ltd
Project: fit-out of level 3 of the 6-storey office building at 41 Callowhill Street, North Sydney NSW 2060

## Brief

The incoming tenant wants an open-plan workplace for 80 people, four meeting rooms, a comms room, a
tea point and a reception facing the main lobby. They want a calm, durable workplace with good daylight
to the open-plan desks.

## Concept

- Meeting rooms are grouped along the core, with glazed fronts to borrow light.
- The existing ceiling grid is kept, with new tiles throughout.
- Carpet tiles to the office areas and meeting rooms; vinyl to the tea point.
- The reception is a simple desk with a feature wall behind.

## Services coordination

- Sprinkler heads, detectors and EWIS speakers are relocated to suit the new rooms.
- VAV boxes are relocated to serve the meeting rooms.
- A split system cools the comms room.

## Amenities and access

The base-building toilets on level 3 are not altered.

The certifier confirmed the existing path of travel from the principal pedestrian entrance to level 3 already complies; no affected-part upgrade is required.

## Next steps

We will take this concept into design development once the tenant has signed off the layout, and
coordinate it with the engineers' designs.

Revision 1 updates the meeting room sizes after the tenant's comments on Revision 0.
