## Scope

Before the pump room and level 3 works start, we will survey the areas the works will disturb for
hazardous materials, so the contractors can price and plan any removal. We will inspect the pump room,
the adjoining store, the risers and level 3, take samples of suspect materials, and have them analysed
by a NATA-accredited laboratory. We will then update the building's asbestos register for those areas
and issue a report.
