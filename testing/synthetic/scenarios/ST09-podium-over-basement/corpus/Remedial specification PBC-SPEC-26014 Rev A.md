# Remedial specification: balcony waterproofing

Parapet Building Consultants Pty Ltd
Kurrajong Apartments, 41 Kurrajong Parade, Hurstville NSW 2220
Principal: The Owners - Strata Plan No. 912345
Read with drawing RW-101 Rev B, the Karrak Consulting Engineers balustrade fixing design, and the
landscape architect's and hydraulic engineer's podium details.

## General

1. **Extent.** Renew the waterproofing of all 42 balconies, Levels 1 to 6, and of the ground-floor
   courtyard podium over the basement car park, like for like.
2. **Approvals.** The works are exempt development: like-for-like repair with no change to the external
   appearance; no development consent or construction certificate is required.
3. **Occupation.** The building will remain fully occupied throughout the works. See Resident access and
   protection.
4. **Contract.** AS 4000-1997 General conditions of contract, with special conditions, as set out in the
   conditions of tender PBC-T-26014.
5. **Regulated design.** This specification and drawing RW-101 are regulated designs under the Design
   and Building Practitioners Act 2020; a design compliance declaration accompanies them. The contractor
   must be a registered building practitioner and must lodge the building compliance declaration.
6. **Authority assets.** The works do not build over or next to any sewer main or water main.
7. **Delivery.** There will be no staged delivery: all 42 balconies and the courtyard podium are renewed
   under one contract in one continuous stage. The contractor may work on up to eight balconies at a
   time.

## Strip-out

1. Remove pot plants, furniture and other loose items left on the balcony, by arrangement with the occupant.
2. Protect the glass balustrade panels, door frames and door glazing.
3. Demolish the tiles, bedding and failed membrane down to the structural slab. Do not damage the
   door frames or sills.
4. Remove the existing outlet fittings and cut the balustrade post grout pockets back to sound
   concrete.
5. Remove all debris through the lift in covered bins.

## Substrate and falls screed

1. Prepare the slab to a clean, sound surface. Report any defective concrete to the superintendent
   before proceeding.
2. Lay a bonded sand-cement falls screed at a minimum fall of 1:80 to the outlets, with no ponding.
3. Keep the finished screed level so that the door sill set-down is maintained.

## Outlets and overflows

1. Fit new outlets with puddle flanges and clamping rings, connected to the existing downpipes.
2. Install one new overflow per balcony through the balustrade line, discharging clear of the slab
   edge, as shown on RW-101 Detail 6.
3. Apart from the podium drainage below the courtyard, the existing building stormwater system, which
   has no on-site detention, is not altered.

## Membrane system

1. External liquid-applied membrane to AS 4654.2, compatible with the falls screed and tile adhesive,
   applied in two coats to the manufacturer's thickness.
2. Turn the membrane up behind the door sills and up the walls to a minimum of 100 mm above the
   finished tile level.
3. Bond-breaker at all internal corners and at the slab to wall junctions.
4. Seal the membrane to every outlet flange and overflow.

## Podium

1. Lift the courtyard pavers and pedestals, number them and store them on site for reuse.
2. Remove the planting and soil from the planter boxes. Set aside plants marked for reuse by the
   landscape architect.
3. Remove the failed podium membrane down to the structural slab, and prepare and screed to falls as
   for the balconies.
4. Lay a new podium membrane turned up the planter box walls, with protection board and a drainage
   cell over it.
5. Fit new podium outlets with puddle flanges and add overflows, to the hydraulic engineer's details.
6. Protect the basement car park below: close the bays under the open area, keep the car park
   ventilated and lit, and provide temporary drainage so no water reaches the car park while the
   membrane is open.
7. Podium flood test: 50 mm of water for 24 hours over each bay of membrane before any cover, with the
   car park soffit watched below.
8. Reinstate soil, planting, pedestals and pavers to the landscape architect's details. The
   reinstated soil depth must not exceed the structural engineer's load check.

## Balustrades

1. Retain the existing glass balustrade panels and handrails, protected throughout.
2. Re-fix the posts to the slab edge with side-fixed brackets outside the membrane, to the structural
   engineer's design. The structural engineer certifies the fixings.
3. Fill the old core holes and make good before the membrane is laid.

## Tiling

1. Protection screed and tile bed laid over the cured membrane, falling with the substrate.
2. Porcelain tiles selected to match the existing as closely as possible, approved by the
   superintendent from a sample.
3. Movement joints at the perimeter and at no more than 4.5 m centres.

## Hold points

The superintendent inspects and releases each hold point on every balcony, and on each bay of the
podium, before the work is covered.

1. Substrate: prepared slab and falls screed.
2. Outlets: outlets, flanges and overflows fitted.
3. Membrane flood test: 50 mm of water for 24 hours before screed. Any leak is repaired and the
   test repeated.
4. Before cover: balustrade fixings and membrane upturns, with the structural engineer's sign-off on
   the fixings.

## Resident access and protection

1. Give each occupant 7 days' written notice before work starts on their balcony.
2. Keep the balcony doors operable and weathertight at the end of each day.
3. Protect the lift car, lobbies and common corridors used for access.
4. Working hours 7.30 am to 4.30 pm, Monday to Friday. No noisy work on weekends or public holidays.
5. Keep each balcony out of use for no more than 15 working days.
