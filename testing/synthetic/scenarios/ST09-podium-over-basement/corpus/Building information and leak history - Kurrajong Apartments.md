# Building information and leak history

Kurrajong Apartments, 41 Kurrajong Parade, Hurstville NSW 2220.
For consultants invited to propose under brief NSM-912345-RFP1.

## Building

- Strata Plan 912345, 42 lots, managed by Northgate Strata Management for the owners corporation.
- 42 apartments in a 6-storey building, seven apartments on each residential level (Units 101 to 107
  through to Units 601 to 607). Every apartment has one tiled balcony.
- The building was constructed in 2008 and the strata plan was registered the same year.
- The building is out of statutory warranty. The owners corporation is not pursuing any claim.
- 6 storeys, with a reinforced concrete frame, aluminium-framed glass balustrades, aluminium sliding doors to the
  balconies.
- A single-level basement car park (level B1) of 48 bays. Part of it sits below the ground-floor
  communal courtyard, which has paving and raised planter boxes.

## Records held

The following can be made available to the appointed consultant:

- the registered strata plan and the current by-laws;
- the 2008 approved architectural and structural drawings (incomplete set);
- the 2019 hazardous materials survey of the common property, which found no asbestos;
- the capital works fund plan, last updated in 2023;
- invoices and reports for the sealant repairs carried out since 2023.

No internal damage has been reported in any lot. No plant or services are located on the balconies.
