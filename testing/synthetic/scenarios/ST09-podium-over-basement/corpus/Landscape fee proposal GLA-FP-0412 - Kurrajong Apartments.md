## Our understanding

The ground-floor communal courtyard at Kurrajong Apartments sits over the basement car park. Its
paving and planter boxes are to be lifted so that Parapet Building Consultants can have the podium
membrane renewed, and then put back. The owners corporation wants the courtyard to look and work as it
does now, with the planting renewed where it cannot be saved and the build-up over the new membrane
detailed so that it drains freely and does not damage the membrane again.

We walked the courtyard on 2 March 2026 with your building manager and recorded the existing paving,
planter boxes and planting.

## Services

1. Planter and soil build-up reinstatement over the new membrane: details for the protection, drainage
   and filter layers, the soil mix and depths, and the paver and pedestal reinstatement, coordinated
   with Parapet's podium details and the structural engineer's load limits.
2. Planting replacement schedule: a survey of the existing planting, a list of plants to be set aside
   and reused, and a schedule of new plants suited to shallow planter soil.
3. Root barrier and drainage layer coordination: the root barrier over the membrane, the drainage cell
   and the planter outlets, coordinated with the hydraulic engineer's outlet and overflow design.
4. Construction-phase inspections: three inspections, at the drainage layer, at soil placement and at
   completion of planting, with a short report after each.
