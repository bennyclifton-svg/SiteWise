## Scope

The courtyard is a podium over the basement car park. Its outlets drain through pipes hung from the
car park ceiling, and the podium has no overflows. With the podium membrane being renewed, the owners
corporation needs the courtyard outlets, overflows and the pipes below checked and, where needed,
redesigned. Our services are:

1. Podium outlet and overflow capacity check: catchment and rainfall intensity for the courtyard,
   capacity of the existing outlets, and the number, size and location of new overflows.
2. Charged or gravity drainage through the car park: confirm whether the existing pipe runs below the
   podium can stay as gravity lines or need to be replaced, and design the replacement runs, hangers
   and connections to the existing stormwater lines within the car park.
3. Construction-phase inspection and certificate: one inspection of the outlets and pipework before
   cover and a certificate of the installed drainage.

The balcony outlets and overflows are detailed by Parapet Building Consultants and are not part of our
scope. The existing stormwater system beyond the car park is not altered.
