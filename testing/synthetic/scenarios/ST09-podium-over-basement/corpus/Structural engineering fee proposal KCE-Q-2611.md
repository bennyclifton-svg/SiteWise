## Scope

We understand Parapet Building Consultants is investigating and specifying the renewal of the balcony
waterproofing, and that the owners corporation needs a remedial structural engineer for the balcony
slabs and the balustrade fixings. Our services are:

1. Assess the existing balcony slabs and slab edges at the four balconies opened up by Parapet:
   slab thickness, cover and condition, and report.
2. Design and certify the re-fixing of the balustrade posts, check the load of the new falls screed on
   the slabs, and write a provisional concrete repair specification for any defects found when the
   balconies are opened. Item 2 also checks the podium slab over the basement car park for the
   reinstated soil and planter load on the new courtyard membrane.
3. Answer tender queries on our documents.
4. Inspect the balustrade fixings and any concrete repairs during construction, and issue the fixing
   certificate on completion.

Inspections will reach the balconies through the lift and the fire stairs, by arrangement with the
building manager. No excavation and no new basement works are included; the existing basement car park
structure is not altered, so no geotechnical input is required.
