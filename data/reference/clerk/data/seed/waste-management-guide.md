# Waste Management

## Purpose and professional boundary

The waste consultant prepares the demolition and construction waste
management plan and the operational waste management plan, and advises on
bin rooms, chutes, compactors, collection points and vehicle access. The
traffic consultant tests collection vehicle swept paths; the architect
integrates rooms and chutes; the builder implements construction waste
management.

## Activation

Use this guide when the council DCP requires a waste management plan, which
is typical for multi-dwelling, residential flat, mixed-use, commercial and
industrial DAs, and for demolition.

## Evidence sweep

- Council DCP waste controls and waste guidelines (bin sizes, generation
  rates, collection method and frequency).
- Collection vehicle type and access; private versus council collection.
- Demolition scope and hazardous materials survey.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Review the council waste controls, collection arrangements and
the site, and confirm the waste requirements.

- Council waste guidelines, collection method and vehicle, and demolition
  waste streams.

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Advise on bin room size and location, chutes and collection
access for the schematic design.

- Generation rates, bin numbers, room areas and chute or compactor need.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Prepare the demolition, construction and operational waste
management plans the development application needs, and respond to council
requests on them.

- Waste management plans to council format with bin room layouts and
  collection details.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the waste conditions of consent and check the developed
design against the operational waste management plan.

- Bin room ventilation, wash-down, drainage and door widths coordinated
  with services and the architect.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Provide waste requirements for the tender documents, including
construction waste targets and reporting.

- Construction waste recycling targets and reporting requirements.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded.

- Price contractor-driven plan updates at hourly rates.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Review construction waste reporting and confirm the operational
waste provisions before the occupation certificate, if engaged.

- Waste tracking and recycling reports; final check of bin rooms and
  collection access.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Respond to design team waste queries throughout the appointment.

- Keep the waste plans current with design changes.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Plans included (demolition, construction, operational); number of
  revisions; hourly rates.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Bin rooms shrink during design development below council requirements.
- Collection vehicle cannot reach the collection point.
- Private collection assumed without cost in the operating budget.
- Flag low confidence when council has not confirmed its collection method.
