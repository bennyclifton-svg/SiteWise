# Surveying

## Purpose and professional boundary

The surveyor records existing site conditions and boundaries and sets out
and checks the works. Confirm the required registration and jurisdictional authority of the land
surveyor defining boundaries and signing subdivision or strata plans. The builder often engages set-out
surveys; state which party engages which survey.

## Activation

Use this guide for every project that needs design levels, boundary
definition, set-out, height or position certification, or subdivision or
strata plans.

## Evidence sweep

- Title, deposited plan, easements and covenants.
- Existing survey information and its datum (AHD and MGA).
- Council and certifier requirements for survey reports.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Prepare a detail and boundary survey to AHD and MGA with the
features the design team needs.

- Boundaries, levels, contours, trees, structures, services, adjoining
  building heights and windows, and easements.
- Title and easement search.

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Provide supplementary survey information the schematic design
needs, if engaged.

- Additional features, adjoining building details or roof levels.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Provide survey plans and certification the development
application needs.

- Survey plan for lodgement; height plane or shadow survey inputs where
  council requires them.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Prepare any subdivision, easement or boundary adjustment plans the
consent requires.

- Draft plans of subdivision, easements or strata where conditions need
  them.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Confirm survey information in the construction documentation and
state set-out responsibilities for tender.

- Datum, control marks and set-out responsibility in the tender documents.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded; the builder normally engages construction
set-out.

- Record which surveys the builder must provide.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Provide the set-out checks and survey certificates the certifier
needs, and the final subdivision or strata plans.

- Footing set-out, floor level and height checks, and identification survey
  at completion.
- Final plans of subdivision or strata for registration.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Respond to design team survey queries throughout the
appointment.

- Keep survey revisions and datum records in the document register.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Scope of features and extent of survey; datum; deliverable formats (CAD,
  PDF, point cloud).
- Rates for set-out and certification visits; subdivision or strata plans
  priced separately.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Survey without boundary definition used for setback-critical design.
- Adjoining building windows and levels missing for privacy and
  overshadowing.
- Set-out responsibility unclear between client surveyor and builder.
- Flag low confidence when the survey datum differs from the design datum.
