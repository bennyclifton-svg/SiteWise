# Geotechnical Engineering

## Purpose and professional boundary

The geotechnical engineer investigates the ground and advises on site
classification, foundations, excavation, retention, groundwater and
earthworks. The structural and civil engineers design the elements; the
geotechnical engineer provides their design parameters and inspects founding
conditions. Contamination sampling is a separate environmental scope unless
combined deliberately.

## Activation

Use this guide for every new building, extension with new footings,
basement, retaining wall, earthworks or pavement, and for sites with
reactive clay, fill, rock, groundwater, slope instability or acid sulfate
soils.

## Evidence sweep

- Survey, proposed building footprint, basement depth and loads.
- Geological and acid sulfate soil maps, previous reports and neighbouring
  investigations.
- Access for drilling rigs and underground service locations.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Carry out a geotechnical investigation scaled to the proposed
development, and report site classification and design parameters.

- Desktop review and site walkover; boreholes or test pits at a stated
  number and depth; laboratory testing.
- Report: subsurface profile, site classification (AS 2870 for residential),
  bearing capacities, groundwater, earthquake site sub-soil class and
  retaining parameters.
- Combine with contamination or acid sulfate soil sampling only when
  scoped.

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Advise on foundation, excavation and retention options for the
schematic design.

- Footing type options, basement retention and dewatering approach, and
  their cost and programme risk.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Provide the geotechnical report and statements the development
application needs.

- Report for lodgement; groundwater and dewatering advice for WaterNSW
  approval where a basement intercepts groundwater.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the geotechnical conditions of consent and provide
supplementary investigation and design advice.

- Additional boreholes if the design changes; review of the structural
  design against the geotechnical parameters.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Provide geotechnical information and inspection requirements
for the tender documents.

- Hold points for footing and excavation inspections; answers to tender
  queries on ground conditions.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded; the geotechnical engineer normally stays with
the client.

- Price contractor design support (for example piling design review) at
  hourly rates.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Inspect founding conditions, excavation and retention at hold
points, and certify them.

- Footing and pile founding inspections, excavation face inspections,
  earthworks compaction testing and monitoring review.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Respond to design team queries on ground conditions throughout
the appointment.

- Keep the inspection and certificate register current.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Number, type and depth of boreholes or test pits, and laboratory testing.
- Inspection rate and number of inspections allowed.
- Exclusions (contamination, acid sulfate soils, service location).

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Investigation too shallow for the final basement depth.
- Groundwater not measured over time; dewatering approval missed.
- Founding inspections not booked, so certification is incomplete.
- Flag low confidence when boreholes are far from the final footprint.
