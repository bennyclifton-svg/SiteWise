# Landscape Architecture

## Purpose and professional boundary

The landscape architect designs external spaces, planting, deep soil and
podium landscape, and public-domain works where council requires them. The
civil engineer owns levels and drainage; the arborist owns existing tree
protection; the structural engineer owns podium loads. The landscape
architect shares podium waterproofing and drainage interfaces with the
architect and hydraulic engineer.

## Activation

Use this guide when the DA needs a landscape plan, the planning controls set
deep soil, canopy or landscaped area targets, or the project includes podium,
roof or public-domain landscape.

## Evidence sweep

- DCP landscape, deep soil and canopy controls; Apartment Design Guide for
  residential flat buildings; council public-domain manual.
- Arborist report, survey and civil levels.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Review the site, existing trees and landscape controls, and set
the landscape targets.

- Site visit; review the arborist report, controls and targets for deep
  soil, landscaped area and canopy.

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Prepare the landscape concept for the schematic design.

- Landscape concept, deep soil and landscaped area calculations, and podium
  soil depth and load allowances.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Prepare the landscape plans the development application needs
and respond to council requests on them until determination.

- DA landscape plan, planting schedule, compliance calculations and
  public-domain concept where required.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the landscape conditions of consent and develop the
landscape design.

- Detailed landscape, irrigation, podium build-up and public-domain design.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Prepare landscape documentation for the construction certificate
and tender, and answer tender queries.

- Drawings, specification, planting schedule and maintenance period
  requirements.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Price, as a separate option, novation to the design and construct
contractor.

- Review of substitutions for plant stock and materials.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Inspect landscape works, certify completion and monitor the
establishment period.

- Plant stock inspection, set-out and soil inspections, practical completion
  certificate and end of establishment inspection.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Coordinate landscape with the civil, architectural and arborist
consultants throughout the appointment.

- Keep landscape compliance calculations current against the consent.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Lump sum fee by stage; public-domain scope included or excluded; number
  of inspections and establishment period visits.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Deep soil calculations lost as the basement grows.
- Podium soil loads not given to the structural engineer.
- Public-domain works and council approval missed.
- Flag low confidence when council has a public-domain manual not yet
  reviewed.
