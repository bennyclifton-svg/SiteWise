# Town Planning

## Purpose and professional boundary

The town planner advises on the planning controls and approval pathway,
prepares the planning report (in NSW, the statement of environmental effects
or, for larger proposals, an environmental impact statement), coordinates the
specialist reports the application needs, lodges and manages the application,
and advises on conditions and modifications. The planner does not design the
building or certify NCC compliance. Clerk frames the scope; it does not give
planning opinions on permissibility.

## Activation

Use this guide whenever the project needs a DA, a complying development
certificate that depends on planning controls, a State significant
development or infrastructure application, a modification, or a planning due
diligence report before purchase.

## Evidence sweep

- Planning certificate (section 10.7 of the Environmental Planning and
  Assessment Act 1979), zoning, LEP and DCP controls, and the SEPPs that
  apply.
- Site constraints: heritage, bushfire, flood, biodiversity, acid sulfate
  soils, contamination, easements and covenants.
- Previous approvals and conditions, and any pre-DA advice.
- The client's brief, yield targets and programme.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Carry out planning due diligence: controls, constraints,
permissibility, likely approval pathway and the specialist reports needed.

- Site visit and review of the planning certificate, controls, constraints
  and approval history.
- Advise on permissibility, the approval pathway (exempt, complying
  development, local DA, regionally significant or State significant) and
  the consent authority.
- List the specialist reports the application will need, with programme
  and cost implications.

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Test the schematic design against the planning controls and
advise on compliance, variations and approval risk.

- Controls compliance table (height, floor space ratio, setbacks, parking,
  landscaping) and identification of variations.
- Advice on Clause 4.6 variation requests and their risk.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Lead the pre-DA meeting, prepare the planning report and
lodgement package, lodge the application and manage it through assessment to
determination.

- Pre-DA meeting request, package and attendance; record the advice.
- Statement of environmental effects (or environmental impact statement),
  Clause 4.6 requests and coordination of specialist reports.
- Lodge on the NSW Planning Portal; manage notification, submissions,
  requests for information and panel meetings until determination.
- State whether the assessment stage is in the lump sum or at hourly rates.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the conditions of consent, advise on compliance and
prepare any modification or review application.

- Conditions review with the project team: prior-to-CC, prior-to-OC and
  ongoing conditions.
- Section 4.55 modification or section 8.2 review applications where needed.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Advise on consistency of the construction documentation with the
consent, if engaged.

- Review design changes that may need a modification before the
  construction certificate.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded; the planner normally stays with the client.

- Price modification applications driven by contractor changes separately.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Advise on condition compliance and modifications during
construction and before the occupation certificate, if engaged.

- Support the conditions compliance evidence the certifier needs.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Report the application status, risks and decisions required to
the client throughout the appointment.

- Keep the approvals tracker and the conditions register current.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Lump sum fee for due diligence, pre-DA and lodgement; assessment-stage fee
  basis; hourly rates for modifications and appeals.
- Specialist reports the planner coordinates versus those the client
  appoints.
- Land and Environment Court appeals excluded unless priced separately.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Specialist reports appointed late, delaying lodgement.
- Assessment-stage responses left out of the fee.
- Conditions review skipped, so prior-to-CC items surprise the programme.
- Flag low confidence when permissibility depends on an uncertain
  definition, a savings provision or a draft planning instrument.
