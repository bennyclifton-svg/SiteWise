# Access Consultant

## Purpose and professional boundary

The access consultant advises on compliance with the Disability (Access to
Premises — Buildings) Standards 2010, NCC Part D4 and the AS 1428 series, on
adaptable and livable housing requirements, and on access performance
solutions. The certifier decides compliance; the access consultant advises
and reports.

## Activation

Use this guide for Class 2 to 9 buildings, residential flat buildings with
adaptable or livable housing targets, and refurbishments that trigger access
upgrades to the affected part or the path of travel.

## Evidence sweep

- NCC Part D4, the Premises Standards, AS 1428.1, and the DCP adaptable or
  livable housing requirements (Apartment Design Guide for residential flat
  buildings).
- For existing buildings, an access audit of the path of travel.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Review the brief and the existing building, and identify the
access requirements and upgrade triggers.

- Existing building access audit where relevant; confirm the adaptable and
  livable housing targets.

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Review the schematic design for access compliance and advise on
design changes.

- Entry, lift, parking, sanitary facility and circulation advice.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Prepare the access report the development application needs and
respond to council requests on it until determination.

- DA access report with adaptable and livable housing compliance.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the developed design for access compliance and prepare any
access performance solutions.

- Design review against D4 and AS 1428; performance solution reports where
  departures are proposed.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Review the construction certificate documentation for access
compliance and issue the access compliance report.

- Construction certificate access report; answers to tender queries.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded; price contractor design review at hourly rates.

- Review substitutions affecting tactile indicators, hardware and
  sanitary fixtures.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Audit the completed works for access compliance before the
occupation certificate.

- Pre-completion and final access audits with a defects list.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Respond to design team access queries throughout the appointment.

- Keep performance solutions and their conditions in the register.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Lump sum fee by stage; number of design reviews and site audits;
  performance solutions priced separately.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Path-of-travel upgrades for refurbishments missed in cost and scope.
- Adaptable unit layouts compromised in design development.
- Final audit booked too late to fix defects before handover.
- Flag low confidence when a performance solution is proposed without the
  certifier's early agreement.
