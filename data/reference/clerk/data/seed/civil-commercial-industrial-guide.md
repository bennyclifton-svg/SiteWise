# Civil Engineering: Commercial and Industrial

## Purpose and professional boundary

The civil engineer designs stormwater, on-site detention and water quality,
bulk earthworks, pavements, internal roads and hardstands, and public-domain
works such as crossings, kerbs and road upgrades. Flood studies may sit with
the civil engineer or a specialist flood consultant; state which. Clerk frames
the scope and interfaces; it does not size pipes or judge flood levels.

## Activation

Use this guide for commercial, industrial, institutional and mixed-use sites,
subdivisions that support them, and any project with public road or drainage
works. Use `civil-residential.md` for single dwellings and small
multi-dwelling projects.

## Evidence sweep

- Detail survey, BYDA plans, council drainage and flood information, and the
  water authority's asset records.
- Geotechnical report (pavement subgrade, groundwater, acid sulfate soils).
- Planning controls: council DCP stormwater and water sensitive urban design
  requirements, flood planning level and flood-related development controls.
- Traffic report for vehicle types, swept paths and road upgrade needs.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Review the survey, flood and drainage information and the site,
and identify the civil constraints and investigations needed.

- Site visit; review the survey, flood mapping, downstream drainage
  capacity and existing easements.
- Identify investigations needed (flood study, CCTV of existing drainage,
  pavement testing).

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Prepare the civil concept for the schematic design: site
levels, stormwater strategy, flood response and vehicle pavements.

- Site grading and cut and fill balance; building floor levels against
  flood planning levels.
- Stormwater, on-site detention and water quality (MUSIC modelling where
  council requires it) strategy.
- Hardstand and pavement concept for the design vehicles.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Prepare the civil and stormwater documents the development
application needs and respond to council requests on them until
determination.

- Concept stormwater management plan, erosion and sediment control plan,
  and flood impact assessment where required.
- Civil input to the traffic report and public-domain drawings.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the civil conditions of consent and develop the civil
design, coordinated with the consultant team.

- Detailed stormwater, on-site detention, water quality, earthworks and
  pavement design.
- Roads Act 1993 section 138 and council public-domain approvals for works in
  the road reserve; water authority approvals for drainage connections.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Prepare civil documentation for the construction certificate
and tender, and answer tender queries.

- Civil drawings, specification and quantities; respond to tenderer RFIs and
  review civil tenders.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Price, as a separate option, novation to the design and
construct contractor and the post-contract civil design.

- Novation terms and fee basis; review of contractor-proposed changes to
  levels, pavements and drainage.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Inspect civil works at hold points and provide the
certification and works-as-executed information council and the certifier
need.

- Inspect subgrade, drainage before backfill, on-site detention and pavement
  layers.
- Works-as-executed review, on-site detention certification, positive
  covenants, and council handover of public assets.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Attend design meetings and coordinate civil levels and drainage
with the architect, structural and landscape consultants throughout the
appointment.

- Keep council and authority approvals in the approvals tracker.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Lump sum fee by stage, hourly rates and a flood-study allowance or
  exclusion.
- Number of site inspections included; public-domain approvals included or
  excluded.
- Stated inputs relied on (survey, geotechnical, traffic).

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Floor levels fixed before flood levels are confirmed.
- Downstream drainage has no capacity and needs an easement or upgrade.
- Public-domain works and council bonds missing from the cost plan.
- Pavement design uses the wrong design vehicle.
- Flag low confidence when flood mapping is out of date or the site is on
  mapped acid sulfate soils.
