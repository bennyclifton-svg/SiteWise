# Arborist

## Purpose and professional boundary

The consulting arborist (typically AQF Level 5) assesses trees on and near
the site, reports the impact of the development under AS 4970 (protection
of trees on development sites), and specifies and supervises tree protection.
Tree removal or pruning needs council approval unless exempt; the arborist
advises but does not approve.

## Activation

Use this guide when trees on the site, the road reserve or adjoining land
could be affected by demolition, excavation, services, access or
construction.

## Evidence sweep

- Survey with tree locations, trunk diameters and canopy spreads.
- Council tree controls (DCP and the Biodiversity and Conservation SEPP
  vegetation provisions), significant tree registers and heritage listings.
- Proposed building footprint, services routes and excavation.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Assess the trees on and near the site and report their health,
retention value and protection zones.

- Tree assessment with species, health, structure, retention value, tree
  protection zone and structural root zone (AS 4970).

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Advise on the design moves needed to retain priority trees.

- Encroachment advice for footprint, basements, driveways and services.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Prepare the arboricultural impact assessment the development
application needs and respond to council requests on it until determination.

- Arboricultural impact assessment with encroachment calculations and
  recommendations for removal, retention and protection.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the tree conditions of consent and prepare the tree
protection plan and specification.

- Tree protection plan, root investigation where encroachment is major,
  and specification for protection fencing and ground protection.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Provide tree protection requirements and hold points for the
tender documents.

- Tree protection specification and hold points in the tender documents.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded; the arborist normally stays with the client
as project arborist.

- Record the project arborist role in the contract.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Act as project arborist: certify protection before works,
supervise work within protection zones and certify tree condition at
completion.

- Certify protection installation, supervise excavation and pruning within
  protection zones, and provide the completion certificate council requires.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Respond to design team queries on trees throughout the
appointment.

- Keep the tree register and certificates current.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Number of trees covered; report types included; rate per supervision
  visit and number allowed.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Street and neighbouring trees missed.
- Services trenching through protection zones not coordinated.
- Project arborist certification missed, delaying the occupation
  certificate.
- Flag low confidence when trees on adjoining land cannot be inspected.
