# Traffic and Transport

## Purpose and professional boundary

The traffic consultant assesses parking demand and supply, vehicle access,
loading, swept paths and the traffic impact of the development, and prepares
construction traffic management advice. Road and pavement design sits with
the civil engineer. Clerk frames the scope; it does not judge traffic
capacity.

## Activation

Use this guide when the project changes parking, access or loading, needs a
traffic impact assessment for a DA, uses a classified road, or needs a
construction traffic management plan.

## Evidence sweep

- DCP parking rates, the Transport for NSW guide to traffic generating
  developments, and AS/NZS 2890 parking standards.
- Site access, classified road status and Transport for NSW concurrence
  triggers.
- Existing traffic counts, public transport and active transport.
- Loading, waste collection and emergency vehicle needs.

## Services by stage

Each stage opens with an **RFP line** that code can render as the lead of a
grouped RFP item. Stage ids match `architectural-services-guide.md`. Use only
the stages inside the appointment boundary; price the rest separately or
leave them out.

### Inception, site and existing information [inception]

**RFP line:** Review the site access, parking controls and traffic context,
and identify the traffic constraints and surveys needed.

- Site visit; review road hierarchy, sight lines, existing driveways and
  public transport.
- Identify surveys needed (traffic counts, parking occupancy).

### Concept and schematic design [concept]

**RFP line:** Advise on parking numbers, access location, loading and
vehicle movements for the schematic design.

- Parking provision against DCP rates; access and ramp location; loading
  dock and waste vehicle strategy.
- Preliminary swept paths for the design vehicles.

### Pre-DA consultation and development application [development_application]

**RFP line:** Prepare the traffic and parking assessment the development
application needs and respond to council and Transport for NSW requests on it
until determination.

- Traffic and parking impact assessment with swept paths and AS/NZS 2890
  compliance.
- Green travel plan where required; responses to council and Transport for
  NSW.

### Conditions of consent and design development [design_development]

**RFP line:** Review the traffic conditions of consent and check the developed
design against them and AS/NZS 2890.

- Car park, ramp, loading and signage design review; Local Traffic Committee
  submissions for line marking or signage in the road reserve.

### Documentation and tender [documentation_tender]

**RFP line:** Certify the car park and access design for the construction
certificate, and advise on construction traffic requirements for tender.

- AS/NZS 2890 compliance certificate for the construction certificate.
- Construction traffic management principles for the tender documents.

### Novation and post-contract design [post_contract_design]

**RFP line:** Usually excluded; price contractor design support at hourly
rates.

- Review contractor changes to ramps, parking or loading.

### Construction and completion [construction_completion]

**RFP line:** Review the construction traffic management plan and certify the
completed car park and access before the occupation certificate, if engaged.

- Construction traffic management plan review and road occupancy
  coordination.
- Final inspection and compliance certificate for the car park.

### Throughout the appointment [coordination]

**RFP line:** Respond to design team queries on parking, access and loading
throughout the appointment.

- Keep council and Transport for NSW correspondence in the approvals tracker.

## Consultant RFP minimum returnables

- Lump sum fee by stage; survey costs included or excluded; hourly rates.
- Transport for NSW and Local Traffic Committee processes included or
  excluded.

## Common failures and low-confidence flags

- Waste and emergency vehicle swept paths not tested.
- Ramp grades and headroom fixed late, forcing redesign.
- Construction traffic management left entirely to the builder.
- Flag low confidence when the site is on a classified road or near a
  signalised intersection.
