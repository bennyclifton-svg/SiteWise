Cancellations and Refunds

Version 2026-11-07 · Last updated 7 November 2026

In short. Cancel any time from your Account page. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for and are not charged again. Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of being charged. Monthly plans are not refunded for part-months. If SiteWise fails you, the Australian Consumer Law protects you whatever this page says.

1. How to cancel

Open Account → Manage billing and choose Cancel plan. Only the account owner can do this. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Until then you keep everything your plan includes, and you will not be charged again.

After the period ends, your account moves to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted. If you have more projects, documents or storage than the Free plan allows, everything stays readable, but new uploads and projects are paused until you are within the Free limits or choose a plan again.

2. Monthly plans

Monthly fees are not refunded for part of a month, except where section 5 applies.

3. Yearly plans

If you cancel within 14 days of being charged for a yearly plan, whether it is your first payment or a renewal, we will refund that payment in full. Email us to ask (see section 7). We will remind you by email before a yearly plan renews.

After 14 days, yearly fees are not refunded for the unused part of the year, except where section 5 applies.

4. Changing plans

When you upgrade, the new plan starts straight away and Stripe credits the unused part of your old plan against the new price. When you downgrade, the change takes effect at the end of your current period.

5. When we always refund

We refund the unused part of fees you have paid in advance if:

  • we close your account without cause (Terms of Service, section 10.2);
  • we change a paid plan or these terms in a way that materially disadvantages you, and you cancel because of it (Terms of Service, sections 9.2 and 16); or
  • you are charged in error, for example twice for the same period.

6. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. For major failures with the service, you are entitled to cancel your service contract with us and to a refund for the unused portion, or to compensation for its reduced value. You are also entitled to be compensated for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. If the failure does not amount to a major failure, you are entitled to have problems with the service rectified in a reasonable time and, if this is not done, to cancel your contract and obtain a refund for the unused portion of the contract.

Nothing on this page limits those rights.

7. How to ask for a refund

Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from your account's email address, or include it in your message. We reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe and usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days.

If something is wrong with a charge, please contact us before disputing it with your bank. We can usually fix it faster.
