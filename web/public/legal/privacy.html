2. What we collect

Account information

Your email address, a scrambled (hashed) form of your password, your role in your account, when you accepted our terms, and whether you have confirmed your email. We never store your password itself.

Billing information

If you buy a paid plan, Stripe collects your name or business name, billing address, ABN or other tax number if you give one, and payment details. We receive and keep your plan, billing status, renewal dates and Stripe's customer reference. We do not receive or store full card or bank account numbers.

Project content

The documents and data you or your team upload. These often contain personal information about other people, for example names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and signatures of clients, consultants, contractors and authorities. We hold this information on your behalf to provide the Service to you.

Technical and usage information

When you use SiteWise, our servers record your IP address, browser type, the pages and actions requested, and the time. We also count documents read, storage used and projects created for each account, to apply plan limits and to understand how the Service is used.

Communications

Messages you send us, and records of the account emails we send you.

3. How we collect it

Mostly directly from you when you sign up, upload documents or contact us. We also receive information from Stripe about your payments, and from an account owner who invites you to join their team (your email address).

If you upload a document that contains someone else's personal information, you are responsible for having the right to share it with us for this purpose (see the Terms of Service, section 5.3).

4. How we use it

  • To create and run your account, sign you in and keep it secure.
  • To store, read, organise and show your project documents and data, which is the Service.
  • To bill you, send tax invoices and apply your plan's limits.
  • To send you service emails: confirming your address, password resets, team invitations, billing notices and important changes. You cannot opt out of these while you have an account, because they are needed to provide it.
  • To give support, fix problems, prevent misuse and meet our legal obligations.
  • To understand how the Service is used and improve it, using counts and summaries rather than the content of your documents.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your content to train artificial intelligence models. We only send marketing emails if you have agreed to receive them, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, as the Spam Act 2003 requires.

5. Cookies and browser storage

SiteWise sets one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary and lasts up to 14 days or until you sign out. Your browser also stores a few display preferences on your device (light or dark theme, panel widths and similar). We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and our pages load no third-party scripts.

Stripe's payment pages, which you visit when you subscribe or manage billing, are run by Stripe and covered by Stripe's privacy policy.

8. How we protect it

  • All connections to SiteWise are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes.
  • Each account's data is kept separate, and every request is checked against the account it belongs to.
  • The database accepts connections only from the server itself.
  • Backups are encrypted before they leave the server.
  • Access to servers is limited to the people who run SiteWise and protected by key-based sign-in.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify affected people and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.
