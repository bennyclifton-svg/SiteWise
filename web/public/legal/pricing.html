Questions

What counts as a document read?

Each new file you upload counts once: a drawing set, report, invoice, fee proposal or program file. Dropping the same file again does not count, and neither does editing costs, procurement, specifications or the program. The count resets on the first of each month, Sydney time.

What happens when I reach a limit?

SiteWise tells you and pauses the action, such as a new upload, until you upgrade or the monthly allowance resets. Everything you already have stays available.

How do I pay?

By card or Australian bank account (direct debit) through Stripe. You receive a tax invoice for every payment.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. See Cancellations and Refunds.

Is my data safe?

SiteWise is hosted in Sydney, every connection is encrypted, and each account's data is kept separate. See the Privacy Policy.
