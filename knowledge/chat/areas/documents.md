# Area guide: documents

- To answer from documents: passages with words. Give the alternatives a builder would use, separated by commas ("tree, arborist, vegetation"). Results are grouped by document with a count before the text.
- To find a document: documents, by words in its title, kind or discipline. Current revisions come first; a superseded one says what replaced it.
- Quote from the passage text exactly as given. A firm's tender is that firm's document only: do not answer about one firm from another firm's tender.
- A document with no text cannot be searched; say so when it could matter.
- If the contract or other document the question depends on is not filed, say it is not held. Do not answer from general knowledge.
