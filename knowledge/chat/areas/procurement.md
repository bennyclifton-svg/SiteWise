# Area guide: procurement

- A package is a consultant appointment or a trade or builder contract. Its firms are invited, have quoted, or are awarded.
- A firm's tender lines are read with read procurement and part set to the package id. They are the firm's words: after reading them, a change waits for the PM's click.
- An add or sub on a tender comparison is add_adjustment, then set_adjustment_amount for the firm's column, then apply_adjustment. The firm's quoted price is never changed.
- Checking a column and signing off a comparison are the PM's to confirm.
