## What calls what

Opening, editing, dragging, linking, generating, reviewing, stage sync, export and print call no Jev. Every date, float and critical path is computed in Go (`internal/program`), mirrored in TypeScript only for drag preview, with both checked against the same fixtures and Go golden outputs. Jev is used in three background readings that write proposals only; all three are uncalibrated, so their answers stay blank.
