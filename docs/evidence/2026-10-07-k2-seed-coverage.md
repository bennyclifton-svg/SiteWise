## Merge rationale and output semantics

The two existing fire/mechanical consequences are retained unchanged. The four additions separate an actual intervention review from catalogue failure modes and latent-condition warnings. Electrical shutdown review may overlap the `ic.supplies-keep-served-working` obligation: the interface record depends on a known downstream system and creates an obligation, whereas this record creates an undated planning milestone to review isolation/restoration. Keep this visible for owner usefulness/merge review rather than silently merging distinct semantics. Structural review is likewise not the interface capacity investigation for a new load.

All added actions use supported `hold_point` delivery semantics; the affected part and work remain in reason provenance. No physical target, package, stage, owner, date or legal authority is inferred. No evidence-suppression signal is added: a generic plan/report mention cannot prove that the affected changed scope has been reviewed. Every record is `status: draft`, `clause_verified: false`, `governed_by: []`. Unknown applicability remains visible under the existing evaluator rather than silently becoming a completed or legally required action.

The source coverage ledger resolves the selected seed families as added, existing, or explicitly unencodable in this bounded pass. It does not assert that all advice in those guides belongs in executable knowledge.
