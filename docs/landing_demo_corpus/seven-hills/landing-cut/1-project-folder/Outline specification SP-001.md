# Outline specification

Axis Studio Pty Ltd

Contact: Mei Chen, Director

Project: 11 townhouses and Torrens subdivision, 14–18 Wianamatta Avenue, Seven Hills NSW 2147
Client: Wianamatta Developments Pty Ltd

This outline specification is read with the drawings. It lists the work in each section for all 11 townhouses.

## 01 Demolition

Demolish the three cottages, sheds, slabs and paving. Remove asbestos cement sheeting by a licensed removalist under the hazardous materials survey.

## 02 Earthworks

Strip topsoil, remove uncontrolled fill under the buildings and tank, and trim to the levels on C-100.

## 03 Piers and footings

Bored concrete piers to the engineer's schedule, founded in stiff clay or shale.

## 04 Ground slabs

Reinforced concrete stiffened raft slabs, 32 MPa, on a 0.2 mm vapour barrier, with termite collars to penetrations.

## 05 Wall framing

Prefabricated MGP10 timber wall frames, H2 termite treated. Timber I-joist upper floor with 19 mm particleboard flooring.

## 06 Roof framing

Prefabricated timber roof trusses at 600 mm centres.

## 07 Separating walls

Twin timber stud fire and sound rated separating walls, FRL 60/60/60, carried to the underside of the roof sheeting.

## 08 Masonry

Face brick veneer to the lower storey, 230 by 110 by 76 mm, with stainless steel ties.

## 09 Cladding

Fibre cement weatherboard cladding to the upper storey on a ventilated cavity, paint finish.

## 10 Roofing

Prefinished corrugated steel roof sheeting at 5 degrees, with matching gutters, downpipes and flashings, over R1.3 anticon blanket.

## 11 Windows and external doors

Powdercoated aluminium framed double glazed windows and sliding doors. Solid core entry doors. Panel lift garage doors with motors.

## 12 Insulation

R5.0 ceiling batts and R2.5 wall batts.

## 13 Waterproofing

Liquid applied membranes to bathrooms, ensuites, laundries and balconies, to AS 3740 and AS 4654.

## 14 Linings

10 mm plasterboard to walls and ceilings, water resistant board to wet areas, 90 mm cove cornice.

## 15 Joinery

Laminate kitchens with 20 mm engineered stone benchtops, vanities, built-in wardrobes and laundry cupboards.

## 16 Floor finishes

Porcelain tiles to the living areas and wet areas. Carpet on underlay to bedrooms and stairs. Sealed concrete to garages.

## 17 Painting

Three coat low-VOC acrylic paint system to walls, ceilings and trim.

## 18 Hydraulic services

Sanitary plumbing and drainage, cold and hot water, a 3,000 L rainwater tank with pump to each dwelling, and a 315 L heat pump hot water unit. No gas.

## 19 Electrical services

A three phase supply and meter to each dwelling, switchboards, LED lighting, power, hard wired smoke alarms, a 5.0 kW photovoltaic system with inverter, and a 32 A circuit for a future electric vehicle charger.

## 20 Communications

NBN fibre to each dwelling, with a home distributor and data points to living areas and bedrooms. Video intercom to each entry.

## 21 Mechanical services

Ducted reverse cycle air conditioning with day and night zones. Ducted exhaust to bathrooms, laundries and rangehoods.

## 22 Appliances

Induction cooktop, electric oven, rangehood and dishwasher to each kitchen.

## 23 Stormwater

Pits, pipes, trench drains and a 120 m³ below-ground reinforced concrete on-site detention tank.

## 24 Driveway and paving

Reinforced concrete common driveway with a broom finish, exposed aggregate crossing and paved courtyards.

## 25 Retaining walls and fencing

Concrete sleeper retaining walls to 1.2 m. 1.8 m lapped and capped timber boundary fences and powdercoated aluminium front fences.

## 26 Landscaping

Turf, mass planting, 18 trees, garden edging, drip irrigation and letterboxes.
