General notes

Read these before you rely on a number.

  1. Every value states its source.Read from a document, typical for the building type, set by you, or waiting for your decision.
    DocumentsTypicalYouTo check
  2. Your word is final.A later upload can flag a value for review. It never overwrites what you set.
  3. Unknown is a value.An unpriced line, an unstated specification and a missing date stay unknown. Nothing is filled in to look complete.
  4. An issue freezes the record.Issued specifications and frozen cost baselines stay exactly as they were issued.
  5. One reader, no chatbot.A single model reads where a document must be read. Dates, money and arithmetic are worked out in code.
