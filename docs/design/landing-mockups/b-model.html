1

Files your documents in about a second.

Drag in the drawings, reports, quotes and invoices. SiteWise reads the number, revision, title and discipline off each one and puts it in the register. Drop a new revision and the old one is marked superseded.

2

Builds your project profile.

It reads the whole set and writes the job up on one page: what's being built, the site and planning, the client, the budget, and the scope of works, system by system.

3

Builds your cost plan.

It works out the lines your job needs: authority fees, each consultant, construction and contingency. Award a consultant and their fee drops in by stage. Drop in an invoice and claimed to date updates.

4

Builds your program.

Stages and activities from approvals through to completion, laid out as a Gantt chart. Drag a bar or change a duration and every date after it moves. Export to Excel or print it A3.

5

Sets up and evaluates your tenders.

It works out which consultants and trades the job needs and writes the RFP for each. When the quotes come back, drop them on the row. SiteWise lines the prices up stage by stage so you can compare and award.
