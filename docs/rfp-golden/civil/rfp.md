# RFP: Civil engineering services (draft v1)

Built from `ledger.md` (Part 1 Arthur St warehouse; Part 2 Camp Yarramundi).
`[ ]` = from profile · `{IF …}` = conditional · `(#n)` = ledger rows
(review only). NSW only. Separate from the structural RFP.

The price schedule follows the 2024 Yarramundi RFT (stage × element), the
only golden structure all four tenderers could use (Part 2). The RFT left
meetings, inspections, OSD, flood and intersection approvals open, and
prices spread 2.8x; this draft fixes them in sections 4 and 5.

Scale rule for section 5: **complex** when the site area is over [2] ha,
or there are works in a public road or an intersection, or a flood flag,
or a drinking water catchment (NorBE), or the use is vulnerable (Class 9,
3 with overnight stay). Otherwise **simple**.

### 2. Your role

Civil engineer for work outside building footprints: earthworks, roads and
pavements, levels, stormwater and water quality. The hydraulic engineer
designs roof drainage, downpipes and pumped systems; utility designers do
water, sewer, power and comms; you combine their layouts with yours (#42,
#80, #105, #134). The traffic engineer sets parking numbers, design
vehicles and intersection layout; you design to them (#94-97).
