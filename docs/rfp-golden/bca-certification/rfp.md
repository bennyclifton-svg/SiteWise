## 7. Evaluation

First assess ability to perform the proposed role, independence, completeness of scope, registrations, insurance and material contract departures. Then evaluate **technical coverage and method 35%, named team/independence 25%, achievable program 15%, evaluated fee 25%**. These weights are proposed client choices, not statutory rules. Compare on the same stage/allowance/option basis and consider disbursements and contingency exposure. Approval guarantees are not an advantage. The client may seek clarification and is not obliged to accept the lowest fee. (#43-44,55)
