Source: `Golden Consultant RFP\Traffic`. All three answer the Arthur St
warehouse extension (Homebush West NSW, Strathfield DCP) in September 2023.

| V | Varga Traffic Planning | 3 | $11,182 ($12,300 incl. GST: report $5,900, counts $3,200, SIDRA $3,200) | fixed fee to report production; after that time-charge |

Note: B describes the extension as 2,895 m² warehouse; the other
disciplines' proposals say 2,135 m². The RFP's project table is where the
consultants take quantities from; it should state them once.

Key: I included · X excluded · H hourly · O optional.

## Stages

None of the three firms staged the work. All priced one DA traffic report;
V split it into report, counts and modelling. Everything after the report
(council responses, meetings) is hourly.
