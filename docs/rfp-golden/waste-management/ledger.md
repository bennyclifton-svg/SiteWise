## Conflicts, gaps and owner decisions

A combined plan, B operational-only, E separately selected reports/equipment drawings and L separately priced reports are not comparable whole-project offers. None proves executed acceptance, current insurance, approved collection arrangements or quantities for another project. B's unbounded later support must become a finite priced appointment; A/E revision allowances must identify what a round means; L's included clarification versus charged changed scope must be stated. Reports do not establish that contractors have committed to facilities, collection vehicle, frequency, pricing or access. No full detailed equipment/services design is implied by strategy advice.

Before issue resolve project facts, local approval/control basis, waste streams and evidence, demolition/remediation extent, operator and collection confirmation, room/routes/vehicle feasibility, quantities, ownership/tenancy rights, appointment end, revision/meeting/site allowances, specialist responsibilities, targets/verification, operating-cost advice, agreement, deliverable use/reliance and insurance. No statutory or technical rate has been verified from an instrument in this preparation.
