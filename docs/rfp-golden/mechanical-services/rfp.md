## 2. Your role

Design and coordinate the selected mechanical systems to the agreed maturity within the appointment boundary. Establish the design basis, perform and retain relevant load/airflow/pressure and equipment-selection calculations, and provide coordinated drawings, specifications, schedules and testable controls requirements. Distinguish conditioned air, outdoor air, exhaust and make-up air; a room AC unit alone does not establish adequate ventilation. Identify incomplete evidence, dependencies and required decisions before relying on them. (#2-7,12-18,23-25,40-43,48)

Fire engineering defines the accepted strategy and performance-solution requirements through a **separate appointment**. Fire services separately covers appointed wet/dry systems. Mechanical implements selected smoke exhaust, pressurisation, shutdown and damper functions under that strategy; identify the cause-and-effect author, controls programmer, power provider, test performer and witness. Mechanical drawings do not establish a new fire strategy. (#2,12,23,29,42,49)

Contractors supply/install, prepare assigned fabrication/shop and as-built information, and execute commissioning; your design, review, inspection and witnessing roles must be expressly selected. Review does not transfer responsibility for another designer's work or provide continuous supervision or certification of unseen work. Whole-building energy assessment/rating certification, structural design, acoustic assessment, utility/ASP3 and AV are separate appointments unless specifically added. Provide the mechanical energy calculations, plant efficiency/controls requirements and inputs those specialists need. (#4-7,16-18,24,29-31,43,48-49)
