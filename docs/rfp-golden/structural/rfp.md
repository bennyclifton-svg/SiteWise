# RFP: Structural engineering services (draft v1)

Built from `ledger.md`. `[ ]` = from profile · `{IF …}` = conditional ·
`(#n)` = ledger rows (review only). NSW only. Structural and civil are
separate RFPs (owner decision 2026-10-09).

Evidence limit: one warehouse extension (B, N, and the PM's normalised
comparison E). No golden proposal priced construction phase services or
regulated design (DBP Act); those stages are marked "practice".

Scale rule for section 5: building class first (Class 1, 7, 8, 10 simple;
Class 2-6, 9 complex), raised to complex for a budget over $20M, a
heritage flag, transfer structures, basements, or building over or next to
an authority asset (Sydney Water, rail).

### 2. Your role

Structural engineer, engaged by the client, coordinated by the architect
(#62). You design and document the permanent structure. Where elements are
better designed by a specialist contractor (piles, post-tensioned slabs,
specialist floors), you write the performance documentation and the
contractor designs and certifies them (#41-43).

### 3. Scope by stage

Price each stage that applies as a lump sum. Each stage ends at client
sign-off.

| Stage | Deliverable | Shown when |

| Planning approval | Structural design statement for the application; responses to authority requests during assessment, up to the cap in section 4 (#16, #20) | Pathway not exempt |
