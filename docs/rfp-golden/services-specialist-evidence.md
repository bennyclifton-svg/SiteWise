# Specialist scope found in the combined services proposals

Date: 10 October 2026. Status: draft; owner review required. This companion preserves meaningful priced specialist services from the combined PDFs without silently adding them to mechanical, electrical, hydraulic or fire-services appointments. It is source evidence, not an issued specialist RFP or an instruction to purchase every service. Historical technical references remain `verified: false`; fees are not current benchmarks.

Source identities, hashes and page counts: [inventory](source-inventory-2026-10-10.md). Physical PDF page locators below. All five combined-services PDFs were reviewed; complete page coverage is recorded in the [fire-services ledger](fire-services/ledger.md). Abbreviations: **A** `Services/ADP 03.pdf` (Camp, 2024); **D** `Services/Fee Multi Services (ADP).pdf` (Arthur Street, 2023); **B** `Services/BSE 02.pdf` (Camp, 2024); **F** `Services/Fee Multi Services (BSE).pdf` (Arthur Street, 2023); **C** `Services/Fee Multi Services (COVA).pdf` (Arthur Street, 2023). Camp and Arthur Street are different projects. Optional offers and blank acceptance forms do not establish an appointment.

## Existing disciplines and unpriced references

A also prices structural services ($16,000 + $38,000 + $34,000 = $88,000 ex GST) with camp-specific modular cabins, conference facility and existing-hall load interfaces (pp15,23-24,32-33). The existing structural golden folder was expressly outside this preparation's change scope; this source is not silently imported into it. D's acoustic/structural/WSC headings, broken contents links, staff biographies and exclusion pages do not establish positive priced appointments. Specialist capability and unrelated case studies were discarded.

If these specialist services are commissioned separately, prepare a dedicated project-specific scope using this evidence, relevant seeds and current requirements. None is included merely by selecting a core M/E/H/fire discipline. All source commercial terms and package-price dependencies still require reconciliation; standalone prices cannot be assumed from bundled offers.
