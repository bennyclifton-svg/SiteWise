## 2. Your role

Provide hydraulic advice, investigation and coordinated design for the selected systems, uses and stages. Establish the evidence and design basis; calculate demand/capacity, identify constraints, coordinate connections and plant space, and deliver the specified drawings, calculations, specifications, schedules and scoped statements. Flag inadequate information and obtain an agreed basis before reliance. (#2-9,14-22,27-29,40-49)

**Fire services is a separate appointment**, covering sprinklers, hydrants, hose reels, fire pumps, fire tanks and associated fire system design; fire engineering separately addresses strategy and performance solutions. This hydraulic appointment coordinates water-source, refill, backflow, space and shared infrastructure interfaces without absorbing wet-fire design. Dry-fire ownership remains assigned between fire services and electrical. (#4,16,35,51)

Separate domestic-water storage/boosting from fire storage/pumping; roof/downpipe drainage from civil site drainage; connections to treatment from treatment/process design; and consultant review/witnessing from contractor installation and testing. Appoint competent specialists for treatment, medical/laboratory/process fluids or gases when triggered. Authority decisions, installation certification and permission to operate remain with the named parties. (#2-4,8,14-17,27-28,40-43,48-51)
