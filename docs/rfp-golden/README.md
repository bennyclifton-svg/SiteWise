# Golden consultant RFP library

The new discipline pairs below are **drafts for owner review**, prepared from the supplied consultant fee proposals and relevant seed guidance. Each ledger preserves source evidence, conflicts and gaps; each RFP synthesises a reusable appointment with selectable services, stages and quantified allowances. All 13 are now encoded in SiteWise as draft templates, alongside the original five. They have not been issued to consultants.

The RFPs support different building uses and new works, fit-outs, refurbishment, extensions, remediation and advisory work through actual project facts. Selecting a discipline does not purchase every listed service. Complete the project fields, base scope, options, quantities, interfaces and agreement before issue.

**Fire services and fire engineering are separate disciplines.** Fire services covers selected fire-system design, including sprinklers, pumps and tanks. Fire engineering covers strategy, performance solutions and analysis. Allocate each dry-fire system between fire services and electrical, including controls, programming, testing and certification interfaces. Mechanical smoke-control design implements the agreed fire strategy under its own defined scope.

Mechanical, electrical and hydraulic services are separate drafts using shared combined-services evidence. The [specialist evidence companion](services-specialist-evidence.md) preserves meaningful ESD/energy, electrical utility/ASP3 and AV offers without silently adding them to these appointments.

Historical proposals show offered scope, not necessarily accepted or performed work. Prices and insurance are historical observations. Seeds extend coverage but do not establish project facts or current law. Technical and regulatory identifiers from unverified sources remain `verified: false`. Proposed procurement terms are identified separately from source positions. Owner review and current project-specific verification remain necessary before issue.
