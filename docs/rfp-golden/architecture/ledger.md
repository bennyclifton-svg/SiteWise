## Consultants the architect expects others to provide (A p5)

DA stage: town planner (SEE, lodgement, council liaison), QS (min 2 estimates),
landscape (DA plan), structural (existing buildings review, sketch, design
statement), civil (stormwater plan, flood report TBC), electrical, mechanical,
hydraulic and wet fire, access, BCA, ESD (BASIX, Section J), acoustic,
traffic (TIA), bushfire, ecology (BDAR TBC), waste, geotech/contamination,
hazardous materials.
DD/tender/CC stage: landscape, structural, civil, electrical and dry fire,
mechanical, hydraulic and wet fire, ESD, access, BCA, PCA, fire engineer,
acoustic, QS, façade engineer, kitchen consultant, bushfire.
