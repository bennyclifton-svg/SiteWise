## 2. Your role

Design and coordinate the selected **fire services systems** within the appointment boundary. Establish their evidence-based design basis, calculations, layouts, equipment requirements, interfaces and verification requirements at the selected maturity. Report missing evidence and approvals before relying on assumptions. A concept tank/pump allowance is not a completed hydraulic design or proof of the existing network's adequacy. (#1-4,8-10,16-18,25-26,40-44)

Fire engineering strategy, performance solutions and modelling are a **separate appointment**. The BCA adviser/certifier establishes their appointed compliance assessment/decision functions; authorities decide their own approvals. Incorporate accepted strategy requirements and refer departures or changes back to those responsible. Contractor installation, shop/fabrication design, testing and certification are separately assigned: consultant review or witnessing does not execute the tests, continuously supervise work or certify unseen installation. Authority network design/applications and connection works require named appointments. (#3,9-12,17-20,25-29,41,43-44,52)
