## 2. Your role

Investigate and interpret ground conditions for the stated decision and provide the appointed geotechnical advice and verification. Start at **[written instruction and input/access milestone]** and finish at **[named final deliverable, monitoring endpoint or construction close-out]**. Identify whether preliminary reports, later laboratory results and monitoring updates sit inside that boundary. Price applicable later services separately. (#1,9,34,45-46,51)

Structural/civil engineers design the foundations, slabs, retention, pavements and drainage using the agreed parameters; identify any separately commissioned specialist geotechnical design. Review does not transfer another designer's responsibility. Contractor means/methods, temporary-works design and general site supervision remain with named parties. Environmental assessment, waste classification, remediation/validation and audit are separate roles, even where one firm offers both services or sampling shares a mobilisation. (#15,24-28,42,45,50)

For advice-only or defect investigation, name the decision, evidence limits and intended reliance. Distinguish observations, tests and interpretations; advise what further evidence would change the conclusion. Do not represent preliminary ground advice as complete design, unconditional site suitability or proof of causation. (#48-49)

## 7. Evaluation

Check completeness, competent resources, adequate method/coverage, insurance and material agreement departures first. Proposed weights: **evaluated price 40%, relevant experience 20%, named team 20%, method/program 10%, compliance 10%**. These are client choices. Normalise quantities, reinstatement, monitoring, environmental scope, laboratory costs, add-ons and options before comparing price. The client may seek clarifications and need not accept the lowest offer. (#29-32,41,54)
