# Town planning ledger

Source: `Golden Consultant RFP\Town Planning`. All three proposals answer the
same Engine Room VM RFP (August 2023) for the Hale Capital warehouse
extension at 145-151 Arthur St, Homebush West NSW (Strathfield LGA, E4
General Industrial, FSR 1:1, height 12 m, 2,135 m² extension with mezzanine
office over an existing OSD tank, near a Sydney Water sewer asset; DA
pathway confirmed by an earlier Mecone due diligence report).

**Finding about the source RFP.** Willowtree's "understanding" (W p3-4)
repeats the RFP's project phases word for word, and they are architectural
phases: schematic design for DA, detailed design, respond to tenderer RFIs.
Willowtree then priced "Detailed design: N/A". The 2023 RFP sent the same
architect-shaped stage list to every discipline. A planner's work has its
own stages. This is the main reason each discipline gets its own stage list.

Key: I included · X excluded · H hourly · O optional · C capped / allowance.
