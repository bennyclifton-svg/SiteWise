# RFP: Town planning services (draft v1)

Built from `ledger.md`. `[ ]` = from profile · `{IF …}` = conditional ·
`(#n)` = ledger rows (review only; not printed).

Scope: NSW only for now (owner decision 2026-10-09).

Evidence limit: all three golden proposals are for one NSW industrial DA.
Stages for SSD, REF, CDC, modifications and appeals come from general NSW
practice, not the golden set, and are marked "practice" below. Thresholds
are unverified until checked against the instrument.

Scale rule for section 5 (planning is driven by pathway and risk, not by
building class): **complex** when the pathway is SSD, or the application
goes to a planning panel, or a development standard is varied (cl 4.6), or
the site has a heritage flag, or notification is likely to draw objections
[user toggle]. Otherwise **simple**.

### 2. Your role

Planning consultant for the applicant. You prepare the planning report,
collate and lodge the application, and manage it through assessment. The
architect leads design; specialist consultants are engaged by the client
(#59). You review their plans and reports for planning compliance before
lodgement (#58), and you help scope specialist reports the authority
requires.

Price each stage that applies as a lump sum unless marked hourly.
