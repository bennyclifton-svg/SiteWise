# RFP scope of services opens with the consultant's purpose

Owner direction, 7 November 2026, after reviewing the STO2 apartments
architect RFP. Lane B (wording and layout); no Jev, no new budget path.

## Problem

Section 2 of every consultant RFP opened with contract mechanics. The
architect's read "Lead design consultant: you coordinate the consultants…
You are not responsible for the accuracy of their work", then listed twelve
consultants each marked "(to be appointed)", then the novation option. It
never said what the job is. Most other disciplines opened with compressed
notes ("Design selected systems to purchased maturity…").

## Decision

Every discipline's role reads in the same order:

1. **Purpose.** One or two sentences: who you are and what outcome you are
   engaged to achieve.
2. **Duties.** "To do this you:" and three or four plain lines on how.
   The same on every project.
3. **Fit for purpose by building type.** One sentence chosen by the
   project's facts (NCC class, house module, existing building, heritage,
   flood or basement flags). Nothing prints while the class is unknown.
   Sentences that name an instrument (Apartment Design Guide, the Design
   and Building Practitioners Act) are NSW-only and carry a `practice`
   provenance marked as not verified against the instrument.
4. **Boundary.** Who else owns what, and what the advice does not
   guarantee. Kept from the old wording, rewritten in plain English.

Architecture and town planning set `design_team: true`. The other
consultants print as a **Design team** table (consultant, engaged by,
status) under the role instead of a sentence. `{engagement}` now prints
only the subconsultant sentence, or nothing.

When the table prints, the procurement-route rows (`novation`,
`novation_option`, `dnc_handover`) and the discipline's own appointment
rows follow it, beside the stage pricing. Without a table the role stays one
block, as before.

Every existing role key and its condition is kept, so the answer keys still
hold. The scope-alignment inventory's role entries are updated to the new
wording on purpose.
